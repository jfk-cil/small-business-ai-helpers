# Get found on Google: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/get-found-on-google/ and answer the questions.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), and type "go".
3. Do part 1 in Google as you read it: search your business name, tap "Own this business?" or go to google.com/business. Choose your categories, hours, and address or service area.
4. Verify. Google picks how (video, phone, email or postcard). Type the code only into Google. Verification can take a few days.
5. Paste in your description and services (parts 2 and 3). Add five real photos: signboard, outside, inside, your work, your logo.
6. Find your review link (part 5). Save the review message as a WhatsApp Business quick reply, for example `/review`.
7. Send the helper to yourself on WhatsApp so you can copy it again any day.

## Every week (10 minutes)

- **Monday:** post one Google update from your four weekly posts, with a fresh photo.
- **After every job or sale:** send `/review` to that customer. Every customer, not only the happy ones.
- **Friday:** open your profile. Reply to new reviews (paste the review into the helper, without the name). Check your hours, and add holiday hours before festivals.
- **Once a month:** add new photos. Ask the helper for four more weekly posts.

## When a person must handle it

- Someone else controls your profile, or it is suspended or locked: use Google's own help pages, or ask a builder you trust.
- A review threatens you or accuses you of a crime: post only a calm reply, and ask a qualified person before you do anything more.
- Paid Google Ads, or changes to your website code: a person who does this work.
- Licenses, tax or legal questions: a qualified person.

## Never type these

Your Google password or verification code. Customers' names or phone numbers. Aadhaar, PAN or passport details. Bank, UPI or card details. Passwords or OTPs. Health details. Staff ID numbers or wages. Family details. Photos of people.

Type your own phone, address and website straight into Google. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and start again with a [blank].

**Remember:** this improves your chances; ranking depends on Google. No one can promise you the top spot, and no one can pay Google for it. Never write or buy reviews.
