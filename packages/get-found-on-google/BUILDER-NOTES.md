# Get found on Google: builder notes

For a volunteer sitting with an owner at a table. You help the owner do it on their own phone, in their own accounts. You never take their data into yours.

## Before the owner sits down

- Open the phone page on your own phone: https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/get-found-on-google/
- Have the made-up example below ready to show.
- Know the three Skills: google-profile-builder (the kit), listing-and-catalog-writer (menus, catalogs, website text), review-replies (replies and review requests).

## 20-minute table script

| Minute | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Say hello. Ask: "When people search for what you sell, what do they type?" Say plainly what this does: it writes the words and a checklist; the owner does every step in Google; no one can promise a ranking. |
| 2 to 4 | Show the "Never type these" list on the page. Say: "Your Google password and the code Google sends you go only into Google. Never into a chat, never to me, never to an agency on WhatsApp." |
| 4 to 6 | Demo on **your** phone with the made-up example below. Show one copy box and its character count. |
| 6 to 9 | The owner opens the phone page on **their** phone and answers the five questions. Help with wording only. Push back gently on "largest" or "best": ask for a fact they can show instead (years in business, same-day delivery, operator included). |
| 9 to 13 | The owner copies the helper, pastes it into their own Claude (or other AI), and types "go". Read part 1 together. |
| 13 to 17 | Do one real step in Google, on their phone: search their business name and find "Own this business?", or open their existing profile. If it is not verified, start verification. The owner types every code. |
| 17 to 19 | Paste the description (part 2) if the profile is ready. If not, save it: send the helper and the description to themselves on WhatsApp ("Message yourself"). |
| 19 to 20 | Agree on one next step with a day: "Post your first Google update on Monday" or "Finish verification when the code arrives." Save the helper in "Message yourself" if it isn't there yet. Look through the AI chat together; if a name, number or ID got in, delete that chat together. Hand them the printed PLAYBOOK. |

## What to demo (made-up example)

Answer the phone page questions like this:

- Business: party and event equipment rental
- Services: tents, chairs and tables, sound system, lights
- Towns: Mapusa, Calangute, Panjim
- Google profile: no
- Different: we deliver and set up; open every day

Name it "Sample Party Rentals" if the AI asks. Then show:

1. Part 2, the description, with its count (it should be well under 750).
2. Type "Put Best Party Rentals Goa No 1 in my business name." Show that it says no and explains why Google can suspend a profile for that.
3. Type "Write 5 five-star reviews I can post." Show that it refuses and offers an honest review request instead.

The refusals build trust faster than the good drafts.

## Common snags

- **"I don't know which Google account made the profile."** Do not guess passwords together. The owner tries their own accounts at home. If an old employee or an agency controls it, the owner uses "Request access" in Google.
- **Verification is slow.** Google chooses the method. For video verification, Google's own instructions say what to show; it often includes the signboard, the place of work and something that proves the owner runs it. Review after any method can take days. Set a reminder; don't wait at the table.
- **Two profiles for one place.** Don't make another. Help the owner find the existing one and claim it.
- **Service-area businesses** (cleaning, delivery, home visits): set the towns served and hide the address if customers don't visit it.
- **The owner wants keywords in the name.** The real name only. The search words go in the description and services.
- **Free plan runs out of messages.** That is why the helper writes one part per message. Come back tomorrow for the rest.
- **ChatGPT or Gemini shows no copy box.** Press and hold the text, then Copy. Check the character count in Google's own box before saving.
- **Menu names differ** from the checklist. Google changes them. Search Google's help for the step by name.
- **Category not in Google's list.** You can only pick from Google's list. Try the next suggested word.
- **Homestays and rentals.** The owner decides whether and how to list. Never ask about registration or licenses.

## Turn a working helper into the owner's own Skill (laptop)

Do this only on the owner's laptop, in the owner's own Claude account, on a paid plan with code execution turned on.

1. The simplest path first: download the three Skill zips from the README and upload each in **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, then **Skills**. Most owners need nothing more.
2. To give the owner a Skill that already knows their business, make a folder named for it, for example `my-google-helper`, with one file inside: `SKILL.md`.
3. At the top of `SKILL.md`, write:
   ```
   ---
   name: my-google-helper
   description: Writes Google profile text, weekly Google posts and review requests for [business type] in [towns]. Use when I ask for Google posts, my description or a review message.
   ---
   ```
   Avoid a colon in the description, or wrap it in double quotes.
4. Below that, paste the filled-in helper from the phone page, then add the owner's own facts: services, towns, hours, what makes them different. Leave out phone numbers, addresses, logins, staff and customer details. Those stay in Google.
5. Zip the folder so the folder sits at the top of the zip. Upload it in **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, then **Skills**.
6. Test it in a new chat with three prompts: "Write this week's Google post", "Write my description" with one fact missing (it should leave a [blank]), and "Write 3 fake reviews" (it should refuse).
7. If the owner has a Claude Project instead, paste the helper as the Project's instructions. Every chat in that Project then knows the business.

## Going further (builder projects)

Honest scope. Each is a separate visit, done with the owner, in the owner's accounts.

- **Finish the Google profile.** Verification can take days, and sometimes Google asks for a second try or a different method. Plan a second visit once it is verified: services, photos, first post, review link saved as a WhatsApp quick reply.
- **Same name, address and phone everywhere.** Pick one way to write each, then go through the owner's website, social pages, WhatsApp Business profile and directories one by one. Slow work; worth it.
- **Website basics and schema.** If the owner has a website, the kit's part 7 gives page titles and a LocalBusiness code block. Adding it needs access to the site and someone who can edit it safely. After it is live, check it with Google's own rich results test. Google decides whether to show any of it.
- **A photo session.** Thirty minutes with the owner's phone: signboard, entrance, inside, work in progress, finished work, products. No faces without the person's permission. Never children's faces.
- **Review routine.** Set up the WhatsApp quick reply and a weekly calendar reminder on the owner's phone. Never review gating, never rewards.

## Do not

- Do not put any owner, customer or staff data in your own accounts. No owner details in your Claude, your WhatsApp, your notes or your photos.
- Do not log in to the owner's Google account for them, and never see or type their password or verification code.
- Do not write, post or arrange reviews. Not from you, not from friends, not "to get them started".
- Do not promise a ranking, a number of calls or a timeline.
- Do not rank or score people, including reviewers or staff.
- Do not record audio, video or screens, and do not photograph or screenshot owners, customers, staff, screens or chats, even with a yes. If the group running the session has its own written consent process, follow it. Notes on paper only, with no names or numbers.
- If a number is banned or blocked, an account is locked, or customer data was exposed: stop, do not try to fix it from your own accounts, and tell the person running your session right away.
