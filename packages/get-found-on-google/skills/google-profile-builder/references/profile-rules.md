# Google profile rules in plain words

These are the basics of Google's guidelines for a Google Business Profile, as of October 19, 2026, written simply. **Rules change.** Before you rely on any line here, check Google's own help pages (search "Google Business Profile guidelines" and "Google Business Profile local ranking"). This is not legal advice; for anything about licenses or the law, check with a qualified person.

## The rules (break these and Google can remove or suspend a profile)

1. **Business name = your real name.** Use the name on your signboard, bills and letterhead. Don't add search words, town names, phone numbers or "best" (not "Sample Hire, Best Equipment Rental Goa"). Put those words in your description and services instead.
2. **A real place, or a real service area.**
   - Customers come to you (shop, yard, café, lab, office): show your address.
   - You go to customers (delivery, cleaning, home visits): set a service area (the towns you serve) and hide your address if customers don't visit it. Google lets you list several areas; keep to the places you really serve.
   - No virtual offices, P.O. boxes, a friend's address, or a place with no staff during your opening hours.
3. **One profile per business location.** Don't make a second profile for the same place to get more space on the map. A real second branch with its own staff and signboard can have its own profile.
4. **Categories describe what you are**, not everything you sell. One primary category (the closest match in Google's list) plus a few secondary ones. You can only pick from Google's list; you can't type your own.
5. **Reviews must be genuine.**
   - Never write reviews of your own business, and never ask staff, family or friends to post them.
   - Never pay, give discounts, gifts or prize entries for reviews.
   - Ask every customer, not only the happy ones. Don't send unhappy customers somewhere else first so that only happy ones review ("review gating").
   - Never post reviews of a competitor.
6. **Description and posts:** no links in the description, no phone numbers typed into posts (use the Call button), nothing misleading, no prices or offers you can't honor.
7. **Photos:** real photos of your business. No stock photos made to look like yours. People's faces only with their agreement.
8. **Verification:** Google decides how you verify (video, phone call or text, email, or postcard). Type the code only into Google. Never share it with anyone, including an AI chat or an "SEO agency" on WhatsApp.

## What usually helps local results

Google says local results depend mainly on three things:

- **Relevance:** how well your profile matches what someone searched. A complete profile with the right categories, real services, and the words customers use helps here.
- **Distance:** how far you are from the person searching, or from the place they typed. You can't change this, which is why you won't show first in every town.
- **Prominence:** how well known you are. Reviews (how many and how good), the same name, address and phone on other websites and directories, photos, regular posts and a website all help.

Things that usually help, in order of effort:
1. Claim and verify the profile.
2. Fill every field: categories, hours, services, description, photos.
3. Ask every customer for a review, every week, with your review link.
4. Reply to reviews (use the review-replies helper).
5. Same name, address and phone everywhere (website, Facebook, Instagram, Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha and others).
6. One post a week and new photos every month.

**No one can guarantee a ranking.** Ads can appear above the results, but no one can pay for a higher free place, and no one can guarantee one. Anyone who promises "top of Google" or "No. 1 in Goa" is promising something they can't control. This kit improves your chances; ranking depends on Google.

## How to get your review link

Menu names change a little; if you can't find these, search "get my Google review link" in Google's help.

1. On your phone, open the Google app or Google Maps, signed in with the account that manages your profile.
2. Search your business name exactly. Your profile tools appear above or beside your listing.
3. Tap **Ask for reviews** (sometimes under **Read reviews** or **Customers**, then **Get more reviews**).
4. Tap **Copy link** (or Share, then Copy).
5. Paste the link into your saved WhatsApp message where it says [your review link]. Save it as a WhatsApp Business Quick Reply so you can send it in two taps.

| Business | Words to search for |
|---|---|
| Equipment hire | "Equipment rental agency", "Construction equipment supplier", "Tool rental service", "Party equipment rental service", "Crane rental agency" |

## Places to keep your name, address and phone the same

Google Business Profile · your website (footer and contact page) · Facebook page · Instagram bio · WhatsApp Business profile · Justdial · IndiaMART · Sulekha · TradeIndia · Bing Places · Apple Business Connect · any trade association or local directory you are listed in.

Pick one way to write each (for example "Shop 4" or "Shop No. 4", not both) and use exactly that everywhere.
