**Owner types:**
> Sample Equipment Hire, Verna. We rent generators, scaffolding, concrete mixers, welding machines and air compressors to builders, contractors and event companies. Yard in Verna, customers can collect. We deliver and pick up in Margao, Vasco, Panjim and Mapusa with our own trucks. Hire by the day, week or month. Monday to Saturday 8:30 am to 6:30 pm. I made a Google profile but never verified it. We have a website. Every machine is checked before it goes out. We're the No. 1 rental people around here, but people can't find us on Google.

**Helper replies:**

I left out "No. 1 rental people around here": customers and Google can't check it. Checkable facts work better, so I used "every machine is checked before it goes out" and "our own trucks". If you can show proof of your size, a specific line like "[number] generators and [number] sets of scaffolding" is still better than "No. 1".

**Your Google profile kit: equipment hire in Verna**
This improves your chances; ranking depends on Google. Ads can appear above the results, but no one can pay for a higher free place, and no one can guarantee one.

**1. 10-minute checklist**
1. Claim: search "Sample Equipment Hire" on Google, open your profile and finish verification. If you can't get in, go to google.com/business with the same Google account. If someone else controls it, use "Request access".
2. Verify: Google chooses how (video, phone, email or postcard). Type any code only into Google, never into a chat with AI.
3. Business name: "Sample Equipment Hire", exactly as on your signboard and bills. No extra words like "Goa" or "rental".
4. Primary category: search Google's list for "Equipment rental agency", "Construction equipment supplier" or "Tool rental service". Pick the closest one Google offers.
5. Secondary categories: search for "Generator shop", "Scaffolding rental service" and "Party equipment rental service" (only if you hire to events often). Pick the closest ones Google offers.
6. Address and service area: customers collect from your Verna yard, so show the address. Also add a service area: Verna, Margao, Vasco, Panjim, Mapusa.
7. Hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 6:30 pm; Sunday closed. Add holiday hours before festivals.
8. Phone and website: type them into Google yourself. Don't paste them here.
9. Description and services: paste parts 2 and 3.
10. Photos: logo, cover photo of the yard, the signboard from the road, machines lined up, a truck loading. Real photos; no faces unless the person agrees.
