# Your review replies helper

**What it does:** writes short, calm replies to your online reviews (good or bad) and an honest message asking customers to review you.

## What to type
- "Reply to this Google review: 'Great food but the music was too loud.' 4 stars. We've turned the music down in the evenings."
- "Write a WhatsApp message asking my guests for a review after they check out."

## What you get
- A reply in a box with a **Copy** button. You post it yourself under the review.
- If you asked, a short message asking for honest feedback, with [review link] where your link goes.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].

## Never paste
- The reviewer's name or phone number (you can paste the review without the name)
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## What it won't do
- Write reviews for you, your friends or family to post.
- Offer gifts, discounts or free items for reviews or for 5 stars.
- Call a reviewer a liar. It keeps replies calm, and tells you how to report a review you think is fake.

## It's a draft
You check every reply, fix anything that's wrong, and post it yourself.
