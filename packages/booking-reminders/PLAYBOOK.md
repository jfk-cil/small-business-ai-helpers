# Bookings and reminders: your playbook

Every booking gets the same clear messages. You send them in two taps.

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. Write your preparation steps in your own words: what to bring, how much, how to keep it, by what time. The AI uses exactly these and adds nothing.
2. Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/booking-reminders/ and answer the 4 questions. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**. Send it to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
3. Paste it into a new AI chat. Describe one typical booking, for example "water test, Thursday October 22, 10 am, at our lab". Check every line. Fill each [blank].
4. **Quick replies** (WhatsApp Business > Business tools > Quick replies > +): save each message as `booked`, `tomorrow`, `today`, `pls-confirm`, `reschedule`, `cancel`, `missed`, `thanks`. Keep day, date and time as blanks you change each time.
5. **Labels:** make **Waiting YES**, **Confirmed**, **Done**.
6. **Booking sheet** (Google Sheets app), columns: Booking code, Service, Date, Time, Where, Confirmed?, Reminder sent?, Done?, Notes. A code, not the customer's name.
7. **Google Calendar:** a daily event for yourself, "Send tomorrow's reminders", at your chosen time.

## Every day (10 minutes)

1. **Morning:** send each of today's bookings `/today`. Mark "Reminder sent?" Yes. Label the chat **Waiting YES**.
2. Someone replies YES: mark "Confirmed?" Yes, label **Confirmed**.
3. No reply by your chosen time: send `/pls-confirm` once. Never twice; call them or let it go.
4. **After each visit:** mark "Done?", label **Done**. Send `/thanks`, or `/missed` if they didn't come.
5. **Evening:** send each of tomorrow's bookings `/tomorrow`.

Before any confirmation, check the slot is really free and the weekday matches the date.

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Count this week's no-shows in your sheet. Going down, or not?
2. Customers got the preparation wrong? Make your steps clearer and update the quick replies.
3. Hours, days or rules changed? Make a new helper on the page and update the quick replies.
4. Clear old labels. Delete any AI chat where a name or number slipped in.

## When a person must handle it

- What a test result means, or whether water, food or a place is safe: a qualified person
- A time you haven't confirmed yet
- Complaints, refunds and any charges
- Health questions, ID or guest registration
- A customer who is upset or confused

## Never type these

- Customers' names and phone numbers (use a booking code)
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details, and staff ID numbers
- Bank, UPI and card details, passwords and OTPs
- Health information, wages, family members' details, photos of people
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine)

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
