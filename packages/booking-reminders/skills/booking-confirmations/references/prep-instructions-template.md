# Prep instructions: fill this in once

Customers often come unprepared because nobody told them exactly what to do. Write your own instructions here once, in your own words, then paste them into the chat whenever you ask for booking messages. The helper copies them; it never adds steps of its own.

Only you (or your qualified staff) know the right technical values. Anything you leave as [blank] stays [blank] in the messages until you fill it.

Don't put customer names, phone numbers, ID numbers or payment details here.

## Template

```
Service: [blank]
Customer comes to us, or we go to them: [blank]
What to bring: [blank]
How to prepare: [blank]
How to keep it until the visit (if anything): [blank]
By what time: [blank]
Where (opening days and hours, or "we come to you"): [blank]
How to change or cancel, and how far ahead: [blank]
When we remind them (day before, on the day, at what time): [blank]
After the service (results ready when and how, thank you, review link): [blank]
```
