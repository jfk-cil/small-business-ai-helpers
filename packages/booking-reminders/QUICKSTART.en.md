# Bookings and reminders

**The problem:** Customers book, then forget what to bring or don't turn up. Confirming and reminding each one by hand takes time.

**What you get:** A full set of booking messages (confirm, remind, reschedule, no-show, thank you). The full version adds a booking sheet and a daily routine.

https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/booking-reminders/

## How to use it

1. Copy the helper.
2. Open Claude, or the AI you already use, and start a new chat.
3. Paste the helper and send it.
4. Then paste a customer message or describe today's job, without names or numbers.

**Never type these:** Customers' names or phone numbers, ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport), bank, UPI or card details, passwords or OTPs, health details.

Free to use and adapt (CC0). It drafts; you check and send everything yourself.
