# Start here: your playbook

One job. One helper. One week. Then decide what's next.

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/start-here/
2. Answer the 2 questions. Say what really eats your week, in your own words. Tap the mic if typing is slow.
3. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**.
4. Open Claude (or the AI you already use). Start a new chat, paste, send.
5. Answer its short questions. Type "pass" for any you don't want to answer.
6. Read the one job it names. If it's not right, say so and why.
7. Copy your success checklist (3 to 5 lines) and send it to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
8. Open the helper it named from the list on the page. Make that helper too, and save it in "Message yourself".

## Every day (10 minutes)

1. Do the one job with the named helper. Only that job.
2. Check every draft before you send it. Fill in each [blank] yourself.
3. Look at your success checklist. Tick what happened today.

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Read your success checklist. Is the job taking less of your time?
2. Yes: keep going. Add a second job only when the first one is a habit.
3. Not yet: change one thing (your facts, your tone, the time of day you do it).
4. Wrong job: run this helper again and say what you learned.

## When a person must handle it

- Bookkeeping, cash, payroll, wages, tax and GST: your accountant or a qualified person.
- Hiring, judging or disciplining staff: you.
- Anything legal, medical or about safety: a qualified person.
- Any message you're not sure about: you.

## Never type these

- Customers' or guests' names and phone numbers
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details, and staff ID numbers
- Bank, UPI and card details, passwords and OTPs
- Health information, wages, family members' details, photos of people

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].

It's a draft. You check it, and you decide what to send.
