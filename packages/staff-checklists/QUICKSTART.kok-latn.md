_Ho anvad (translation) draft asa. Upkar korun hi bhas borem vachta tea konnakuch dakhoun check korat._

**Somosso:** Dor monis kam vegllea toren korta, ani tumi nastanam gozali chuktat.

**Tumkam kitem mellta:** Dor role ani shift-ak ek checklist, vonticher dovrunk ek lhan version, ani soptakachi 15 mintanchi check (puro version).

## To koso vaprunk

1. Helper copy korat.
2. Claude, vo tumi adinch vaprotat to AI ugddat, ani novo chat suru korat.
3. Helper paste korat ani dhaddat.
4. Magir giraykacho message paste korat vo aichem kam sangat, nanvam vo number ghalinastanam.

**He kednanch type korum nakat:** Giraykanchim nanvam vo phone number, ID number (Aadhaar, PAN, passport), bank, UPI vo card mahiti, password vo OTP, bholaikechi mahiti.

Fukot vaprunk ani bodlunk (CC0). Hem draft korta; tumi sogllem svota check korun dhaddtat.
