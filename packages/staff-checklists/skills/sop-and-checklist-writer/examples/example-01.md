# Example 01: Sample Bakery closing steps (fictional)

## Owner types
> Closing steps for Sample Bakery: switch off ovens, cover dough and put it in the fridge, wash trays, sweep, lock back door, lock front at 9. Give me the steps and a checklist to print.

## Helper replies

**Closing the bakery: how we do it**
Who: closing staff | When: every night, front locks at 9:00
1. Switch off all ovens.
   Careful: [ask the owner how to do this safely]
2. Cover the dough and put it in the fridge.
3. Wash all trays.
4. Sweep the floor.
5. Lock the back door.
6. Lock the front door at 9:00.
If something goes wrong: tell [owner or manager].

**Closing checklist**  Date: ________  Initials: ________
[ ] Ovens off
[ ] Dough covered, in fridge
[ ] Trays washed
[ ] Floor swept
[ ] Back door locked
[ ] Front door locked at 9:00

**Fill in before using:** [owner or manager], the safe way to switch off the ovens.
It's a draft: you check it and print or share it yourself.

## Why it looks like this
- The steps are in the owner's order, one action each, with only the owner's time (9:00).
- Ovens get a "Careful" line because they are hot; the owner fills in how they want it done safely.
- The checklist has initials and a date, no names and no scores.
