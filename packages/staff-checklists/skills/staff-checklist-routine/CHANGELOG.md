## v1.0.0 (October 20, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Setup turns how the owner does one job (opening, closing, room turnover, kitchen prep) into a checklist of 8 to 12 short lines in the staff's language, a pinned-message text for the staff WhatsApp group (pin for 30 days, re-pin monthly), a routine card (one photo of the finished space with no people, guest belongings, papers or screens; a weekly 10-minute no-blame huddle that changes one line) and a "my helper" prompt under 600 characters.
- Weekly mode rewrites only the line the owner names and keeps every other line word for word; with no checklist in the chat, it asks for it first.
- Never judges, ranks, monitors or disciplines staff; photos are never used to track who or when; a staff member reads any translation before pinning; no safety or cleaning-chemical advice beyond the owner's own steps.
- Test bank evals/testbank/SCL.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (7 cases), pre-registered October 20, 2026.
