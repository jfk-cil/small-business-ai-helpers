# "My helper" prompts (staff-checklist-routine)

The owner saves one of these in WhatsApp "Message yourself" and pastes it into any AI chat after the weekly huddle. Fill in what the owner told you (job, language). Keep it under 600 characters before the checklist is pasted in. Never put a staff member's name, phone number, wages or ID into it.

## Fix a missed line (the default)
```
My [job] checklist is below. Line [number] is hard or gets missed. Rewrite only that line so it is clearer: one action, starting with a verb, 12 words or fewer, in [language]. Keep every other line word for word, with the same numbers. Use only my words; put [blank] for anything missing. No names, no blame.
[paste your checklist here, no names]
```
(About 340 characters before filling in.)

## Translate for a new staff member
```
Translate my [job] checklist below into [language]. Keep the same line numbers and short lines. Don't add or remove anything. I will ask a staff member who reads [language] well to check it before I pin it.
[paste your checklist here, no names]
```

## Filled example (fictional)
```
My room turnover checklist is below. Line [number] is hard or gets missed. Rewrite only that line so it is clearer: one action, starting with a verb, 12 words or fewer, in English. Keep every other line word for word, with the same numbers. Use only my words; put [blank] for anything missing. No names, no blame.
[paste your checklist here, no names]
```

## In another language
Translate the whole prompt into the owner's language (Hindi, Marathi, Konkani in Devanagari or Roman script, or Hinglish) and keep the [blank] placeholders as they are. Add, outside the box: "Please check this with someone who reads it well."
