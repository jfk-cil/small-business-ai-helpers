# Staff checklists: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/staff-checklists/ and answer the questions.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), and send it.
3. Type one role and one shift, for example "kitchen, opening". Start with the shift where things slip most.
4. Read the steps out loud. Fix any step that is not how you really do it. Fill every [blank].
5. Do the same for each role: opening, during the day, closing.
6. Print the short version of each. Stick it where the work happens: by the fridge, the oven, the counter.
7. Send the helper to yourself on WhatsApp so you can copy it again any day.

## Every day (10 minutes)

- **Each shift:** staff tick the wall card, add the date and their initials. Nothing else.
- **At closing:** glance at the cards. If a box is empty, ask "Was something in the way?" Fix the step, not the person.
- **Cash:** steps only. Count the till and write it in the book. Never put money amounts on a card, a sheet or in a chat.

## Every week (15 minutes)

- Sit with your team. One question per role: "Which step doesn't work, and which is missing?"
- Ask the helper to rewrite that checklist with the change. Print the new card.
- Ask each person one thing they would like to learn next. Add it as a step they lead.

## When a person must handle it

- Food safety, fire, gas and licensing rules: a qualified person.
- A step involving gas, electricity, hot liquids, knives or cleaning chemicals: you show how to do it safely, in person.
- Pay, warnings, or a problem with one person's work: you, face to face. Never a checklist, a sheet or an AI.

## Never type these

Staff names, phone numbers, ID numbers or wages. Customers' names or numbers. Aadhaar, PAN or passport details. Bank, UPI or card details. Cash totals or till counts. Passwords or OTPs, including wifi. Health details. Family details. Photos of people.

Use roles, like "kitchen" or "bar", not names. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and start again with a [blank].
