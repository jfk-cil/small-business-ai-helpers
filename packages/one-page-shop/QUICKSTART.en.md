# Your shop on one page

**The problem:** People find you on Instagram, but there's no single place to see everything you offer and buy, book or ask in one tap.

**What you get:** An offer map and checklist, words for a one-page shop with one button per offer, a ready page file, and replies for each path. The full version adds a weekly check.

https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/one-page-shop/

## How to use it

1. Copy the helper.
2. Open Claude, or the AI you already use, and start a new chat.
3. Paste the helper and send it.
4. Then paste one customer message at a time (copying several adds names or numbers), or describe today's job. Delete names and numbers first.

**Never type these:** Customers' names or phone numbers, ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport), bank, UPI or card details, passwords or OTPs, health details, sales or money figures (your public prices are fine).

Free to use and adapt (CC0). It drafts; you check and send everything yourself.
