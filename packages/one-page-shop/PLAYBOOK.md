# Your shop on one page: your playbook

One link, one page, one button per offer. You publish it and keep it true.

## Set up once (45 minutes, or two short sittings)

1. Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/one-page-shop/ and answer the 4 questions. List each offer on its own line. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**. Send it to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
2. Paste it into a new AI chat and type "go". Read the offer map. Check the one action for each offer: buy, book, ask or join a list. Copy the 10-item checklist into a note on your phone.
3. In that same phone note, not in the AI chat, gather your real links: your shop link, payment link, booking form link, Instagram link and your WhatsApp number with the country code.
4. Type "ok" for the page words. Change any word that doesn't sound like you. Fill each [blank] with a fact you are sure of, or write "ask me".
5. Type "ok" for the page itself. On a phone you get the words laid out for Google Sites or a link-in-bio service; say so if someone with a laptop is helping you or your website builder accepts HTML, and you get a page file instead. Put the page online from your phone, or ask someone you trust who has a laptop to use your website builder. Replace every placeholder with your own link.
6. Tap every button on your phone, on mobile data, not only on Wi-Fi.
7. Put the one link in your Instagram bio, your WhatsApp Business profile and your Google Business Profile.
8. Type "ok" for what happens after the click. Save each quick reply in WhatsApp Business (Business tools > Quick replies), and keep the confirmation and follow-up in your phone note.

## Every day (5 minutes)

1. Answer new questions and orders within the time your page promises.
2. Start from the saved quick reply for that offer. Change a word or two to fit.
3. After an order or booking, send the confirmation. Share payment details in chat only after you have confirmed the order.
4. Send the one follow-up if someone asked and went quiet. Then stop.
5. Make a tally mark for each inquiry, order and booking.

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Do the weekly check: count inquiries, orders and bookings (and page clicks, if your tool shows them). Counts only, no names or amounts.
2. Pick one thing to fix this week, like a missing price or a button that confuses people.
3. Update the page: sold-out pieces, new workshop dates, changed prices. Remove anything that is no longer true.
4. Tap every button once more on your phone.
5. New offer? Ask the helper for one more block and button, and a quick reply for it.

## When a person must handle it

- Every order, booking and question: you reply and confirm
- Prices, custom-order quotes and discounts: you decide
- Refund, returns and delivery terms, GST, and what your payment provider asks your page to show: a qualified person
- Putting the page on a website builder: someone you trust who has a laptop, in your own account, while you log in yourself
- An unhappy customer or a payment problem: you, calmly, in a private chat

## Never type these

- Customers' names, phone numbers and emails
- Passwords and OTPs, and keys or logins for your shop, payment or website tools
- Bank, UPI and card details, and passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Health information, staff ID numbers and wages, family members' details, photos of people
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine)

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank]. Pasted a password or key? Change it in that tool too.
