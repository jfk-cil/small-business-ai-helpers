# Your menu and listing helper

**What it does:** turns your rough menu, product list or page notes into clean text people can read and find on Google.

## What to type
- "Fix my menu: chicken cafreal 320, prawn curry rice 380, bebinca 150."
- "Write my homestay listing for a booking site. 5-room homestay in Mandrem, 10 minutes from the beach, Goan breakfast."

## What you get
- Text ready to paste into your menu, WhatsApp catalog, website or booking listing.
- For website and booking-site text: a character count, so it fits.
- For your Google Business Profile, use the google-profile-builder helper instead.
- A short "What I changed" note and a "To fill in" list for anything you didn't give, like a price.
- For a stay listing in Goa: a [registration number] line for you to fill in.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It won't
- Add things you don't have (a pool, a sea view, "organic").
- Write "#1", "best" or a number of reviews that isn't true.
- Say a food or treatment cures or prevents an illness.
- Repeat search words over and over. Google treats that as spam. It uses them once or twice, where they fit.

## It's a draft
You check every price, time and ingredient, then you publish it yourself.
