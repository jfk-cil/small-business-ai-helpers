# Offer map examples for storefront-page-builder

Three fictional businesses. Use them to see the shape of a good Offer map and how the 10 checklist items change with the business. Never copy their facts into a real owner's map.

The one action is always exactly one of: **Buy**, **Book**, **Ask**, **Join list**. If the owner didn't choose, suggest one and write "(suggested)".

## How to pick the one action
- **Buy:** the item has a fixed price and can be paid for now (shop app, payment link).
- **Book:** there is a date and a seat (class, workshop, session, room).
- **Ask:** the price or the item depends on a conversation (custom order, commission, one-of-a-kind piece, bulk order).
- **Join list:** nothing to sell today, but people want to hear first (next retreat, new collection, next batch).

One offer, one action. If an offer seems to need two ("buy or ask"), pick the one most people should take and put the other in the FAQ.

## 1. Sample Studio (artist, Assagao): originals, prints, workshops, commissions

Owner said: "Original paintings and prints are in my shop app. Workshops I take on WhatsApp. Commissions people DM me on Instagram. People find me on Instagram mostly."

| Offer | Who it's for | The one action | Where it happens today | What's missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original paintings | Collectors, home buyers | Ask | Instagram DMs | No clear ask path; sizes [blank] |
| Prints | Gift buyers, visitors | Buy | Shop app | Shop link not in bio |
| Painting workshops | Beginners, visitors | Book | WhatsApp | No booking form; dates [blank] |
| Commissions | Couples, families, cafés | Ask | Instagram DMs | No reply promise |

```
1. One page showing originals, prints, workshops and commissions.
2. Put that one link in your Instagram bio and WhatsApp profile.
3. One button each: Ask, Shop prints, Book, Ask about commissions.
4. Prints button opens your shop app, not your Instagram.
5. Workshop button opens a booking form or WhatsApp with a ready message.
6. Add a reply promise: "I reply within [blank]."
7. Fill in workshop dates, print sizes and shipping, or write "ask me".
8. Save a WhatsApp quick reply for each offer.
9. Test every button on a phone, on mobile data.
10. Weekly check: count inquiries, orders and bookings.
```

## 2. Sample Bakehouse (home bakery, Margao): daily bakes, custom cakes, baking class

Owner said: "Bread and cookies people order on WhatsApp for the next day. Custom cakes need a chat about design. I want to start a monthly baking class."

| Offer | Who it's for | The one action | Where it happens today | What's missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily bread and cookies | Neighbors, offices | Buy | WhatsApp | Order cut-off time [blank] |
| Custom cakes | Birthdays, weddings | Ask | WhatsApp | Notice needed [blank] |
| Baking class | Home bakers | Join list (suggested) | nothing yet | No sign-up form yet |

```
1. One page: daily bakes, custom cakes and the baking class.
2. Same link in WhatsApp profile, Instagram bio and on every box.
3. One button each: Order today, Ask about a cake, Join the list.
4. Order button opens WhatsApp with "I'd like to order" ready.
5. Show the order cut-off time, or write "ask me".
6. Add a reply promise: "I reply within [blank]."
7. A thank-you message ready for every order.
8. Class list: say how people can leave the list.
9. Test every button on a phone, on mobile data.
10. Weekly check: count orders, cake inquiries and list sign-ups.
```

(Health claims about any food stay out, even "healthy" ones the owner asks for.)

## 3. Sample Flow Yoga (yoga teacher, Morjim): drop-in classes, private sessions, retreat

Owner said: "Drop-in classes, people pay at the shala. Privates I fix on WhatsApp. I'm planning a retreat and want a list of interested people. People find me through Google and word of mouth."

| Offer | Who it's for | The one action | Where it happens today | What's missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drop-in classes | Travelers, locals | Book | Walk-in | Class times not online |
| Private sessions | Beginners, people wanting one-to-one | Ask | WhatsApp | No ready message |
| Retreat | Past students | Join list | nothing yet | Dates [blank]; sign-up form |

```
1. One page: drop-in classes, private sessions and the retreat list.
2. Same link on Google, WhatsApp profile and the studio poster.
3. One button each: See class times, Ask about privates, Join the list.
4. Class times written on the page, or "[blank]" until you fill them.
5. Privates button opens WhatsApp with a ready message.
6. Retreat list says what people get and how to leave.
7. Add a reply promise: "I reply within [blank]."
8. No health claims: classes are described as an experience only.
9. Test every button on a phone, on mobile data.
10. Weekly check: count bookings, private inquiries and list sign-ups.
```

## Common mistakes
- Two actions on one offer ("Buy or DM me"): visitors stall. Pick one.
- A button that opens Instagram: people scroll away. Point buttons at the place the action happens.
- "Link in bio" that opens only the portfolio: no way to buy or book.
- A price or date guessed to make the page look complete: leave [blank] or "ask me".
- Making up a deadline ("only 3 seats left") to push people: leave it out unless the owner says it is true.
