# Example 01: event club looking for company bookings (fictional)

**Owner:** "I run Sample Event Club near Panjim. We host DJ nights and have a hall for private parties. Weekends are fine, but weekdays are empty. Who should I go after?"

**Your best customer types**
- **Offices and coworking spaces around Panjim planning team outings:** they often book on weekdays, which fills your empty days (worth checking).
- **Colleges in North Goa that run fests and farewells:** they need a hall and sound for one evening (worth checking).
- **Wedding and event planners:** they look for venues for sangeet nights and after-parties (worth checking).

**Where to find them**
- **Offices and coworking:** LinkedIn company pages, Google Maps, coworking spaces' public event pages.
- **Colleges:** college fest pages on Instagram, college websites, event listings.
- **Planners:** Instagram hashtags, wedding venue listing sites, Google Maps.
