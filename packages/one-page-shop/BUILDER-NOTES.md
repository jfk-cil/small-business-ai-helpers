# Your shop on one page: builder notes

For a volunteer sitting with an owner at a table. Aim: the owner leaves with an offer map they agree with, the 10-item checklist in a phone note, page words for every offer, and either a live page or a clear plan for who puts it online and when.

## Before you sit down

- Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/one-page-shop/ on your own phone, ready to demo.
- Have a printed copy of [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) for the owner.
- Know the three Skills: storefront-page-builder (offer map, page words, page file, what happens after the click), listing-and-catalog-writer (clear text for each offer) and target-customer-finder (who each offer is for).
- Be ready to say no, kindly, to "log in and set it up for me", "connect my payments" and countdown timers. Owners often ask.
- The owner uses their own phone, their own accounts and their own AI account. Not yours. The owner types their own links and passwords, never you.

## Demo with a made-up example

Answers to type on your phone:
- Offers: "Hand-poured candles, a Saturday candle-making class, custom candles for weddings and events"
- Who and where: "Visitors and locals, mostly through Instagram and friends"
- Action: "Buy candles, book the class, ask about custom candles"
- Tools: "A free shop app, a payment link, WhatsApp"

Call it "Sample Candle Studio" if the AI asks.

What to point out:
- The offer map gives each offer exactly one action, and "What's missing" names the gap ("Shop link not in Instagram bio").
- Button labels are short and start with a verb ("Book a class"). Every link is a placeholder like [your booking form link].
- Prices, class times and materials the owner didn't give stay [blank]. Nothing is guessed.
- Type "Add a timer that says only 2 left." Show that it won't add fake urgency.
- Type "Log in to my shop app and connect my payments." Show that it says it can't log in or take payments, and keeps payment details off the page.

## Common snags

- **Wrong language.** Change the language on the page and make the helper again, or type "Write to me in Hindi" in the chat. The page words can be in whatever language the owner's customers read. Translations are drafts; check with someone who reads it well.
- **The owner pastes a password, payment key or login.** Stop. Don't read it out. Delete that chat (three dots > Delete). Ask the owner to change that password or key in the tool itself, today.
- **"Can it set up my shop or take payments?"** No. The buttons point to the shop app, payment link or booking form the owner already has. Payments happen there.
- **Phone-only owner.** The page file needs a laptop or a website builder that accepts HTML. On a phone, use Google Sites or a link-in-bio service with the page words. That is a fine first page.
- **The website builder won't take the file.** Some builders accept custom HTML only on paid plans, or show it inside a frame. Rebuild the page with the builder's own text and button blocks, using the page words.
- **The WhatsApp button opens the wrong chat.** The number goes in with the country code first (91 for India), no +, no spaces, no dashes, no leading zero. Tap it on the owner's phone to check.
- **A placeholder is still on the page.** In the page file, links that still hold a placeholder show a dashed red outline. Go through every one before sharing the link.
- **Too many offers.** The map keeps up to 6 rows and groups the rest. Suggest leading with the 3 that matter most this season.
- **Refund or returns wording.** It leaves these as [blank]. A qualified person checks the wording, especially if the payment provider asks the page to show policies.
- **Credits or free messages run out.** The helper is plain text. Paste the same helper into ChatGPT or Gemini (free) and carry on. Only the Skills and Projects stay inside Claude.
- **No "Try it here" box.** That box only appears when the page is opened inside Claude. Copying the helper always works.

## How to put the page on a free builder

Do this with the owner, on the owner's phone or laptop, in the owner's own accounts. The owner logs in and types passwords themselves. Check each service's current terms first: free plans, limits and menus change often.

- **Phone only: Google Sites or a link-in-bio service.** Use the page words, not the HTML file. Put the headline and the line about the owner at the top (or in the profile text). Then, for each offer, add a text block and a button with the button label and the owner's real link. Add the questions and answers as text. Publish and copy the page's address.
- **The owner's existing website builder.** Add a new page called "Start here" or "Shop and workshops". If the builder has a custom HTML, code or embed block, paste the page file there. If not, rebuild it with the builder's own blocks. Link the new page from the home page.
- **GitHub Pages (laptop), only if its terms fit.** GitHub's rules say Pages is not meant for running an online business or a site mainly for selling. Read GitHub's current terms before you use it for a shop page, or pick another option. Free for public repositories. Anything in a public repository can be seen by anyone, so the file holds only public links. The owner creates the account. Make a new public repository, upload the file named `index.html`, open Settings > Pages, choose the main branch and save. The address appears after a short wait.
- **Before sharing:** every placeholder replaced; every button tapped on mobile data; prices and policies are ones the owner is sure of, or "ask me".
- **Point everything at it.** Instagram: Profile > Edit profile > Links > Add external link. WhatsApp Business: Settings > Business tools > Business profile, then add the link as the website. Use the same link on the Google Business Profile, cards and posters. Menus differ by app version.

## Turning a working helper into the owner's own Skill (laptop)

Only on the owner's laptop and the owner's own Claude account, on a paid plan with code execution turned on (Settings > Capabilities).

Easiest: download the three Skills from the package page (storefront-page-builder, listing-and-catalog-writer, target-customer-finder) and upload each in Settings > Capabilities > Skills.

To make a Skill that already knows the owner's offers:
1. Make a folder with a short lowercase name, for example `my-shop-page`.
2. Inside it, make a text file called `SKILL.md` that starts like this:
   ```
   ---
   name: my-shop-page
   description: Keeps my one-page shop up to date for my candle studio: candles to buy, a Saturday class to book, custom candles to ask about. Use when I add an offer, change a price or date, or need a reply for someone who tapped a button.
   ---
   ```
3. Under that, paste the helper text from the phone page, the offer map the owner agreed on, and the true facts they want repeated (what each offer includes, the reply promise). No customer names, phone numbers, links with private details, passwords or payment keys.
4. Zip the folder (the folder itself goes inside the zip) and upload it in Settings > Capabilities > Skills.
5. Test it in a new chat by typing "go".

You can also paste the helper into a Claude chat and ask Claude to turn it into a Skill; with code execution on, it can make the zip for the owner to download.

## Going further (builder projects)

Be clear with the owner about size and cost before you start any of these.

- **Quick replies and labels in WhatsApp Business.** On the owner's phone: the greeting message, one quick reply per offer, and labels such as New inquiry, Order, Booking, Paid, Done.
- **A simple order and booking count** (Google Sheets, owner's account). Columns A to E: Date, Offer, Action (Buy, Book, Ask, Join), Status, Next step. Weekly bookings: `=COUNTIFS(C2:C,"Book",A2:A,">="&TODAY()-7)`. No customer names or phone numbers in any sheet that will be pasted into AI.
- **Clear text for each offer.** Use listing-and-catalog-writer for each shop listing and workshop description, so the page and the shop app say the same thing.
- **Reminders for bookings.** The [Bookings and reminders](../booking-reminders/) package writes the confirm, remind and reschedule messages for classes.
- **Getting found.** Put the page link on the owner's Google Business Profile; the [Get found on Google](../get-found-on-google/) package helps with the rest.
- **Posts that lead to the page.** The [Posts that bring customers](../social-posts/) package helps turn followers into buyers, with one clear next step in each post.
- **An automatic WhatsApp bot** is a separate, bigger project: it needs the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, the customer's opt-in and approved message templates.
- **Never build** checkout forms that collect card or UPI details, scripts that track visitors, or anything that stores the owner's payment keys or logins. Payments stay inside the owner's shop app or payment provider.

## Do not

- Put the owner's data in your own accounts. No customer lists, links, emails or numbers in your Claude, your sheets or your phone.
- Publish the page from your own accounts or under your name. It lives in the owner's accounts.
- Ask for or touch the owner's passwords, OTPs, payment keys or logins. They log in themselves.
- Write refund, returns, delivery or GST terms for the owner. A qualified person checks those.
- Record or photograph anything without saying so out loud first and hearing a yes.
- Post the owner's name, business, page link, screenshots or customer details anywhere public, including GitHub issues and social media.
- Promise sales or results.
