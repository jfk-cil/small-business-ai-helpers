# Your shop on one page

One link for everything you offer, with a clear button for each: buy, book or ask.

## The problem

Makers, artists and small studios often sell several things at once: finished pieces or products, classes or workshops, and custom orders. These usually live in different places: a portfolio site, a free shop app with a payment link, Instagram and WhatsApp. People find the business online, but there is no single place where they can see everything and know whether to buy, book or ask. Running it all alone, the owner has no time to build the common thread. The goal is one entry point with a clear link for each offer, and a simple path from each tap to an order or a booking.

## What you get

The phone helper works in four steps, one per message, and asks before moving on:

1. **An offer map**: each offer, who it's for, the one action (buy, book, ask or join a list), where that happens today, and what's missing. Then a 10-item checklist for your one-link entry point.
2. **The words for your page**: a short headline, one line about you, one block and one button per offer, how to order or book, and up to 5 questions and answers.
3. **One simple page file** (HTML) you can put on a free website builder, with every link left as a placeholder like [your shop link] or [your WhatsApp link].
4. **What happens after the click** for each offer: a quick reply, a confirmation and one polite follow-up.

The full version (Skills) also:
- writes a WhatsApp greeting message and a weekly 15-minute count of inquiries, orders and bookings;
- gives plain steps for putting the page online for free, and for setting your Instagram bio link and WhatsApp Business website link;
- writes clear product, catalog and website text for each offer;
- works out who each offer is for and where to find more people like them.

## Limits

- **Drafts only.** It writes words and a page file. You check every word and add your real links. It can't log in to anything, take payments, send messages or count clicks.
- **You publish the page.** It never puts the page online for you. Every link stays a placeholder like [your shop link] until you type in your own, and it never fills in a real phone number.
- **No payment details or logins.** It never asks for passwords, OTPs, payment gateway keys or website logins, and it keeps UPI and bank details off the page. Buttons go to your shop link or payment link; you share payment details in chat after you confirm an order. If you paste a password or key by mistake, change it in that tool.
- **No made-up facts.** No invented prices, sizes, materials, dates, delivery terms, refund rules or reviews. Anything you didn't give stays [blank].
- **No fake urgency.** No "only 2 left", countdowns or "selling fast" unless you say it is true. A customer's words go on the page only if you add them, with that customer's permission.
- **A plain page.** The page file has no scripts, tracking or forms, so it sends nothing anywhere. A shopping cart or online booking stays in the shop app or booking tool you already use; the page links to it.
- **No legal or tax advice.** Refund, returns and delivery wording, GST, consumer rules, and what your payment provider asks your page to show: check with a qualified person.
- **Free hosting terms change.** Free website builders, link-in-bio services, Google Sites and GitHub Pages change their plans, limits and menus often. Check each service's current terms before you rely on it.
- **Plans:** the phone helper works on any Claude plan, including free, and in ChatGPT or Gemini. Uploading Skills needs a paid Claude plan with code execution turned on. Claude Projects (saving the helper as instructions) also need a paid plan. Claude Code and Cowork need a plan that includes them. Free plans have message limits; if you reach one, paste the same helper into another AI.
- The Hindi, Marathi and Konkani versions of the page are draft translations. Check them with someone who reads the language well.

## Privacy

Never type these into the page or into any AI chat:

- Customers' names, phone numbers and emails (describe types, not people)
- Passwords and OTPs, and keys or logins for your shop, payment or website tools
- Bank, UPI and card details (the drafts use [your UPI ID])
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

You don't need to paste your real links or WhatsApp number either. The helper uses placeholders, and you type your own links into the page yourself. If you paste a private detail by mistake, delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].

The phone page checks your answers for phone numbers, email addresses, UPI IDs, Aadhaar-like and PAN-like numbers, card numbers and passwords before it makes the helper. It does not check what you paste into the AI afterward. Each AI company has its own rules about keeping chats, so check the privacy settings in the app you use.
