# Message library: follow-up-planner

Starting points for a 3-step sequence. Change the words to fit the owner's facts and voice. Every [blank] stays a [blank] unless the owner gives the fact. Each message is under 500 characters and ends with [your name or group name].

How the steps work for every group:
- **Step 1:** says who you are, asks once, clearly.
- **Step 2:** adds one useful thing (the key fact again, an easier way to say yes, an offer to answer questions). Not "just checking in".
- **Step 3:** the last message. Polite, says it is the last one, makes "no thanks" easy, leaves the door open.

Never add: guilt ("without you", "we're counting on you"), pressure ("last chance", "hurry", "only a few left"), invented numbers ("we fed 300 families"), invented deadlines or matching gifts, or tax-benefit claims the owner did not state.

## 1. Donors and past supporters

**Step 1**
```
Hi [name],
This is [your name] from [group name]. Thank you for supporting us before.
We are now raising funds for [what it is for]. Would you like to give again this time?
You can give [how to give, e.g. by UPI: [your UPI ID]].
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 2**
```
Hi [name],
Following up on my message about [what it is for].
Any amount helps us [what the money does, in the owner's words].
Happy to answer any questions. Just reply here.
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 3 (last)**
```
Hi [name],
This is my last message about [what it is for]. If now isn't a good time, that's completely fine. Just reply "no thanks" and I won't message again about it.
Thank you for being part of [group name].
[your name or group name]
```

If the owner wants to mention impact and gives no number, write "[blank] families" (or children, animals, students) and list it under Fill in. If the owner states a tax benefit (for example 80G), keep their exact words and tell the owner: "check with a qualified person before sending."

## 2. Members (renewal)

**Step 1**
```
Hi [name],
Your membership with [group name] is due for renewal on [date].
The fee is [fee]. You can pay [how to pay].
Would you like to renew?
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 2**
```
Hi [name],
A gentle reminder about renewing your membership ([fee], [how to pay]).
If something has changed or you have a question, just reply here.
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 3 (last)**
```
Hi [name],
This is my last reminder about renewal. If you'd rather not renew, just reply "no thanks" and I won't message again about it.
You are welcome back anytime.
[your name or group name]
```

Never write that membership ends or benefits stop on a date unless the owner says so.

## 3. Volunteers

**Step 1**
```
Hi [name],
We are looking for volunteers for [activity] on [day, date] at [place], from [start time] to [end time].
Would you like to join us? Just reply yes and I'll send the details.
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 2**
```
Hi [name],
Following up about [activity] on [day, date]. Even a short time helps.
If you can come, just reply yes. If you have a question, ask here.
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 3 (last)**
```
Hi [name],
This is my last message about [activity]. If you can't make it this time, no problem at all. Just reply "not this time" and I'll keep you posted on the next one.
[your name or group name]
```

For an event, every follow-up must land before the event day. If the gaps don't fit, drop step 2 and send step 3 a day or two before (say so to the owner).

## 4. Sales leads and quotes

**Step 1**
```
Hi [name],
Thank you for asking about [service or product]. I sent the quote on [date].
Would you like to go ahead, or should I change anything?
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 2**
```
Hi [name],
Following up on the quote for [service or product].
If it helps, I can [easier next step from the owner, e.g. a short call or a site visit].
Just reply here.
[your name or group name]
```

**Step 3 (last)**
```
Hi [name],
This is my last message about the quote. If it's not for you right now, just reply "no thanks" and I won't message again about it.
If you need [service or product] later, message here anytime.
[your name or group name]
```

Never add a discount, a price rise, an expiry date or "only a few slots" unless the owner says it is true.

## 5. Busy weekend catch-up

For weekends when bookings and questions pile up and a few people don't get a reply. Do this once, on Saturday night or Monday morning, not all weekend.

**The routine**
1. Saturday night or Monday morning: open your tracker or your chat labels ("Waiting for reply", "Step 1 sent", "Step 2 sent").
2. Type the rows here with codes, not names: Code | waiting since | reply or step due | status, plus today's date.
3. Work down the catch-up list, oldest first: people waiting for your answer, then follow-ups due.
4. Update Step and Last message date for each code, and mark anyone who said no or asked to stop as Stop (Do not contact).

The catch-up list never changes the stop rules: nobody marked Stop gets a message, never more than 3 follow-ups per person, never two on one day.

**Sorry for the slow reply** (`sorry-slow`): for someone who wrote to you and is still waiting. Add your own answer under it, or "Let me check and confirm shortly."
```
Hi! Sorry for my slow reply.
[your answer to their question]
[your name or group name]
```

**Sorry, still interested?** (`sorry-still`): for someone due a follow-up. It counts as their next step; if step 3 was already sent, don't send it.
```
Hi! Sorry I didn't get back to you sooner.
Are you still interested in [what they asked about]? Just reply here and I'll help.
[your name or group name]
```

Never add a reason the owner didn't give ("we were fully booked"), a discount or a deadline.

**For next weekend (the free app, set up once)**
- An away message for busy hours (in the app's Business tools; menus change, so check in the app), in the owner's words, for example: "Hi! Thanks for your message. We'll reply as soon as we can." It only answers people who write in; it doesn't follow anyone up.
- A label "Waiting for reply" on every chat you couldn't answer, so the catch-up list is quick to make.
- A Google Calendar reminder to yourself: "Weekend catch-up" on Saturday night or Monday morning, at a time you choose.

Fully automatic replies and follow-ups need the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, the customer's opt-in and approved message templates: a builder project (see `automation-options.md`). This helper drafts; the owner sends.
