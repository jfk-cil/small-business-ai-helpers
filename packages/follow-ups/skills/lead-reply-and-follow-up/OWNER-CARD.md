# Your inquiry reply helper

**What it does:** when someone new asks about what you sell, it writes your first reply, plus up to 3 short follow-ups for if they go quiet. The last one is a polite goodbye.

## What to type
- "Someone enquired on our website about a deep clean for a 3BHK villa. Our price is Rs 6,500, one day. I want them to book a site visit. Write my reply and follow-ups."
- "A lead messaged on Instagram asking about private yoga classes, then went quiet after I replied. What do I send next?"

## What you get
- **Your first reply**, ready to paste into WhatsApp, Instagram or email. It answers their question first, then asks one simple next step.
- Up to **3 follow-ups**, with when to send each (for chat: about 1 day, 3 days, 1 week; for email: a bit slower). If they have a date coming up, all of them go before it.
- When to stop: as soon as they reply, say no, or ask you to stop.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].

## Tell it your answers
Tell it the price, what's included and when you're free. If you don't, it leaves a [blank]. It never guesses a price or says you're free when you didn't say so.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names (it writes [their name])
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It won't
- Say "only 1 slot left" or "price goes up tomorrow" unless it's true and you tell it so.
- Message a different number, a family member or another account. It follows up only where they wrote to you.
- Write a 4th follow-up, or anything after a "no".

## It's a draft
You check every message, fix anything that's wrong, and send it yourself.
