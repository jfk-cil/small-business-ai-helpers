# Follow-ups: your playbook

Three polite messages at most. Codes, not names. You send every one.

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. **Your sheet** (Google Sheets app), tab "Tracker", columns: Code, Group, First message date, Step, Last message date, Next follow-up date, Status, Notes. Give each person a code (S1, S2...). Names and numbers, if you want them, go at the far right, in your sheet only.
2. Only add people who gave you their number and agreed to hear from you.
3. Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/follow-ups/ and answer the 4 questions. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**. Send it to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
4. Paste it into a new AI chat and type "sequence". Check each message. Fill every [blank].
5. **Quick replies** (WhatsApp Business > Business tools > Quick replies > +): save the 3 messages as `fu-first`, `fu-second`, `fu-last`.
6. **Labels:** **Step 1 sent**, **Step 2 sent**, **Step 3 sent**, **Replied**, **Do not contact**.
7. **Google Calendar:** a daily event for yourself, "Follow-ups: who is due today".

## Every day (10 minutes)

1. Paste today's date and your tracker rows (codes only) into the AI chat, like: `S14 | last message Oct 12 | step 1 | no reply`.
2. For each code it lists: open that chat, type `/fu-second` or `/fu-last`, check it, send.
3. New people: send `/fu-first`. In your sheet, update Step and Last message date. Change the chat label.
4. Someone replied? Answer them yourself. Set Status: Replied, Yes, No or Stop.
5. **End of day:** type notes like "Sent 12, S3 yes, S7 call back Friday, S9 asked to stop. Report." Save the report in "Message yourself".

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Read the week's reports. Put every "call back" in your calendar.
2. Check nobody got more than 3 messages, and nobody marked Stop got any.
3. Remove anyone marked **Do not contact** from your broadcast lists.
4. New ask (new date, new drive)? Make a new helper on the page, type "sequence", update your quick replies.
5. Delete any AI chat where a name or number slipped in.

## When a person must handle it

- Anyone who replies: you answer, and their sequence stops
- Upset supporters, complaints, disputes
- Receipts, tax benefits or money questions: a qualified person
- Someone who asks to stop: mark them, send nothing more
- A family member answering for someone

## Never type these

- Names and phone numbers of supporters, members or customers (use codes), and your contact list
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details, and staff ID numbers
- Bank, UPI and card details, passwords and OTPs
- Health information, wages, family members' details, photos of people
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine)

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
