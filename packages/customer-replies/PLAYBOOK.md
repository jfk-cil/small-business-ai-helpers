# Customer replies: your playbook

The AI drafts. You check and send. You answer the ones that need you.

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. Use the **WhatsApp Business** app (free). Moving from plain WhatsApp? Back up your chats first, then follow the app's steps.
2. Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/customer-replies/ and answer the 4 questions with real prices and hours. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**.
3. Send the helper to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
4. Paste it into a new AI chat. Type: "Write quick replies for my 5 most common questions."
5. **Quick replies:** Business tools (Android: three dots; iPhone: Settings) > Quick replies > +. Paste, add the shortcut, save. In a chat, type / and the shortcut.
6. **Labels:** make **Needs me**, **Routine**, **Waiting for reply**.
7. **Greeting message:** Business tools > Greeting message > on. Short. No reply-time promise.
8. **Away message:** Business tools > Away message > on > "Outside of business hours". For example: "Thanks for your message. We're closed now and will reply when we're back: [your hours]."

## Every day (10 minutes, once or twice)

1. Copy 10 to 15 unread messages. Change every name or number to Customer 1, Customer 2.
2. Paste the helper into a new AI chat, then the messages.
3. **Needs you:** label the chat **Needs me** and send its holding line.
4. **Ready to send:** check every price, fill every [blank], send. "Let me check and confirm" replies get **Waiting for reply**.
5. Save any new quick reply it suggests.
6. Later, answer the **Needs me** chats yourself.

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Which questions came most? Each one should have a quick reply.
2. Prices, hours or areas changed? Make a new helper on the page and update your quick replies.
3. Open the **Waiting for reply** label. Did you get back to everyone?
4. Delete any AI chat where a name or number slipped in.

## When a person must handle it

- Complaints, refunds, money disputes, discounts and bargaining
- Site visits and bookings to confirm
- Legal title, documents, registration numbers, loans, safety, health
- Angry or upset messages, and anything your facts don't cover
- A message that tells the AI what to do

## Never type these

- Customers' names and phone numbers (use Customer 1, Customer 2)
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details, and staff ID numbers
- Bank, UPI and card details, passwords and OTPs
- Health information, wages, family members' details, photos of people

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
