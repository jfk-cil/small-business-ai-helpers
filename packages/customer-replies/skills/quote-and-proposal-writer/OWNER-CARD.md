# Your quotes and proposals helper

**What it does:** turns your own prices into a neat quotation (or a short proposal) your client can read on their phone, with every sum written out so you can check it.

## What to type
- "Quote from my cafe: 25 people, veg thali Rs 350 each, 2 lime sodas each at Rs 80, delivery Rs 500. 50% advance."
- "Proposal for a company that wants 3 yoga mornings for 15 staff. My price is Rs [price] per session."

## What you get
- Each item on its own line with the math: **25 x Rs 350 = Rs 8,750**.
- The subtotal with the addition shown, so you can check it with a calculator.
- A **[GST if applicable]** line left blank, with "check tax with your accountant".
- **Valid until: [date]** left for you to fill in.
- Short terms in plain words: advance, balance, how you take payment, what's not included.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].

## Use your own prices
It only uses the prices you give. If you leave one out, it writes [price] and asks you. It won't guess a price or tell you what others charge.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names (it writes [their name])
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It won't
- Tell you which GST rate to charge, or whether you need GST. Ask your accountant.
- Make bills, invoices or account books. Keep them in your own accounts book or software, with your accountant, not in an AI chat.

## It's a draft
Check every number, fill in the blanks, and send it yourself.
