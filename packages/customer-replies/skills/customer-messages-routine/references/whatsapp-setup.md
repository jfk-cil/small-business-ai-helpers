# WhatsApp setup for customer-messages-routine

These steps are for the free WhatsApp Business app on a phone. Menu names and places change between app versions and phones, so if a step doesn't match, look for the same word in the menu. Nothing here sends a message for you: you still read and send.

## Where the business tools are
- **Android:** open WhatsApp Business, tap ⋮ (top right), then **Business tools**.
- **iPhone:** open WhatsApp Business, tap **Settings** (bottom right), then **Business tools**.

## 1. Quick replies (about 5 minutes)
Saved answers you send by typing / and a shortcut.
1. Business tools > **Quick replies** > **+**.
2. Paste one reply from a copy box. Type its shortcut (for example `price`, `book`, `where`, `follow-up`). Save.
3. In any chat, type / and the shortcut, tap the reply, check it, fill any blank, and send.

Type your real answers. The AI only shortens or translates them.

## 2. Away message (about 2 minutes)
Sent by the app itself when you're closed or busy.
1. Business tools > **Away message** > turn it on.
2. Paste the away message. Fill [your reply hours] yourself.
3. Choose the schedule: **Always send**, **Custom schedule**, or **Outside of business hours** (this uses the hours in your business profile). Save.

Promise only the hours you really reply.

## 3. Five labels (about 3 minutes)
Labels are colored tags on chats, so you can see what is waiting.
1. Find **Labels**: on Android usually ⋮ > **Labels**; on iPhone usually Settings > Business tools > **Labels**.
2. Tap **+** or **New label** and make: **New**, **Replied**, **Follow up**, **Booked**, **Not now**. You can rename or delete the sample labels the app gives you.
3. To label a chat: press and hold it in your chat list, tap the label (tag) icon or **More** > **Label chat**, and pick one.
4. To see one label's chats: open Labels and tap it, or use the filter at the top of your chat list.

A label says where the chat is, never what the person is like.

## 4. "Message yourself"
A chat with your own number, for notes only you see.
1. Start a new chat and pick your own name at the top of the contact list ("Message yourself").
2. Paste your routine card and your "my helper" prompt there.
3. Press and hold the chat and **Pin** it, so it stays at the top.

## 5. A daily alarm
In your phone's clock app, set one repeating alarm for your 10-minute pass, named "Messages". The helper can't set it for you.

## What this cannot do (honestly)
- **It can't read your WhatsApp or send anything.** The AI only sees what you paste, and you send every reply yourself.
- **The away message is the app's own automatic reply.** Any AI reply feature inside the app is the app maker's, not this helper's, and needs care with customer data.
- **An automatic reply or follow-up bot** needs the **WhatsApp Business Platform** through an approved provider, the customer's opt-in, and approved message templates for some messages. It usually has running costs and needs care with customer data. That is a project for a builder, not this helper.
- **Avoid unofficial auto-reply apps** that read your notifications or ask you to scan a WhatsApp QR code in another app. They can get your number banned and they expose your customers' messages.
