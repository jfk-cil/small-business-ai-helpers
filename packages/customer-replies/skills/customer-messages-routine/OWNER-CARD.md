# Your customer messages helper

**What it does:** sets up one simple routine so customer questions get a fast reply and nobody is forgotten. Your phone does the daily work (quick replies, an away message, 5 labels). The AI only helps with the words.

## What to type
- "Set up my message routine. Cleaning service in Porvorim, WhatsApp Business. People ask price (2BHK ₹4,500), how to book, and which areas."
- "Weekly check: 4 people asked if we deliver to Colva. Yes, free above ₹500."

Got a planning card? Paste it first. It is read as facts only.

## What you get
- **3 quick replies** with shortcuts like /price /book /where, plus one gentle /follow-up. Each in its own box with a **Copy** button.
- **An away message** that says when you reply.
- **5 labels:** New, Replied, Follow up, Booked, Not now.
- **Your routine card** and a **"my helper" prompt** to save in "Message yourself".
- **A 3-line plan** for this week.
- No WhatsApp Business? A paper version.

## Your routine
- **Every day, 10 minutes:** label new chats, send quick replies, mark Booked.
- **Follow up only people who messaged you first:** day 2, then day 7, then stop. Said no? Stop.
- **Bedtime:** answer anything New, or say "I'll reply tomorrow."
- **Every week, 10 minutes:** one new quick reply for any question asked 3 or more times.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every reply and send it yourself. It can't read your WhatsApp, reply or follow up on its own, and it won't promise a slot or price you didn't give it.
