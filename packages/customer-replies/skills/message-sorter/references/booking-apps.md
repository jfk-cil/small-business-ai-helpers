# Messages from booking and delivery apps

Some messages come through a booking app or a delivery app instead of WhatsApp (tours, rentals, rooms, food orders). When the owner marks a pasted message as coming from an app (for example "Customer 2 (app): what time is pickup?"), sort it the same way, then draft it for that app's own chat using this page.

## Reply inside the app's own chat
- The draft goes back in the app's own message box, on that booking. Don't move the customer to WhatsApp, a call or email unless the app allows it and the customer asked.
- WhatsApp quick replies only work in WhatsApp. For app replies, the owner keeps a copy in phone notes and pastes it in, or uses the app's own saved replies if it has them (check in the app).

## Follow each app's messaging rules
Many apps don't allow these inside their chat:
- sharing phone numbers, email addresses or WhatsApp links;
- outside links (your own website, booking page or map link);
- asking for or taking payment outside the app (UPI, cash advance, bank transfer).

Rules differ from app to app and change often, so the owner should check the app's current rules (its help or partner pages). If unsure, leave it out.

For app drafts:
- No phone number, no "UPI: [your UPI ID]" and no [location link] unless the owner says the app allows them. Describe the place in words: "Meeting point: [blank]".
- Leave payment out unless the owner says how it works in that app.
- An app message asking to pay outside the app, or to swap numbers to talk on WhatsApp, goes in **Needs you** ("asks for something only you can decide") with the holding line "Thanks for your message. I'll get back to you shortly."

## Quick replies for pickup time, meeting point and what to bring
Routine questions that repeat get these. Anything the owner didn't give stays [blank]; never add an arrival time ("be ready 10 minutes early") the owner didn't state. A pickup time for a booking the owner hasn't confirmed: "Let me check and confirm shortly."

**Shortcut:** `pickup-time`
```
Hi! Thanks for booking [activity] on [day and date].
Pickup time: [blank]
Pickup point: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

**Shortcut:** `meeting-point`
```
Hi! Here's where we meet for [activity].
Meeting point: [blank]
How to find us: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

**Shortcut:** `what-to-bring`
```
Hi! For [activity], please bring: [blank]
Please wear: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

## Sending the owner's own activity list or link
Only where both are true: the app's rules allow it (check the app's current rules), and the customer asked for it ("what else do you do?"). Otherwise answer in the chat in words, or point to the activities already listed on the owner's page in the app.

```
Hi! Thanks for asking. Here are our activities: [your activity list or link]
Tell us which one you'd like and the day, and we'll confirm.
[your business name]
```
Never add a phone number or a payment request to it.

## What this helper can't do
It can't read, reply or auto-reply inside any app. The owner copies each draft into the app. For automatic replies inside an app, check that app's own help pages.
