# WhatsApp Business setup for message-sorter

These steps are for the free WhatsApp Business app on a phone. Menu names and places change between app versions and phones, so if a step doesn't match, look for the same word in the menu. Nothing here sends a message for you: you still read and send.

## Where the business tools are
- **Android:** open WhatsApp Business, tap ⋮ (top right), then **Business tools**.
- **iPhone:** open WhatsApp Business, tap **Settings** (bottom right), then **Business tools**.

## 1. Labels: "Needs me", "Routine", "Waiting for reply"
Labels are colored tags on chats, so you can see at a glance what is waiting for you.
1. Find **Labels**. On Android it is usually ⋮ > **Labels** (on some versions it is inside Business tools). On iPhone it is usually Settings > Business tools > **Labels**.
2. Tap **+** or **New label** and add three labels: **Needs me**, **Routine**, **Waiting for reply**. You can rename or delete the sample labels the app gives you.
3. To label a chat: press and hold the chat in your chat list, then tap the label (tag) icon, or **More** > **Label chat**. Pick the label.
4. To see all chats with one label: open Labels and tap the label, or use the filter at the top of your chat list.

How to use them with this helper:
- After a sort, give each "Needs you" chat the **Needs me** label.
- After you send a routine draft, label it **Routine** (or remove the label).
- If your reply says "Let me check and confirm shortly", label it **Waiting for reply** so you don't forget to come back.

## 2. Greeting message
Sent automatically by the app when someone messages you for the first time, or after a long gap.
1. Business tools > **Greeting message** > turn it on.
2. Tap the message to edit it. Paste a short greeting written with this helper (ask: "Write my greeting message in my tone").
3. Choose who gets it (everyone, or only people not in your contacts) and save.

Keep it honest: don't promise a reply time you can't keep.

## 3. Away message
Sent automatically when you're closed or busy.
1. Business tools > **Away message** > turn it on.
2. Edit the message. A safe pattern: "Thanks for your message. We're closed right now and will reply when we're back: [your hours]." Fill [your hours] yourself.
3. Choose the schedule: **Always send**, **Custom schedule**, or **Outside of business hours** (this uses the hours in your business profile). Save.

## 4. Quick replies
Saved answers you send by typing / and a shortcut.
1. Business tools > **Quick replies** > **+**.
2. Paste the message from a "Save as quick replies" copy box. Type the shortcut (lowercase, hyphens, 25 characters or fewer). Save.
3. In any chat, type / and the shortcut, tap the reply, check it, and send.

## A daily routine that fits
1. Once: set up "My tone" and "My business facts" with this helper and keep them in a note on your phone.
2. Each evening (or twice a day): copy 10 to 15 unread customer messages. Change each name or number to Customer 1, Customer 2 before you paste. WhatsApp Web on a laptop makes copying easier.
3. Paste "My tone", "My business facts" and the messages into a new Claude chat and ask: "Sort my messages."
4. Label the "Needs you" chats **Needs me** and send their holding lines. Send the routine drafts. Save any new quick replies.
5. Handle the **Needs me** chats yourself, when you have time to give them proper attention.

## What this cannot do (honestly)
- **It can't read your WhatsApp or send anything.** Claude only sees what you paste, and you send every reply yourself. There is no official link between the WhatsApp Business app and Claude.
- **No auto-replies from this helper.** In the free app, greeting and away messages are the simple automatic replies. Any AI auto-reply the app offers is Meta's, not this helper's, and needs care with customer data.
- **An automatic reply bot** (one that reads and answers customers on its own) needs the **WhatsApp Business Platform** through Meta or an approved provider, the customer's opt-in, and approved message templates for some messages. It usually has running costs and needs care with customer data. That's a project for a builder, not this helper.
- **Avoid unofficial auto-reply apps** that read your notifications or ask you to scan a WhatsApp QR code in another app. They can get your number banned and they expose your customers' messages.
