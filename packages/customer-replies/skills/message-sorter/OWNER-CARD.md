# Your message sorter

**What it does:** you paste a pile of customer messages. It shows you the few that really need you, and writes the routine replies in your own tone, ready to send.

## What to type
- "Sort my messages. Cleaning service in Margao, 2BHK deep clean ₹4,500. Customer 1: how much for 2BHK? Customer 2: your team broke my lamp. Customer 3: do you work Sunday?"
- "Learn my tone. Here are 4 replies I sent last week: ..." (no names)

## What you get
- **Needs you:** the messages only you should answer (complaints, discounts, disputes, site visits, bookings to confirm, unusual or emotional ones), each with one calm line you can send now.
- **Routine: ready to send:** a reply for each of the rest, each in its own box with a **Copy** button.
- **Save as quick replies:** when the same question comes twice or more.
- One line at the end, like "8 messages: 2 need you, 6 drafted."
- Anything it doesn't know is left as [blank] for you to fill.

**Tip:** do "Learn my tone" and "Set up my facts" once. Keep both on your phone and paste them before your messages each time.

## Before you paste
WhatsApp copies the customer's name or number with the message. Change it to Customer 1, Customer 2 first.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every reply and send it yourself. It can't read your WhatsApp or reply on its own, and it won't promise a price, a slot or a refund you didn't give it.
