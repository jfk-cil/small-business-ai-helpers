## v1.0.0 (October 19, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Three jobs: sort a batch of messages (Needs you first, routine drafts, quick replies, one-line count), learn the owner's tone (8 lines or fewer), set up the owner's business facts.
- Domain notes for real estate (no promises on availability, price, possession, loans or title; "RERA reg. no.: [blank]"; site visits need the owner) and cleaning and home services (rate card only, "[check]", arrival windows only from the owner).
- WhatsApp Business setup notes: labels "Needs me", "Routine", "Waiting for reply", greeting and away messages, quick replies, and an honest note on what can't be automated.
- Pre-registered 6 evals and a 16-case test bank (MSR).
