# Example 01: an estate agent sorts an evening's messages (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sort my messages.
>
> My tone
> Greeting: "Hello!" then straight to the point
> Length: 2 to 4 short lines
> Formality: polite, friendly, not stiff
> Language: English; Hinglish if the customer writes Hinglish
> Sign-off: "Regards, Sample Homes"
> Words I use: "happy to help", "do share"
> Words I avoid: "sir/madam", "cheap", "hurry"
>
> My business facts
> Business: Sample Homes, real estate agent in Porvorim
> Areas we cover: Porvorim, Mapusa, Siolim
> Property types: resale flats and new projects, buy and rent
> Listed projects: Sample Greens, Porvorim (2BHK listed at ₹78 lakh)
> Office hours: 10 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday
> Office location: [location link]
> How site visits work: by appointment only
>
> Customer 1: Hi, can you send details of Sample Greens 2BHK?
> Customer 2: What are your office timings?
> Customer 3: I want to see the Sample Greens flat this Saturday 24 Oct, morning.
> Customer 4: 78 lakh is too much. Give me final price, I can pay full cash.
> Customer 5: hi what time is office open
> Customer 6: Do you have anything in Siolim for rent? 2BHK, family of 4.
> Customer 7: My father passed away and we need to sell his flat in Mapusa. I don't know where to start.
> Customer 8: thanks

**Helper replies:**

**Needs you**

**Customer 3:** site visit to confirm (Saturday October 24, morning). Label: Needs me
```
Hello! Thanks for your interest. Let me check and confirm shortly.
```

**Customer 4:** negotiation, asks for a final price. Label: Needs me
```
Hello! Thanks for your message. Let me look at this properly and get back to you shortly.
```

**Customer 7:** emotional, family sale after a loss. Label: Needs me
```
Hello. I'm so sorry for your loss. Thank you for telling me this. I'll reply to you personally shortly.
```

**Routine: ready to send**

**Customer 1** (details, Sample Greens). Label: Routine
```
Hello! Happy to help.
Sample Greens, Porvorim: 2BHK listed at ₹78 lakh.
RERA reg. no.: [blank]
Do share when you'd like to visit, and I'll check and confirm a time.
Regards, Sample Homes
```

**Customer 2 and Customer 5** (office hours). Label: Routine
```
Hello! Our office is open 10 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday.
Map: [location link]
Regards, Sample Homes
```

**Customer 6** (rent, Siolim 2BHK). Label: Waiting for reply
```
Hello! Yes, we cover Siolim, for rent too.
Let me check the latest availability and confirm shortly.
Do share your budget and move-in month.
Regards, Sample Homes
```

No reply needed: Customer 8 ("thanks").

**Fill in before sending:** the RERA registration number for Sample Greens, and your map link.
