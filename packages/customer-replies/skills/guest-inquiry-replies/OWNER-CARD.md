# Your guest replies helper

**What it does:** writes short WhatsApp answers to the questions guests ask, ready to save as Quick Replies so you can send them in two taps.

## What to type
- "Make quick replies for my homestay: ₹2,000 a night, check-in 12 pm, check-out 10 am, 50% advance."
- "A guest asked: 'Is the room free on Saturday and can we bring a dog?' Write my reply."

## What you get
- Each answer in its own box with a **Copy** button.
- A short **shortcut** word for each (like `price`). Type / and that word in any WhatsApp Business chat to send it.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].
- Your name, UPI ID and map link are left as [your business name], [your UPI ID] and [location link]. Type them in once when you save.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every reply, fix anything that's wrong, and send it yourself. It will not promise a room is free unless you tell it so.
