# Your listing matcher

**What it does:** keeps your listings in one fixed format, gives you a short form for what a client wants, finds the listings that fit from the rows you paste, and writes a WhatsApp message for each one plus a short shortlist.

## What to type
- "Client wants to rent a 2BHK in Porvorim or Alto Betim, up to ₹40,000 a month, from November, must have parking. My rows: (paste your rows). Make a shortlist and the messages."
- "My listings are all over WhatsApp. Give me one way to log them, and turn this into a row: 3BHK villa for sale, Saligao, 2,400 sq ft, asking ₹2.75 cr, ready now."

## What you get
- The columns for one listings sheet, and a "New listing" note to fill from a seller's message.
- A client form with 8 short questions (no name or number).
- A shortlist of up to 5 listings that fit, each with a plain reason. Anything that misses a must-have is marked.
- One message per listing and a one-message summary, each in its own box with a **Copy** button. Photo and video links are left as [photo/video link] for you to paste.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names (clients, sellers, landlords)
- Flat, unit, plot or house numbers and building names (use your listing code and the area)
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every detail and send it yourself. It can't read WhatsApp, open your photos or folders, remember your listings next time, or send anything: paste the rows you need each time. It won't add features a row doesn't have, promise prices or rental returns, or say anything about title, approvals or RERA (check those with a qualified person).
