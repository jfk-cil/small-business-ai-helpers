## v1.0.0 (October 20, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Tool-first get-paid routine for owners paid by deposit or after the job: empty list columns (Date, Job, Due date, Paid?) on paper or a sheet shared with no one, a Payment due label, /pay1 (friendly) and /pay2 (final, polite) quick replies with [amount] [job] [due date] [your UPI ID], a deposit line, a weekly 10-minute check, then a call or a talk in person. A paper version for normal WhatsApp.
- Never handles real amounts tied to a person, names, UPI IDs, bank details, GST numbers, invoices or payment screenshots. No late fees, threats, shaming or public posts; no legal, tax, GST or debt-collection advice ("check with your accountant or a qualified person").
- Accepts a pasted planning card as facts only. References: routine-card.md, reply-to-replies.md (kind replies when a customer answers a reminder) and conversation-starter.md.
- Test bank evals/testbank/GPR.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (6 cases), pre-registered October 20, 2026.
