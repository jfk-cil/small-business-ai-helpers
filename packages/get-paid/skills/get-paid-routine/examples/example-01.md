# Example 01: a cake studio sets up its get-paid routine (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Cake Studio, Margao. Customers pay 50% advance when they order, the rest on delivery. Some pay the balance late and I forget who still owes. I use WhatsApp Business and write to customers in English. A good week for me: no chasing money on Sundays. My first small move: make the Payment due label today.

**Helper replies:**

**1. Your list** (on paper, or a sheet shared with no one)
```
Date | Job | Due date | Paid?
```
Job is the work, never the customer's name ("chocolate cake, Saturday delivery"). Never share it by link or paste it into an AI chat. A sheet with formulas: **tracker-sheet-builder**.

**2. Payment due label:** WhatsApp Business > Labels > + > **Payment due**.

**3. Reminder quick replies**

**Shortcut:** `pay1` (friendly, when a payment is past due)
```
Hi! A friendly reminder that [amount] for [job] is due on [due date].
You can pay by UPI to [your UPI ID].
Thank you!
[your business name]
```

**Shortcut:** `pay2` (final and polite, a week after /pay1)
```
Hi! One last reminder by message: [amount] for [job] was due on [due date].
You can pay by UPI to [your UPI ID].
If something's not right, just reply and we can talk.
Thank you.
[your business name]
```
To save: WhatsApp Business > ⋮ (Android) or Settings (iPhone) > Business tools > Quick replies > +. You fill in the four blanks on your phone, just before sending.

**4. Deposit line** (add it to your order confirmation)
```
To confirm your order, please pay a 50% advance of [amount] by [due date] to [your UPI ID]. The rest is due on delivery.
```

**5. Your routine card** (save it in "Message yourself")
```
My get-paid routine
Job booked: send the confirmation with the deposit line. Add the Payment due label. Write it on my list.
Money arrives: tick Paid, remove the label.
Every week, same day, 10 min: open Payment due. Past due: send /pay1.
Still unpaid a week later: send /pay2. Then call or talk in person.
Kind and private: no added fees, no threats, no public posts.
Never in AI: amounts with a name, names, UPI or bank details, GST numbers, invoices, payment screenshots.
Working when: every Payment due chat has a date on my list.
```

**6. My helper** (save it in "Message yourself"; paste it into any AI chat when you need new wording)
```
Help me, the owner of a cake studio in Margao, write kind WhatsApp payment messages in English. Use only these blanks: [amount], [job], [due date], [your UPI ID]. I fill them in myself just before sending. Never ask me for, or include, names, amounts, UPI or bank details, invoices or payment screenshots. No added fees, threats or public posts. No legal, tax or GST advice. Short, friendly lines. Drafts only: I send them myself.
```

**7. This week** (so there's no chasing money on Sundays)
- Today: make the Payment due label, then save /pay1, /pay2 and the deposit line.
- Every day: new order, add the label and a line on your paper list; money in, tick Paid.
- [day, not Sunday]: your weekly 10-minute check, with a phone alarm.

For GST, tax, invoices, legal or debt questions, check with your accountant or a qualified person.

It's a draft: you fill in the blanks and send everything yourself. What would you change?

(Checks: no amount, name or UPI ID anywhere; the four blanks untouched in every box; only the owner's 50% rule used; reminders 138 and 198 characters; helper prompt 430 characters; routine card 534 characters; about 550 words in all.)
