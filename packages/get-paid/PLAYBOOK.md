# Get paid on time: your one-page playbook

Kind words, a private list, 10 minutes a week. Amounts, names and payment details stay on your phone and your paper, never in the AI.

## Set up once (20 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/get-paid/ and answer the questions. Say how people pay you in general ("an advance when booking, the rest after delivery"). No amounts tied to a person, no names.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), send it, and type "go".
3. Read /pay1 and /pay2 out loud. Would you be happy to get them? Change any word that doesn't sound like you. Leave [amount], [job], [due date] and [your UPI ID] exactly as they are.
4. Save both as quick replies: WhatsApp Business > Business tools > Quick replies > +. Shortcuts `pay1` and `pay2`. Normal WhatsApp? Pin your "Message yourself" chat and keep them there.
5. Type "ok" for the deposit line. Add it to your booking confirmation.
6. Type "ok" for the list columns. Write them at the top of a notebook page, or in a sheet in your own account shared with no one. Job is the work ("2BHK deep clean, Tuesday"), never the customer's name.
7. WhatsApp Business: make a label called **Payment due** (Labels > +).
8. Type "ok" for the weekly check. Set a repeating phone alarm: "Payments, 10 minutes", the same day each week.

## When a job is booked

- Send the confirmation with the deposit line. Fill in the blanks on your phone.
- Add the Payment due label to that chat.
- Write a line on your list: Date, Job, Due date.

## When money comes in

- Check it in your own payment app or bank, never in an AI chat.
- Tick Paid. Remove the label.

## Every week (10 minutes, on your alarm day)

- Open the Payment due label. Look at your list.
- Past due, no reminder yet: send /pay1. Fill in the blanks just before you send.
- /pay1 a week ago and still unpaid: send /pay2.
- /pay2 and still nothing: call or talk in person. No more messages.
- One person, one private chat. Never a group, a Status or a broadcast.

## When a customer replies

- "I already paid": "Thank you! Let me check and confirm shortly." Then check your own payment app.
- "I need more time": ask for a date that works, and write it in Due date. No added fee.
- A problem with the work or the amount: call them. Don't argue by message.
- Not sure what to say? Ask the helper for a kind reply. Paste their message with the name and amount removed.

## When a person must handle it

- Invoices, GST, tax and your accounts: your accountant or a qualified person.
- A late fee, a written agreement, or getting money back by law: a qualified person.
- A disagreement about the work or the amount: you, by phone or in person.

## Never type these

- Customers' names and phone numbers
- Amounts someone owes you, invoices, GST numbers or payment screenshots
- Bank, UPI and card details; passwords, PINs and OTPs
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Sales or money figures (your public prices and your general deposit rule are fine)
- Health information, staff ID numbers and wages, family members' details, photos of people

Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
