# A sales plan across channels: your one-page playbook

One plan, up to three channels, small steps each week. You visit, post and send everything yourself.

## Set up once (30 minutes, or two short sittings)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/sales-plan/ and answer the questions. Only true things. Name your busy and quiet months in your own words.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), send it, and type "go".
3. Read the channel map. Is the next action right for each channel? Fix anything that isn't how you sell.
4. Type "ok" for the 6-month plan. Check that the weekly steps fit your time. If not, say "I have less time" and ask again. If a festival is under "To place", tell the helper its month.
5. Type "ok" for the post bank. Copy it into a phone note called "Post ideas".
6. Type "ok" for cafés and shops. Make what "Have ready" asks for: a price list, a product card, a sample box. Prices and terms are your decision; fill each [blank] yourself.
7. Type "ok" for the monthly check. Paste the columns into row 1 of a Google Sheet in your own account.
8. Send the helper and the plan to yourself on WhatsApp ("Message yourself"). Set a weekly phone reminder: "Sales plan", the same day each week.

## Every week (your planned time)

- Open the plan at this month. Do the steps for each channel, in the order the plan gives.
- **Cafés and shops:** one first message, or one tasting visit. Send the message yourself. Ask before you visit, never at their rush hour.
- **WhatsApp:** send your weekly list only to people who joined. Anyone who says STOP comes off the list at once.
- **Posts:** pick 2 ideas from the bank. Take the photos in one go. Ask for captions if you need them.
- **Busy week?** Drop what the plan says to drop first. Never drop replying to orders.
- Make a tally mark for each order, by channel.

## Every month (20 minutes)

1. Fill in one row of the monthly check: counts only, no money.
2. Compare with your own last month. Where do people stop: they taste but don't order, join but don't buy, save posts but don't message?
3. Write one change for next month. Only one.
4. Refresh the post bank: keep the ideas that got saves or messages, remove the ones that got nothing twice, add ideas for next month.
5. Check in with each café or shop that buys from you: "Anything to change for next month?"

## When a person must handle it

- Prices, wholesale terms, discounts, credit and delivery days for cafés: you decide.
- Food safety and licensing, food labels, ingredient claims and GST: a qualified person.
- A quote for a café: you, with the quote helper in the [Reply to customer messages](../customer-replies/) package.
- Paid ads or boosting posts: a person who runs ads; you set the budget.
- An automatic WhatsApp bot: a separate builder project.

## Never type these

- Customers' names, phone numbers or your contact list; café or shop owners' names and numbers
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details; bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs, including your Instagram, Facebook or Google login
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine)
- Health information, staff ID numbers and wages, family members' details, photos of people

Describe photos in words. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
