# Your social posts helper

**What it does:** plans your posts for a week or a month and writes the captions, so you always know what to post.

## What to type
- "I run a homestay in Assagao. Rooms free from the 20th. Plan my Instagram posts for next week."
- "Make a month of posts for my cafe in Panjim. We have a music night coming, date not fixed yet."

## What you get
- 3 posts for a week, or 12 for a month (ask for more or fewer).
- For each post: the day, a photo or short video idea you can shoot on your phone, a ready caption, and 3 to 5 local hashtags.
- A short "To fill in" list for anything you didn't tell it, like a price or a date.
- For Google Business posts, use the google-profile-builder helper instead.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It won't
- Make up reviews, star ratings or customer quotes.
- Suggest posting photos of people, especially children, without their permission.
- Promise giveaways, prices or dates you didn't give it.
- Write health or cure claims, or dress up a paid or gifted post as a customer's own opinion.

## It's a draft
You check every word, price and date, then you post it yourself.
