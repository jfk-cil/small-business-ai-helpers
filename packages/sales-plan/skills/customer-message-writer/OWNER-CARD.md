# Your customer messages helper

**What it does:** writes short WhatsApp messages for customers who asked to hear from you: new stock, offers, reorder reminders, payment reminders and thank-yous.

## What to type
- "Write a broadcast: new mango jam in stock, ₹180 a jar, order by replying."
- "Write a reminder for customers who buy dog food every month, so they reorder."

## What you get
- A message in a box with a **Copy** button.
- It ends with your name ([your business name]) and a line telling people they can reply STOP.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].
- A short reminder of who to send it to.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names, or customer lists
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## Only for people who said yes
Send these only to customers who agreed to hear from you. Someone who ordered once hasn't agreed to offers yet, so ask them first. It won't write for bought or copied number lists. If someone replies STOP, take them off your list. Send on WhatsApp only, not as bulk SMS or calls.

## It's a draft
You check every message, fix anything that's wrong, and send it yourself.
