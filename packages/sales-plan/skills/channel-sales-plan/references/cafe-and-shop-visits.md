# Cafés and shops: first message and tasting visit

For selling to other businesses: cafés, shops, homestays, resorts, offices. The owner chooses which businesses to approach (to find them, use **target-customer-finder**) and sends every message themselves.

## The first message (70 words or fewer)
Short, about them, one ask. No prices, terms or claims the owner didn't give. Ends with [your business name].

```
Hi! I'm [your name] from [your business name] in [your area]. We make [what you make]. I think [one honest reason it suits their place, in your words]. Could I drop by with a few samples for you to taste? Which day and time suits you?
[your business name]
```

Change it to the owner's words (for non-food, "for you to see" instead of "for you to taste"). If the owner already knows the café owner, start with how ("We met at [where]"). For a longer email, a pitch or follow-ups, use **outreach-email-writer**.

## Tasting visit plan (numbered steps, in the reply)
For a business that doesn't sell food (laundry, candles, soaps, flowers), make it a sample visit: bring a sample of your work instead of a tasting box, and keep the same steps.
1. **Before:** ask for a day and time that suits them (never turn up at a rush hour unasked). Bake or pack the samples that morning, in the amounts you decide: [blank].
2. **Bring:** the sample box, your price list for businesses ([blank: your business prices]), a menu or product card with ingredients in your words, and how to order (your WhatsApp number on the card, [blank: how many days ahead]).
3. **At the visit:** let them taste; ask what they serve now, what sells with their customers, and what they'd want from you. Write down their answers after you leave, without names or numbers in any AI chat.
4. **Don't promise** sales, delivery days, credit, returns or prices you haven't decided. Say: "Let me check and send you a proper quote." For the quote, use **quote-and-proposal-writer**.
5. **After:** a thank-you message the same day, in your words. Follow up once after [blank: how many days]. If they say no or don't reply after that, thank them and stop.
6. **When they say yes:** agree the order steps in writing on WhatsApp (what, how many, when, how to pay: [your UPI ID] or [blank]). Wholesale terms (credit, returns, sale-or-return, who pays for delivery) and food safety and FSSAI labeling: check with a qualified person.

## Businesses that already buy from you
Instead of a first message:
1. Once a month, a short check-in: "How did [item] go this month? Anything to change for next month?"
2. Ask before the owner's season changes (in the owner's words) whether their order should change.
3. Ask if you may mention them in a post ("Find our [item] at [café name]"), and only post it if they agree.

## Never
- Name a real café, shop or person.
- Suggest a wholesale price, discount, margin, minimum order, delivery schedule or credit period.
- Write that the food is healthy or treats anything. Describe taste, ingredients the owner names and how it is made.
- Say the café "will" sell a number of items, or that the owner's product "will" bring them customers.
