# Example 01: a bakery selling to cafés, on WhatsApp and on Instagram (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> I run Sample Bakehouse in Aldona. Sourdough, banana bread and brownies. I want to supply cafés near the beaches, sell direct to people on WhatsApp, and post on Instagram. For me tourists come November to March, Christmas in December is our biggest month, and Carnival is busy too. About 3 hours a week. Start November, 6 months. I haven't started any marketing yet.

**Helper replies:**

**Your 6-month sales plan: [your business name]**
November to April · cafés, WhatsApp, Instagram · about 3 hours a week

**A. Your channels**

**1. Cafés near the beaches**
- Who buys: café owners who want fresh bakes for the tourist season.
- What they buy: sourdough, banana bread, brownies.
- Next action: send the first message (below) to one café you choose this week.
- Have ready: a price list for cafés ([blank: your café prices]), a sample box, a product card with ingredients in your words, your delivery days ([blank]).
- How orders come in: [blank: how cafés will order, and how many days ahead].

**2. WhatsApp (direct)**
- Who buys: people who already buy from you, and people who find you through a café or your posts.
- What they buy: sourdough, banana bread and brownies for home and gifts.
- Next action: put the join line (below) in your bio, your Status and on a small card in every order.
- Have ready: a weekly bake list ([blank: your prices]), payment by [your UPI ID], labels "New order", "Paid", "Delivered".
- How orders come in: a chat to your business number; you confirm each order by hand.

**3. Instagram**
- Who buys: people nearby, and visitors in tourist season.
- What they buy: what the posts show.
- Next action: pick 3 ideas from the post bank and photograph them in one go this week.
- Have ready: a bio with the join line; photos of bakes and hands (no faces without permission).
- How orders come in: a DM or the WhatsApp link in your bio.

**B. Your week** (fits about 3 hours)
- Cafés: one first message, or one tasting visit (about 75 minutes with the visit).
- WhatsApp: send your bake list to the people who joined, and answer orders (about 30 minutes).
- Instagram: photograph and post 2 ideas from the bank (about 60 minutes).
- If a week is busy: skip the café step that week, never the order replies.

**Month 1 · November: Getting ready for the season** (your words: tourists come November to March)
- Cafés: make the price list, product card and sample box; send the first message to two cafés you choose.
- WhatsApp: join line in your bio, Status and order cards; set up labels.
- Instagram: Behind the work, How to order.

**Month 2 · December: Christmas** (your words: your biggest month)
- Cafés: tasting visits with the cafés that replied; ask about their Christmas orders.
- WhatsApp: tell the list your Christmas bakes and the last day to order ([blank: last order date]).
- Instagram: Seasons and festivals, The products.

**Month 3 · January: Steady in the busy season**
- Cafés: a monthly check-in with cafés that buy; one new tasting visit.
- WhatsApp: the weekly bake list, same day each week.
- Instagram: Where to find us (only with the café's permission), Behind the work.

**Month 4 · February: Regulars first**
- Cafés: one new tasting visit; check in with the cafés that buy.
- WhatsApp: ask the list what they'd like you to bake more often.
- Instagram: Your story, The products.

**Month 5 · March: End of your tourist season** (your words: tourists until March)
- Cafés: ask each café how their order should change after March.
- WhatsApp: thank the list; tell them what you'll bake after the season.
- Instagram: Moments, How to order.

**Month 6 · April: Locals and regulars**
- Cafés: keep the cafés that still order; monthly check-in.
- WhatsApp: the weekly bake list and orders for parties at home.
- Instagram: How to order, Behind the work.

**To place:** Carnival ([blank: which month]). Tell me the month and I'll make it that month's theme.

**C. Your post bank** (18 ideas; for captions, ask **social-content-planner**)
```
Theme: Behind the work
- Hands shaping sourdough before sunrise
- The first tray of brownies out of the oven
- Packing a café order
Theme: The products

- Banana bread sliced on a board
- Brownies cut into squares, from above
Theme: Seasons and festivals
- What we bake for Christmas
- The first week of tourist season at the bakery
- Carnival bakes: [blank: what you bake for Carnival]
Theme: Where to find us
- Our bakes at a café near the beach (with the café's permission)
- Banana bread on a café counter (with permission)
- The delivery bag on its way to a café
Theme: How to order
- How to join our WhatsApp bake list
- How to order for a party at home
- What to order ahead for Christmas
Theme: Your story
- Why you started baking in Aldona
- Your favorite ingredient and where it comes from
- A morning at the bakery, start to finish
```
**Monthly refresh:** (1) from your monthly check, mark the ideas that got the most saves or messages; (2) move them to the top and post them again with a new photo; (3) remove ideas that got nothing after two tries; (4) add ideas for next month's theme, then ask **social-content-planner** for captions.

**D. Cafés and shops**

**First message** (send it yourself to a café you choose)
```
Hi! I'm [your name] from [your business name] in Aldona. We bake sourdough, banana bread and brownies. I think [one honest reason it suits your café]. Could I drop by with a few samples for you to taste? Which day and time suits you?
[your business name]
```

**Tasting visit plan**
1. Ask for a day and time that suits them, not their rush hour. Bake the samples that morning, in the amounts you decide: [blank].
2. Bring the sample box, your café price list ([blank: your café prices]), the product card and how to order.
3. Let them taste; ask what they serve now and what they'd want from you. Write notes after you leave, with no names or numbers in any AI chat.
4. Don't promise delivery days, credit, returns or prices you haven't decided. Say "Let me check and send you a proper quote." For the quote, use **quote-and-proposal-writer**.
5. Send a thank-you the same day. Follow up once after [blank: how many days]; if there's no reply, thank them and stop.
6. When a café says yes, agree the order steps in writing on WhatsApp. Wholesale terms, food safety and FSSAI labeling: check with a qualified person.

**WhatsApp join line** (for your bio, Status and order cards; only people who choose to join)
```
Want our weekly bake list on WhatsApp? Tap [WhatsApp link] or scan the code, say YES and save our number. Leave any time: reply STOP.
[your business name]
```

**E. Monthly check** (paste into row 1 of a Google Sheet)
```
Month	Café orders	WhatsApp orders	Orders from Instagram	New cafés	WhatsApp sign-ups	Saves or messages from posts	Tasting visits	One change next month
```
Target: [blank: your target]. Count orders, not money. Sales totals stay in your own accounts book, not in an AI chat.

**Fill in:** your café prices and delivery days; how cafés will order and how many days ahead; your bake list prices; the last day for Christmas orders; Carnival's month; one honest reason per café; your name and business name; your WhatsApp link.

It's a draft: you check it, and you visit, post and send everything yourself. Try it for a month and count what happens.
