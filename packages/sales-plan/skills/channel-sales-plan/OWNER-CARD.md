# Your channel sales plan helper

**What it does:** makes one sales plan for several months when you sell in up to 3 ways, for example to cafés and shops, direct to people on WhatsApp, and through Instagram or Facebook posts.

## What to type
- "I bake in Aldona. Plan 6 months from November: supply cafés, sell on WhatsApp, post on Instagram. Tourists come November to March. 3 hours a week."
- "Sales plan across channels for my pickles: grocery shops, my WhatsApp Community and Facebook. 12 months. 2 hours a week."

## What you get
- **Your channels:** for each one, who buys, what they buy, your next step, what to have ready (price list, samples, menu card) and how orders come in.
- **Month by month:** a theme for each month from your own seasons and festivals, and small weekly steps that fit your time.
- **A post bank:** 12 to 24 post ideas you can reuse, and a once-a-month refresh.
- **For cafés and shops:** a short first message and a plan for a tasting visit.
- **A monthly check:** a sheet to count orders, new cafés, WhatsApp sign-ups and post messages. Your target stays yours to fill in.
- Anything you didn't tell it (prices, delivery days, festival months) is marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people
- Sales totals and money records (keep them in your own accounts book)

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check everything, and you visit, post and send it yourself. It can't post or message for you. It won't promise more sales, invent prices or wholesale terms, or say your food is healthy. Message only people who chose to join, and ask a group's admin before posting there.
