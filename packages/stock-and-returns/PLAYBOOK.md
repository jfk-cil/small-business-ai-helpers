# Stock checks and returns: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (20 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/stock-and-returns/ and answer the questions. Roles, not names: "store assistant", "billing staff".
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), send it, and type "go".
3. Read the routine out loud. Fix any step that is not how you really do it. Fill every [blank]: the day of the month, how long each step takes, what near expiry means for you.
4. Type "ok" for the how-to. Ask the person who set up your billing software to show the purchase return menu once, and write its real name over each [menu name in your billing software].
5. Type "ok" for the tracker columns. Paste them into row 1 of a new Google Sheet in your own account.
6. Type "ok" for the supplier messages. Save the request and the follow-up in a phone note.
7. Print the routine and the how-to. Keep them with the "Set aside" box.
8. Set a repeating reminder in your phone calendar for the check day each month.

## Every month (on your check day)

- **Count:** staff go shelf by shelf, then the storeroom. Expired and near-expiry items go in the tracker with batch, expiry date and quantity.
- **Set aside:** those items go in one labeled box, away from the selling shelves.
- **Decide:** you mark each line Return, Keep or Other, after checking your supplier's terms.
- **Return:** billing staff make one purchase return per supplier. Staff pack one box per supplier with a copy inside.
- **Ask:** you send the request to each supplier, and write the date in the tracker.
- **Sign off:** each step gets initials and a date. It means done, not how well.

## Until it's picked up (5 minutes, twice a week)

- Open the tracker. Any request with no reply by the day you picked? Send the one polite follow-up.
- Still no reply? Call the supplier yourself and note the date and what they said.
- At pickup, get your copy of the purchase return signed or stamped. Write the date.
- Credit note or replacement in? Mark it Yes.

## Once a month, with your team (10 minutes)

- Ask one question: "Which step was hard, and which is missing?"
- Ask the helper to rewrite that step. Print the new routine.
- New staff member? Give them the how-to and do the first check together.

## When a person must handle it

- Whether expired or near-expiry items can be sold, discounted, donated or thrown away, and how: a qualified person and your supplier's terms.
- Medicines, supplements and vet products: a qualified person.
- A dispute with a supplier, or GST on returns: a qualified person, or your accountant.
- A problem with one person's work: you, face to face. Never a tracker, a sheet or an AI.

## Never type these

- Customers' names and phone numbers
- Staff names, ID numbers or wages; the supplier contact's name or number
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details; bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs, including your billing software login
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine), stock value and credit-note amounts
- Health information, family members' details, photos of people

Quantities and dates are fine. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
