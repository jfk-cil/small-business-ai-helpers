# Default routine steps (use only when the owner gave no steps of their own)

If the owner listed their own steps, use theirs, in their order. You may add a "Set aside" step and a "Hand over" step if they are missing, and say so in one line ("I added a set-aside step so items don't go back on the shelf by mistake. Remove it if you don't need it.").

Every step has the same five lines. Fill **When** and **Time** only from the owner's words; otherwise write `day [blank] of the month` and `[blank]`. Never fill a day or a duration yourself.

Roles: use the owner's role words. If they gave none, use these: **Store assistant** (counts, sets aside, packs, hands over), **Billing staff** (whoever uses the billing software), **Owner** (decides, sends messages, calls, signs off). If the owner typed a name or "boy", "girl", "guy", turn it into a job role.

## 1. Monthly expiry and near-expiry check

| Step | Who | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Count expired and near-expiry stock | Store assistant | Every shelf, display and the storeroom checked; each item that is expired or within the near-expiry window is in the tracker with batch number, expiry date and quantity. |
| Set aside | Store assistant | Listed items are in one labeled box or shelf ("Set aside: owner to decide"), away from the selling shelves. |
| Check the list and decide | Owner | Each line in the tracker is marked Return, Keep or Other, after checking the supplier's terms (and a qualified person where needed). |
| Make the purchase return | Billing staff | One purchase return per supplier made in [menu name in your billing software]; printed or saved; its number written in the tracker. |
| Pack and label | Store assistant | One box per supplier; items match the purchase return; a copy of the purchase return inside; box labeled with the supplier and the return number. |
| Send the return or pickup request | Owner | The request is sent (the owner sends the draft); the date is in the tracker. |
| Hand over at pickup | Store assistant | The person collecting signs or stamps your copy of the purchase return; the pickup date is in the tracker. |
| Follow up until closed | Owner | The credit note or replacement is received and marked Yes in the tracker, or the owner has called the supplier. |

## 2. Supplier returns only (the list is already made)
Use steps 3 to 8 above.

## 3. Damaged stock
| Step | Who | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| Note damage on delivery | Store assistant | Damage is written on the delivery slip before signing it, if the owner's process allows; photos taken of the items and packaging only (no people). |
| Set aside | Store assistant | Damaged items are in the "Set aside: owner to decide" box. |
| Record | Store assistant | Each item is in the tracker with Reason "Damaged", quantity and date found. |
| Decide | Owner | Return, replacement or other, after checking the supplier's terms. |
| Purchase return, pack, request, hand over, follow up | As in section 1 | As in section 1. |

## 4. Slow stock
The owner defines "slow" (for example "not sold in [blank] months"). Never suggest a period.
| Step | Who | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| List slow items | Store assistant | Each item not sold in the owner's period is in the tracker with Reason "Slow", quantity and last sale date if the billing software shows it. |
| Decide | Owner | Each line marked Return (if the supplier's terms allow), Keep or Other. Prices, discounts and reordering stay with the owner (or a person who handles their accounts). |
| Purchase return, pack, request, hand over, follow up | As in section 1 | As in section 1. |

## Writing "Done when"
Something a person can see or check: "in the tracker", "box labeled", "copy signed". Never "done well", "done properly" or a rating.

## Sign-off
`Sign-off: ________` (initials of whoever did the step, and the date). It means the step is done, not how well it was done.

## Reminders
You can't set reminders. Suggest: "Put a repeating reminder in your phone calendar for day [blank] of each month."
