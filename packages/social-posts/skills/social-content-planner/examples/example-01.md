# Example 01: a week of posts for Sample Studio (fictional)

## Owner's message
> Sample Studio, pottery classes in Assagao. Saturday beginner class 10 to 12, ₹1,500, max 6 people. I post on Instagram. What should I post this week?

## Output

**Your posts: Sample Studio, this week**

**1. Monday · how it's made**
Photo or reel: reel, 20 seconds. Shot 1: wet clay on the wheel. Shot 2: hands shaping a bowl. Shot 3: the finished bowl on a shelf. On-screen text: "From clay to bowl." Use the app's music library.
Caption:
Every bowl starts as a lump of clay.
Want to make your own? Our beginner class is this Saturday.
Hashtags: #Assagao #GoaPottery #PotteryClass #NorthGoa

**2. Wednesday · the place**
Photo or reel: the studio table set with 6 wheels and tools, no people.
Caption:
Saturday's table is ready.
Beginner class, 10 to 12, ₹1,500. Only 6 places.
WhatsApp us at [your phone or WhatsApp] to book.
Hashtags: #Assagao #GoaWorkshops #PotteryGoa

**3. Friday · question**
Photo or reel: three finished pieces side by side.
Caption:
If you made one thing on the wheel, what would it be: a cup, a bowl or a plate?
Tell us below.
Hashtags: #GoaPottery #Assagao #Handmade

**To fill in:**
your phone or WhatsApp

**Before you post:** ask permission before showing anyone's face. Check every price, date and time.

## Why it looks like this
- Price, time and group size come only from the owner's message.
- "Only 6 places" is true (the owner said max 6), so it is fine to say.
- No student faces: the photo ideas use hands, tools and finished pieces.
