# Your weekly posts helper

**What it does:** helps you post every week with what you already have: a phone album, the app you already post on, a phone reminder and your Google profile. It writes the captions. You take the photos and post.

## What to type
- "Sample Beach Shack, Morjim. I never post. I just took a photo of our grilled fish plate with the sea behind. WhatsApp us to book a table. Set me up."
- "This week's 3 photos: our veranda, breakfast plate, the garden path. End each with: Message us on WhatsApp to book."

## What you get
- **Today (about 10 minutes):** make an album called "Photos for posts", take one photo (no people, never children), post one caption now, set a weekly reminder.
- **Every week (20 minutes):** describe 3 photos, get 3 captions. Post one now, save two as drafts.
- **Every month:** check your Google profile hours, phone and links. Add 2 new photos.
- **My helper:** a short prompt to keep in WhatsApp "Message yourself" and paste into any AI chat each week.
- Each caption in its own box with a **Copy** button, ending with one clear way to message or book. Anything you didn't say is marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people
- Anything on your planning card's "Keep out of any AI" line

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every caption, fix anything untrue, and post it yourself. It can't post, schedule or open your Google profile, and it won't make up offers, prices or reviews.

**It's working when:** you post 3 times a week for 3 weeks in a row, and your Google hours are right.
