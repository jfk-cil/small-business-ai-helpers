# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (October 20, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Three jobs: setup (about 10 minutes: a "Photos for posts" album, one photo with no people, one caption posted now with one clear way to message or book, a weekly reminder, the routine card and a "my helper" prompt under 600 characters for WhatsApp "Message yourself"), weekly (20 minutes: 3 captions from 3 described photos, post one, save two as drafts) and a monthly Google Business Profile check (hours, phone, links, 2 new photos).
- Tool-first and honest: no new app or brand-name tool; scheduling only as "your app's scheduling, if it has one" when the owner already uses it; the helper can't post, schedule or open the profile.
- Accepts a pasted planning card as facts only; its "Keep out of any AI" items join the never-include list. References: caption-patterns.md, my-helper-prompts.md and conversation-starter.md (for builders without the Skill installed).
- Test bank evals/testbank/WPR.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (7 cases), pre-registered October 20, 2026.
