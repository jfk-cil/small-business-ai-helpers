# Example 01: an art workshop's engagement system (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> I run Sample Art Studio in Assagao. I teach watercolor workshops at my studio and sell prints, and I want to start digital workshops. My process reels get me new people, but they don't stay or book. People book by Instagram DM. I have notes and plans but no time: about 3 hours a week.

**Helper replies:**

**Your engagement system: [your business name]**

**1. Three pillars, one weekly series each**
- **Value:** "Tuesday Watercolor Tip": one small tip from your own work, filmed over your hands. People save these.
- **Process:** "Friday Blank to Done": one print from first wash to finish, sped up. Your process reels already work, so this keeps them coming on a set day.
- **Offer:** "Sunday What's On": your next workshop or a new print, and exactly how to book.

**2. Your path from watching to buying**
1. Post: any series above.
2. Ask: "Comment PAINT and I'll send you my free color-mixing checklist."
3. Free thing (you make it): a color-mixing checklist, a starter supplies list, or a short mini-lesson on one wash. Pick one from your notes; I can draft its text from your notes when you're ready.
4. Join: your WhatsApp Community announcement group or a broadcast list, only if they reply YES.
5. Offer: tell the list about each workshop (and later your digital workshop) first, then post it.

**DM reply** (when someone comments PAINT)
```
Hi! Here's your color-mixing checklist: [link]
I share one watercolor tip and workshop news on WhatsApp, about [how often].
Want to join? Reply YES and I'll send the link.
[your business name]
```

**Welcome message** (when someone joins)
```
Welcome! You'll get one watercolor tip and workshop news, about [how often].
To leave, reply STOP or exit any time.
[your business name]
```

**3. Your week** (fits your 3 hours)
- Monday: film all three posts in one go (about 1 hour).
- Tuesday, Friday, Sunday: edit and post that day's series (about 15 minutes each).
- Every day: one reply slot for comments and DMs, sending the checklist to everyone who wrote PAINT (about 10 minutes).
- Total: about 2 hours 55 minutes. If a week gets busy, drop a post, not the reply slot.

**4. What to count each week** (Google Sheet header)
```
Week of	Posts	Saves	Shares	Keyword comments	DMs	New joiners	Sign-ups	Sales	One change to try
```
One row a week. Compare only with your own last week, and pick one change to try. For a sheet with formulas, use **tracker-sheet-builder**.

**Fill in:** the link to your checklist (once you make it) and how often you'll message the list.

It's a draft: you make, post and send everything yourself. I can't send messages or add people for you; save the two messages above as quick replies (WhatsApp Business > Business tools > Quick replies; on Instagram, saved replies in your professional account's tools).

Want a week of posts for these three series? Ask for "a week that converts."
