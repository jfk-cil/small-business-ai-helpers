# Your marketing plan helper

**What it does:** gives you a one-page, 4-week plan to try for more customers, and a way to see if it worked, using the apps and time you already have.

## What to type
- "Bakery in Panjim. Our cookies just sit on the shelf. I have 20 minutes a day and use Instagram. Make me a plan for next month."
- "Make one plan for my rooms, yoga classes and cafe. We're in Assagao."

## What you get
- One goal, who to reach, what to offer, and where to say it.
- A Week 1 to Week 4 list of small things to do.
- A simple way to tell if it worked: things you count on paper each week, like how many people asked about your offer.
- A "To fill in" list for anything you didn't say, like a price or your target.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

You don't need to paste your sales or money records either. Keep those on paper.

## It won't
- Promise you a number of customers or bookings. No one honestly can.
- Make up discounts, prices or numbers.
- Push you into paid ads. Ads are always optional.
- Plan messages to people who didn't agree to hear from you.

## It's a draft
You decide the offer and the price, you check the plan, and you do it yourself.
