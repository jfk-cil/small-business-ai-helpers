# Example 01: a surf school's reels for the first week of the season (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Surf School, Arambol. Beginner lessons every morning, boards and rash vests included, we also teach kids on weekends. The season just started. People book on WhatsApp. Give me 3 reels for this week.

**Helper replies:**

**Your reels this week: [your business name]**

**Reel 1 · Monday · the season starts**
**Hook:** The season's started, and the boards are back on the sand
**Shots** (film alone, phone on a stand)
1. Wide shot of the boards lined up on the sand, early morning.
2. Close on your hands laying a rash vest next to each board.
3. Your feet walking toward the water, board under your arm, filmed from behind.
4. The empty line-up at the water's edge, slow pan.
**On-screen text**
```
The season's started
Beginner lessons every morning
Board and rash vest included
```
**Voice-over or caption** (72 words, about 35 seconds)
```
The season's started, and the boards are back on the sand.
At Sample Surf School in Arambol, we teach beginner lessons every morning.
You don't need your own gear: the board and the rash vest are included.
[one line in your own words about the first mornings of the season]
If you've been meaning to try surfing, come and try it with us.
Message us on WhatsApp to book a morning lesson.
```

**Reel 2 · Wednesday · a question people ask**
**Hook:** "Do I need my own board?" Here's the answer.
**Shots** (film alone, phone on a stand)
1. The question as text over a shot of the empty beach.
2. Close on your hand patting a board on the sand.
3. Close on a rash vest being folded on top of it.
4. Wide shot of the boards ready for the morning.
**On-screen text**
```
Do I need my own board?
No. It's included.
Rash vest too.
```
**Voice-over or caption** (76 words, about 30 seconds)
```
"Do I need my own board?" Here's the answer.
If you're new to surfing, this one's for you.
No, you don't. At Sample Surf School in Arambol, your board and rash vest are included in every beginner lesson.
Lessons are in the morning.
[one line in your own words about what to bring, or leave it out]
So you can come as you are and we'll have your gear ready.
Message us on WhatsApp to book.
```

**Reel 3 · Saturday · kids on weekends**
**Hook:** Weekend surf lessons for kids, here in Arambol
**Shots** (film alone, phone on a stand; no children in any shot)
1. Smaller boards lined up on the sand, no people.
2. Close on your hands laying out the rash vests.
3. A wide shot of the beach from behind the boards.
**On-screen text**
```
Kids' surf lessons
Every weekend
Board and rash vest included
```
**Voice-over or caption** (71 words, about 30 seconds)
```
Weekend surf lessons for kids, here in Arambol.
At Sample Surf School, we teach beginners every morning, and on weekends we teach kids too.
Boards and rash vests are included.
Ages: [blank]. Lesson time: [blank].
[one line in your own words about teaching kids]
If your child has been asking to try surfing, a weekend lesson is a simple way to start.
Message us on WhatsApp to book a weekend lesson.
```

**Before you film**
- Faces: adults only with their permission. Never children's faces, so Reel 3 shows only boards, gear and the beach.
- Music: use the app's own music library. No song or trend is promised to get more views.
- Film only your own boards, beach spot and gear.

**Fill in:** your business name in the heading; your lines about the first mornings, what to bring and teaching kids; kids' ages and lesson time.

It's a draft: you film, check and post everything yourself.

(Hooks: 11, 9 and 8 words. Voice-overs: 72, 76 and 71 words, each box under 500 characters.)
