# Your reel script helper

**What it does:** writes short video scripts (reels, shorts, status videos) from what you really did this week. You can film every shot alone on your phone.

## What to type
- "Sample Surf School, Arambol. Beginner lessons every morning, boards included. Season just started. People book on WhatsApp. 3 reels for this week."
- "Pottery studio in Siolim. I glazed a batch of blue mugs this week. One reel."

## What you get
- 3 to 5 reel ideas for the week (3 unless you ask), or one reel.
- For each: a **hook** (one short line to start), 3 to 6 **shots** to film (hands, work, your place, your product), **on-screen text**, and a **voice-over or caption** of 30 to 60 seconds that ends with one next step.
- Text and voice-over each in a box with a **Copy** button. Anything you didn't tell it is marked [blank].
- A paid or gifted post starts with "Ad" or "Collaboration with [partner]". Also turn on the platform's paid-partnership label.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You film, check and post everything yourself. Show faces only with the person's permission, and never children's faces. It won't promise views, pick a "viral" song, invent reviews, or write before-and-after or health claims. Use the app's own music library.
