# Posts that bring customers

A week of posts and captions in your own voice, with photo ideas you can shoot yourself, and a simple way to turn people who watch into people who buy.

## The problem

Two problems come up again and again for cafés, homestays, shops, studios and workshop businesses.

The first is time. You know you should post. Your customers look for you on Instagram, WhatsApp Status, Facebook and Google. But after a full day of work there is no time left to think of what to say, which photo to take, or which hashtags to use. So posting stops for weeks, then starts again in a rush.

The second comes once you do post. Your reels and posts get views, even new followers, but few of those people stay, message you or buy. There is no steady format that keeps them coming back, and no clear next step from watching to booking. You may have notes and plans for a better routine, but no time to follow them.

## What you get

- A weekly post calendar: the day, the idea in one line, and the kind of post (behind the scenes, how it's made, your place, a tip, a question, and now and then an offer).
- A ready caption for each post, in your voice, each in its own copy box.
- One clear next step in each post (save this, comment a word to get your free guide, or message to book), and 3 short replies to save for comments and messages.
- 3 to 5 hashtags per post, at least two tied to your place.
- A photo or short video idea for each post that you can shoot on your phone in a few minutes.
- 3 caption options when you describe one thing you want to share.
- For Google Business posts, use the [Get found on Google](../get-found-on-google/) package.
- A one-page, 4-week plan: one goal, who to reach, what to offer, which channels, small steps each week, and simple counts to see if it worked.

The full version (Skills) also:
- designs a simple engagement system: three kinds of posts (useful tips, behind the scenes, what's on offer), each a named weekly series; an honest path from a post to a free small thing you make, then to your WhatsApp list (only for people who say yes), then to your workshop or product; a weekly rhythm that fits your time; and a short weekly count;
- writes a week of posts that convert, with 5 quick replies for comments and DMs;
- plans an online workshop launch: an outline from your notes, a two-week launch plan with no fake rush, and sign-up, thank-you and feedback messages.

## Use it in 2 minutes on your phone

Open the phone page: https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/social-posts/

1. Answer four short questions: your business and where, where you post, what makes you special, and anything happening this week. Every question is optional.
2. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**.
3. Open Claude, or the AI you already use, start a new chat, paste the helper and send it.
4. Type "week". It writes a plan for 3 posts (ask for more), each with one clear next step, plus 3 short replies to save. Or describe one thing to share and get 3 caption options.

If you open the page inside Claude, a **Try it here** box lets you run it on the page itself. It runs on your own Claude account. This page saves nothing.

Send the helper to yourself on WhatsApp ("Message yourself") so you can copy it again every week.

## Limits

- **Drafts only, no auto-posting.** It cannot log in to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or Google, and it cannot post, schedule, reply to comments or send DMs for you. You check every word and post or send it yourself. Automatic WhatsApp replies need the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, with each customer's opt-in: a separate builder project.
- **People choose to join.** Your WhatsApp Community, Channel or broadcast list is only for people who say yes. It never adds anyone without asking, never uses bought or copied lists, and treats STOP as stop, at once.
- **No fake engagement.** No bought followers, likes or views, no follow-for-follow, and no giveaways that make people tag others.
- **Only true claims.** It uses only what you tell it. It will not invent offers, prices, discounts, awards, reviews, customer quotes, student results or urgency (no "only 3 seats left" unless it's true), and it will not say "best" or "No. 1" unless you say you can show it.
- **No health claims.** It will not write that a food, drink, class or treatment cures or prevents anything.
- **People in photos need permission.** It suggests hands, food, rooms, views and you. For a customer's or staff member's face, ask first. Never children's faces.
- **No promised results.** No one can promise a number of likes, followers, customers or bookings, and it won't claim to know what "the algorithm" rewards. The plan gives you simple counts so you can see for yourself.
- **Paid ads are optional.** It never makes ads the whole plan and never predicts reach or sales.
- **Free and paid plans.** The phone helper works on any Claude plan, including free, and in ChatGPT or Gemini too. Uploading Skills needs a paid Claude plan with code execution turned on.

## Privacy

Never type these into any AI chat, this one included:

- Customers' or guests' names and phone numbers, or your contact list
- Your students' or followers' names (paste workshop notes without them)
- Aadhaar, PAN or passport details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs, including your Instagram, Facebook or Google login
- Sales figures, money totals or customer lists
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

You don't need to upload your photos either. Describe the photo in words. If you paste something by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].
