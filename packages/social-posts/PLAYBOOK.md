# Posts that bring customers: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/social-posts/ and answer the questions. Only true things.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), and type "week".
3. Read the 3 posts (ask for more if you have time). Change any word that doesn't sound like you. Fill every [blank], like a price or a date.
4. Check each day against its date in your phone's calendar.
5. Save the 3 short replies as quick replies: WhatsApp Business > Business tools > Quick replies, and saved replies on Instagram (menus vary).
6. Send the helper and this week's captions to yourself on WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
7. Set a weekly phone reminder: "Plan posts", Monday morning, 15 minutes.

## If views don't turn into customers (once, about 30 minutes)

With the full version, say: "My reels get views but nobody buys. I have [hours] a week. Build me a system."

1. Pick your three weekly series: one useful tip, one behind the scenes, one "what's on" with how to buy or book. Give each a day.
2. Make one free small thing from your own notes: a checklist, a mini-lesson or a template. Pick one keyword people can comment to get it.
3. Set up a WhatsApp Community, Channel or broadcast list that people choose to join. Save the DM reply and welcome message as quick replies.
4. Start a one-line weekly count in a Google Sheet: posts, saves, shares, keyword comments, DMs, new joiners, sign-ups, sales.

## Every week (15 minutes, Monday)

- Paste the helper into a new chat. Tell it what is happening this week: a new item, festival hours, a workshop. Type "week".
- Fix anything that isn't true or isn't you. Fill the blanks.
- Look at the photo ideas. Plan when you will take them: morning light, before you open.
- Once a month, ask for a 4-week plan for the one thing you most want to sell more of.
- Fill in your weekly count. Compare only with your own last week, and pick one change to try.
- Launching an online workshop? Start two weeks ahead and ask for the launch plan. Real dates, real prices and real seat limits only.

## Every posting day (5 minutes)

- Take the photo. Hands, food, the room, the view, you. No faces without the person's permission. Never children's faces.
- Copy that day's caption from your WhatsApp, paste it into the app, add the photo, post.
- Reply to comments and messages the same day if you can. Start from your saved quick replies.
- When someone comments your keyword, send the free thing and invite them to your list. Add only people who say yes. Anyone who says STOP comes off the list at once.
- Make a tally mark each time someone says "I saw your post." That is your count.

## When a person must handle it

- Paid ads, boosting posts, or someone else managing your accounts: a person who does this work, with money you can afford to lose.
- A health, diet or "organic" claim, or a food label question: a qualified person.
- A collab or a gifted product: say so clearly at the start of the post.
- Automatic replies, WhatsApp bots or auto-DMs: a separate builder project (the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, with each customer's opt-in).
- An angry comment or a bad review: reply calmly. The review replies in the Get found on Google helper can draft one.

## Never type these

Customers' names, phone numbers or your contact list. Students' or followers' names. Aadhaar, PAN or passport details. Bank, UPI or card details. Passwords or OTPs, including your Instagram or Google login. Sales or money figures. Health details. Staff ID numbers or wages. Family details. Photos of people.

Describe photos in words. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and start again with a [blank].
