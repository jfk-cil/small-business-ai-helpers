# Posts that bring customers: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/social-posts/ and answer the questions. Only true things.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), and type "week".
3. Read the 5 posts. Change any word that doesn't sound like you. Fill every [blank], like a price or a date.
4. Check each day against its date in your phone's calendar.
5. Send the helper and this week's captions to yourself on WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
6. Set a weekly phone reminder: "Plan posts", Monday morning, 15 minutes.

## Every week (15 minutes, Monday)

- Paste the helper into a new chat. Tell it what is happening this week: a new item, festival hours, a workshop. Type "week".
- Fix anything that isn't true or isn't you. Fill the blanks.
- Look at the photo ideas. Plan when you will take them: morning light, before you open.
- Once a month, ask for a 4-week plan for the one thing you most want to sell more of.

## Every posting day (5 minutes)

- Take the photo. Hands, food, the room, the view, you. No customers' or children's faces without permission.
- Copy that day's caption from your WhatsApp, paste it into the app, add the photo, post.
- Reply to comments and messages the same day if you can.
- Make a tally mark each time someone says "I saw your post." That is your count.

## When a person must handle it

- Paid ads, boosting posts, or someone else managing your accounts: a person who does this work, with money you can afford to lose.
- A health, diet or "organic" claim, or a food label question: a qualified person.
- A collab or a gifted product: say so clearly at the start of the post.
- An angry comment or a bad review: reply calmly. The review replies in the Get found on Google helper can draft one.

## Never type these

Customers' names, phone numbers or your contact list. Aadhaar, PAN or passport details. Bank, UPI or card details. Passwords or OTPs, including your Instagram or Google login. Sales or money figures. Health details. Staff ID numbers or wages. Family details. Photos of people.

Describe photos in words. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and start again with a [blank].
