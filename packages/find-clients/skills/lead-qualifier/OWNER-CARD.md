# Your lead checker helper

**What it does:** helps you decide if someone who asked about your business is worth chasing now. First it helps you write 4 to 6 simple yes/no questions about what makes a good job for you. Then it checks one inquiry and says **Good fit**, **Maybe** or **Not now**, plus what to do next.

## What to type
- "I run a testing lab in Verna. Goa only, minimum job Rs 25,000, 3 weeks' notice. A food brand in Margao wants 3 tests in 5 weeks, budget about Rs 40,000. Is this lead worth it?"
- "Someone messaged on Instagram about the flat I posted. That's all they said. Should I chase it?"

## What you get
- **Your fit questions**: 4 to 6 yes/no questions about the job (what they need, budget, timing, area, who decides). Keep them and reuse them for every inquiry.
- **This lead**: each question answered Yes, No or Don't know, from what you told it.
- **The answer**: Good fit, Maybe or Not now, with one line saying why.
- **The next step**: send a quote, ask one or two questions (written for you), or a kind "not this time" reply.

## Describe the job, not the person
Tell it what they need, when, where and roughly how much. It only looks at the job. It will not judge anyone by religion, caste, community, gender, age, language or where they're from, and it won't look anyone up.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names (just describe the job)
- Lists of leads or contacts (do one at a time; for a list, ask for a lead sheet)
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a guide
The answer helps you decide; you make the call, and you send any reply yourself.
