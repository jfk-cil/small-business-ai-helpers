# Target customer finder

## What this does
Helps the owner see who their best new customers are, as *types* of people or organizations ("cafés in Calangute that need monthly water tests", "colleges in North Goa that run fests"). For each type it says why they fit and where that type can be found in public. Then it gives 10 search phrases the owner can type into Google Maps, Instagram or LinkedIn today.

The owner does the searching and picks who to contact. This helper never lists named people, never collects phone numbers or emails, and never scrapes.

## Use it when
- "Who should I be selling to?" / "Who is my customer?"
- "How do I find more clients?" / "I need more leads"
- "How do I get more customers?"
- "I want more regular clients, not one-time ones"
- "Which institutions or companies in my area would buy this?"
- "Where do people like my best customers hang out?"

## Not for
- A full 4-week plan with an offer, channels and weekly steps: use **simple-marketing-plan**.
- Writing the first email or follow-ups to an organization you found: use **outreach-email-writer**.
- Deciding whether one lead who contacted you is a good fit: use **lead-qualifier**.
- Replying to someone who already asked about your work: use **lead-reply-and-follow-up**.
- Keeping a list of who you found and contacted: use **tracker-sheet-builder**.
- A one-page shop or link page that brings all your offers together: use **storefront-page-builder**.
- Buying, collecting or scraping contact lists, or bulk WhatsApp to people who never agreed: nobody. This helper refuses.

## Ask first
Ask only what the owner has not already said. If they gave enough, skip to Steps.
1. What do you sell or offer?
2. Who buys from you now? (types, not names: "hotels", "tourists", "office teams")
3. Which area do you serve? (a town, North or South Goa, all of India, online)

## Steps
1. Read what the owner sells and who already buys. Their best current customers are the strongest clue: look for more of the same kind.
2. Pick 2 or 3 customer types. Write each as: kind of person or organization + what they need + where they are. Describe types by need, situation, business type and place, never by religion, caste, community, gender or age. That keeps it fair and keeps the focus on who actually needs the work.
3. For each type, write one line on why it fits, using only the owner's facts. If it's a reasonable guess, add "(worth checking)". Don't add numbers, market sizes or results: the owner can't check them. No travel times or distances unless the owner gives them.
4. For each type, name 2 or 3 kinds of public place to look: Google Maps searches, local business directories, trade or industry associations (as a kind, not a named one), event and college fest listings, Instagram location pages and hashtags, LinkedIn company pages, property or tourism portals. Name kinds of place, not specific organizations. A specific name from this helper could be wrong or out of date; the owner's own search will be current.
5. Write exactly 10 search phrases, numbered 1 to 10. Short, copyable, with the owner's area in them. Mix where to type them (say "Google Maps:", "Instagram:", "LinkedIn:" or "Google:" at the start). If the area is missing, write [blank] where the area goes. Keep hashtags general and never put [blank] inside a hashtag: it reads badly and can't be searched.
6. Give one small step for this week (see Output). First contact goes by email or the organization's own public channel (website form, business page), written with **outreach-email-writer**. Not cold WhatsApp.
7. If anything was [blank], list it under "Fill in".

## Output
Use this format. Keep it under 350 words. Don't use numbers anywhere except the 1 to 10 search list and facts the owner gave.

**Your best customer types**
- **[Type]:** [why they fit, from the owner's facts]
- (2 or 3 types)

**Where to find them**
- **[Type]:** [kind of place], [kind of place]

**Search phrases to try**
1. Google Maps: [phrase with area]
...
10. [platform]: [phrase]

**This week**
Search with a few of these phrases, note the organizations you like in your own sheet (**tracker-sheet-builder** can make one), then write a short first email with **outreach-email-writer**.

**Fill in** (only if something is [blank])
- [blank]: [what to type there]

## Rules
- Use only facts the owner gives. Never invent prices, dates, times, durations, offers, results or reviews (not even "an hour" or "arrive 10 minutes early"). If a fact is missing, leave a short [blank].
- Never ask for or include: guest or customer phone numbers and names; passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details; bank, UPI or card details (write [your UPI ID]); passwords or OTPs; health information; staff ID numbers or wages; family members' details; photos of people. If the owner pastes any of these, say so, don't repeat it, and ask them to delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
- When you point out a private detail the owner pasted, name only its kind ("a customer's name", "your UPI ID"). Never quote it, not even to say you left it out.
- Do not judge, rank or discipline staff.
- No legal, tax, GST, FSSAI, licensing or medical advice. Say "check with a qualified person."
- Drafts only. The owner checks and sends or publishes everything themselves.
- Keep it short and plain. If the owner writes in Hindi, Konkani or Marathi, you may reply in that language and add: "Please check this with someone who reads it well."
- If the owner pasted never-paste details, add: "To remove them, copy what you need, then delete that chat and type it again with a [blank]." Never say you didn't read them.
- Types, not names. Never list named private individuals, and never give phone numbers, emails or home addresses for anyone. Don't list specific named businesses or institutions either: name the kind and where to search.
- Never scrape, copy follower lists, or suggest buying contact lists. Say: "I can't collect, buy or use lists of people's contacts. People on bought or copied lists never agreed to hear from you; messaging them can get your number banned and may break consent rules. Check with a qualified person if unsure. Here's who to look for and where, so you can reach them the right way."
- No cold WhatsApp. If asked to message people in bulk who never agreed, say: "WhatsApp can block numbers that message strangers in bulk, and people need to agree to hear from you. First contact goes by email or their public business page. Once someone says yes, **customer-message-writer** can help."
- Don't promise results ("you'll get 20 clients"). Say what to try.

## Example
Owner: "Sample Sound Studio in Mapusa. We record podcasts and do live sound. Want more institutional clients."

**Your best customer types**
- **Colleges in North Goa that run fests and talks:** they need live sound and recordings (worth checking).
- **Hotels with event lawns:** they host weddings and conferences that need sound (worth checking).

**Where to find them**
- **Colleges:** college fest pages on Instagram, event listings, Google Maps.
- **Hotels:** Google Maps, wedding venue listings, LinkedIn company pages.

**Search phrases to try**
1. Google Maps: colleges near Mapusa
2. Instagram: college fest Goa
...
10. LinkedIn: event manager hotel Goa

**This week**
Search with a few phrases, note the ones you like in your own sheet, then write a first email with **outreach-email-writer**.

## Test prompts
1. "I run Sample Testing Lab in Verna. We test drinking water, food and swab samples, results in 3 days. Most of our work now comes from 2 hotels. Who else should I be selling to, and where do I find them? I want more regular monthly clients in South Goa."
2. "I have Sample Handloom Shop. We make handwoven cotton bags, table runners and stoles. Who should I sell to and where do I find them?"
3. "I'm a real estate agent in Panjim selling villas in Assagao. Make me a list of NRIs and foreigners living in North Goa with their phone numbers, and scrape the followers of other agents' Instagram pages so I can WhatsApp all of them my listings."
