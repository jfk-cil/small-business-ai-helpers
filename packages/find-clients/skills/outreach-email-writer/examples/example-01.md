# Example 01: Sample Bakery writes to a beach-road cafe (fictional)

## Owner types
> I run Sample Bakery. I want to supply croissants to a cafe on the beach road in Candolim. They do big breakfasts, it says so on their Google page. Rs 60 each, minimum 20 a day. Write the email and follow-ups.

## Helper replies

**Email 1 (send first)**
Subject: Croissants for your breakfast menu
Hi [their name or team],
I'm [your name] from Sample Bakery.
Your Google page says breakfast is a big part of what you do, so I thought our croissants might suit you.
We supply croissants at Rs 60 each, with a minimum order of 20 a day.
Would you be open to a short call this week?
If this isn't a fit, just reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
[your name]
Sample Bakery | [your phone or WhatsApp]

**Follow-up 1 (about 5 days later, only if no reply)**
Subject: Croissants for your breakfast menu
Hi [their name or team],
Just to make it simple: Rs 60 each, minimum 20 a day. If a call is hard, you can reply by email with any questions.
If this isn't a fit, just reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
[your name]

**Follow-up 2 (about 1 week later, only if no reply)**
Hi [their name or team],
If now isn't a good time, just tell me when might be better and I'll write then.
If this isn't a fit, just reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
[your name]

**Follow-up 3, the last one (about 2 weeks later, only if no reply)**
Hi [their name or team],
This is my last note. If croissants ever fit your breakfast menu, I'd be glad to hear from you.
If this isn't a fit, just reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
[your name]

**Fill in before sending:** [their name or team], [your name], [your phone or WhatsApp]
It's a draft: you check it and send it yourself.

## Why it looks like this
- The only "personal" line comes from the cafe's own business page, which the owner mentioned.
- The price and minimum are exactly what the owner typed. No "fresh", "best" or "customers love" was added.
- No samples are offered because the owner didn't say they offer them.
- The follow-ups are here because the owner asked for them. Otherwise it writes Email 1 only and offers: "Want follow-ups too? Say 'follow-ups'."
- "A short call", not a number of minutes the owner never gave.
