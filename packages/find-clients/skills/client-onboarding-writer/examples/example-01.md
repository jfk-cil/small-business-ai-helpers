# Example 01: yoga school, new corporate client (fictional)

**Owner:** "Sample Yoga School in Assagao. An office in Panjim said yes to weekly yoga for their team, Wednesdays 7:30 am at their office, starting 21 October. Before that I want a 15-minute call with their HR person and a list of anyone with injuries, and I need to know how much floor space they have. Advance of one month's fee before the first class."

**Welcome message** (copy and paste)
```
Hi [their name],
Thank you for choosing us for weekly team yoga!
Classes are on Wednesdays at 7:30 am at your office, starting 21 October.
Reply here with any questions.
[your name], Sample Yoga School
```

**What happens next**
1. A 15-minute call with your HR team: [blank].
2. One month's fee paid in advance to [your UPI ID]: before the first class.
3. First class: Wednesday 21 October, 7:30 am.
4. Weekly classes: every Wednesday, 7:30 am.

**What we need from you**
- How much floor space the room has.
- One contact person (their role) for the call.
- A note to your team: anyone with an injury can tell the teacher privately before class.

(21 October 2026 is a Wednesday, so the day and date match. Health details are private. The helper does not collect a list of injuries; people tell the teacher themselves.)

**Your first-week checklist**
- [ ] Send the welcome message.
- [ ] Book the 15-minute call and note the date here: [blank].
- [ ] Save the agreed fee and dates where you can find them.
- [ ] Check the advance has come in before 21 October.
- [ ] Put every Wednesday 7:30 am in your calendar.

**Fill in:** [their name] is your contact. [your name] is your name. [your UPI ID] is your UPI ID. The [blank]s are the call date.

**Before you send:** check every date and amount against your quote. You send it yourself.
