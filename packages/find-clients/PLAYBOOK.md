# Find new clients: your playbook

A few good businesses each week, found by you, contacted one at a time.

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. Open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/find-clients/ and answer the 4 questions. Describe your 3 best customers as types ("a new café", "a 20-room resort"), not names. Tap **Make my helper**, then **Copy helper**. Send it to yourself in WhatsApp ("Message yourself").
2. Paste it into a new AI chat and type "go". Work through steps 1 and 2: customer types and search phrases. Save them in "Message yourself".
3. **Your lead sheet** (Google Sheets app), columns: Business, Type, Where I found it, One true thing about them, Last message date, Step, Next date, Status, Notes. This sheet stays yours. Don't paste it into the AI.
4. Ask the helper for step 4: a first message for each type and one follow-up. Save them in a note on your phone, or as Gmail templates on a computer (Settings > Advanced > Templates).
5. Write down what a good client looks like for you: smallest job, areas you cover, how much notice you need.

## Every day (10 minutes)

1. Try 2 or 3 of your search phrases on Google Maps, Instagram or LinkedIn.
2. Add up to 3 businesses to your sheet. Note one true thing about each (what they sell, something on their own page).
3. Send 1 or 2 first messages: by email, their contact form, their business page, or in person. Add the one true thing. Never to personal numbers.
4. Send any follow-ups that are due (check the Next date column). Stop after a "no thanks".
5. Someone replied? Answer them yourself. Mark it in your sheet.

## Every week (15 minutes)

1. Count in your sheet: messages sent, replies, meetings. Which customer type replies most?
2. Keep the types that reply. Drop the ones that don't. Ask the helper for new phrases.
3. Ask one happy client if they know someone who needs you.
4. Visit or message one local business that could refer customers to you.
5. Someone said yes? Ask the AI for a welcome message and your first-week checklist.

## When a person must handle it

- Every reply from a business: you answer it
- Price talks and discounts
- Contracts, terms, billing details and tax: a qualified person
- "No thanks" or "please stop": stop, and mark it in your sheet
- Deciding who to work with: you decide

## Never type these

- Names, phone numbers and emails of clients or leads, and contact or follower lists
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details, and staff ID numbers
- Bank, UPI and card details, passwords and OTPs
- Health information, wages, family members' details, photos of people
- Sales or money figures (your public prices are fine)

Pasted one by mistake? Delete that chat and type it again with a [blank].
