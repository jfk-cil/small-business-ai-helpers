# Kit templates: staff-hiring-and-onboarding

Skeletons to fill with the owner's facts. Anything the owner did not give stays a [blank]. Never add pay, benefits, incentives, training, registrations or legal requirements the owner did not state. Cut lines that don't apply.

## B. Recruitment kit

### Job post (1,200 characters or fewer)
```
[Role] needed: [area]
[your business name] is looking for a [role] to [what the work is, in the owner's words].

Where: [area or areas]
Hours or shifts: [blank]
Pay: [blank]
You'll need: [requirements the owner gave, one per line]
To start: please bring the originals of [documents the owner checks] to show us in person.

To apply: [how to apply: WhatsApp [your business WhatsApp number] / [app or form link]]
We reply within [blank].
[your business name]
```
- Title: the role and the area, nothing else. No "urgent" unless the owner says so.
- "What the work is": the tasks, not the patient or client. For health roles, no medical claims (see healthcare-notes.md).
- "You'll need": only the owner's requirements. Leave out anything on the list in fair-hiring.md.

### WhatsApp version (500 characters or fewer)
```
[Role] needed in [area]
Work: [one line]
Shifts: [blank] · Pay: [blank]
Need: [main requirement]
Apply: [app or form link] or reply here.
[your business name]
```

### Outreach messages (80 words or fewer each)
One per kind of source. The owner sends each one to a place they choose. Never write that a place has agreed, has members waiting, or will share the post.

Training colleges and institutes:
```
Hello! I run [your business name] in [area]. We're looking for [role] for [shifts: blank]. Could you share this with your recent students or alumni, if that's okay with you? The job post is below. Thank you!
[your name], [your business name]
```

Professional associations or groups:
```
Hello! [your business name] in [area] is looking for [role]. If it fits your group's rules, could you share the post below with members? Happy to answer any questions.
[your name], [your business name]
```

Community and local WhatsApp groups (only where the group allows job posts):
```
Hi all! We're looking for [role] in [area]. Details below. Please share with anyone who might be interested. Thank you!
[your business name]
```

### Places to try (plain list, no box)
- Job sites (search 'jobs in [area]'; check each site's terms and costs).
- Training colleges, institutes and professional associations near you.
- Community and local WhatsApp groups (only where the group allows job posts).
- People who already work with you (ask if they know someone; no referral bonus unless the owner gives one).
Say nothing about what any site costs, how many people it reaches, or how fast it works.

### Applicant quick replies (500 characters or fewer each)
Pick 3 to 6. Shortcut ideas: `pay`, `shifts`, `area`, `what-to-bring`, `how-to-register`, `next-steps`.
```
Hi! Thanks for asking.
Pay for [role] is [blank].
Shifts: [blank].
Any other questions, just reply here.
[your business name]
```
```
Hi! Here's how to register:
1. Open [app or form link]
2. Fill in your details (it takes [blank])
3. We'll message you about a time to meet in person.
Please don't send photos of documents on WhatsApp. Bring the originals when we meet.
[your business name]
```

### Finish your registration (500 characters or fewer)
```
Hi! You started registering with [your business name] but haven't finished yet.
Finish here: [app or form link]
Stuck on a step? Reply here and we'll help.
If you're no longer interested, no problem, just tell us.
[your business name]
```
Send it once. If there's still no reply, leave it.

## C. Onboarding

### Onboarding checklist (one box)
```
Onboarding: [role], starting [day] [date]

Day 0 (before they start)
[ ] Send the welcome message
[ ] Confirm first shift: [date], [time], [place]
[ ] Tell them who to call: [role, e.g. duty coordinator]
[ ] Share what to read: [house rules / service guide]

Day 1
[ ] Meet in person, show them around
[ ] Document check (below)
[ ] Shadow [role] on [first visit or shift]
[ ] Answer questions, share the contact for help

Week 1
[ ] First shifts: [dates]
[ ] Check in: "How is it going? What would help?"
[ ] Fix anything they asked for

Document check (in person)
[ ] ID: see the original, hand it back
[ ] Qualifications: see the original certificates
[ ] Registration with the relevant council or body (if the role has one)
[ ] Police verification (if you require it)
[ ] [other checks you named]
```
Then, outside the box, always:
- "Check with a qualified person which registrations and checks your service needs."
- "Never paste documents, ID or registration numbers into any AI chat, this one included."
- Bank details for pay and emergency contacts are collected on paper or in your own system, never in this chat.

### Welcome messages (500 characters or fewer each)
```
Hi [their name]! Welcome to [your business name].
Your first shift is on [day] [date] at [time], at [place].
Please bring the originals of [documents] to show us in person.
Questions? Call [role] on [your business WhatsApp number].
[your business name]
```
```
Good morning [their name]! Today is your first day with us.
Please come to [place] at [time]. [role] will meet you.
See you soon!
[your business name]
```
```
Hi [their name], thanks for your first week with us.
How is it going? Is there anything that would help?
Just reply here, or tell [role] when you see them.
[your business name]
```
The Week 1 message never rates, scores or warns.

## A. Manual setup when the owner wants it "automatic"
An automatic WhatsApp flow needs the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, people's opt-in and approved message templates. That's a builder project, not this helper. What the owner can do now, by hand, in a few minutes a day:
- **WhatsApp Business labels**, one per stage: New, Replied, Registering, Docs check, Ready. Tap and hold a chat > Label.
- **Quick replies** for the first reply, FAQ answers and the finish-your-registration reminder (type / and the shortcut).
- **Greeting message** (sent to people who message for the first time) and **away message** (outside your hours), both in Business tools.
- **Google Calendar reminder to yourself** each day, for example "Check the Registering label." The owner picks the time.
- **A tracker sheet** using codes (Applicant 1, Applicant 2), never names or numbers: use **tracker-sheet-builder**.
Menu names can differ a little by phone.
