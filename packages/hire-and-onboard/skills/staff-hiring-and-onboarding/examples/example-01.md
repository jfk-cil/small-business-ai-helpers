# Example 01: a recruitment kit for home-visit nurses (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Home Care, Porvorim. We need nurses (GNM or B.Sc Nursing) for home visits to elders in Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim. 12-hour day or night shifts. Pay not decided yet. Must have own two-wheeler. Apply on our app or WhatsApp. People keep asking about pay, area and what documents to bring, and many start the app but don't finish.

**Helper replies:**

**Job post**
```
Nurses needed: Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim
[your business name] is looking for nurses for home visits to elders and people who are homebound.

Where: Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim
Hours or shifts: 12-hour day or night shifts
Pay: [blank]
You'll need:
- GNM or B.Sc Nursing
- Your own two-wheeler
To start: please bring the originals of your certificates and ID to show us in person.

To apply: register on our app [app or form link] or WhatsApp [your business WhatsApp number]
We reply within [blank].
[your business name]
```
(520 characters)

**WhatsApp version**
```
Nurses needed: Porvorim, Mapusa, Panjim
Work: home visits to elders and people who are homebound
Shifts: 12-hour day or night · Pay: [blank]
Need: GNM or B.Sc Nursing, own two-wheeler
Apply: [app or form link] or reply here.
[your business name]
```
(245 characters)

**Outreach: nursing colleges and institutes**
```
Hello! I run [your business name] in Porvorim. We're looking for nurses (GNM or B.Sc Nursing) for home visits in Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim. Could you share this with your recent students or alumni, if that's okay with you? The job post is below. Thank you!
[your name], [your business name]
```
(51 words)

**Outreach: community and local WhatsApp groups**
```
Hi all! We're looking for nurses for home visits to elders in Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim. Details below. Please share with anyone who might be interested. Thank you!
[your business name]
```
(31 words)

**Places to try**
- Job sites (search 'jobs in Porvorim'; check each site's terms and costs).
- Nursing colleges and institutes near you, and nurses' associations or groups.
- Community and local WhatsApp groups (only where the group allows job posts).
- Nurses who already work with you: ask if they know someone.

**Applicant quick replies**

**Shortcut:** `pay`
```
Hi! Thanks for asking.
Pay for nurses is [blank].
Shifts are 12 hours, day or night.
Any other questions, just reply here.
[your business name]
```
(143 characters)

**Shortcut:** `area`
```
Hi! Visits are in Porvorim, Mapusa and Panjim.
You'll need your own two-wheeler to get to them.
[your business name]
```
(116 characters)

**Shortcut:** `what-to-bring`
```
Hi! When we meet, please bring the originals of your nursing certificate and ID to show us in person. We'll hand them straight back.
Please don't send photos of documents on WhatsApp.
[your business name]
```
(204 characters)

**Finish your registration**
```
Hi! You started registering with [your business name] but haven't finished yet.
Finish here: [app or form link]
Stuck on a step? Reply here and we'll help.
If you're no longer interested, no problem, just tell us.
[your business name]
```
(234 characters)
Send it once. If there's still no reply, leave it.

Check with a qualified person which registrations and checks your service needs before you add any to the post. Patients' details never go into any AI chat.

**Fill in before sending:** pay, how soon you reply, your app link, your business WhatsApp number, your name and business name.
It's a draft: you check it and send or post it yourself. A person decides who to hire.
