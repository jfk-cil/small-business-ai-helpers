# Your hiring and onboarding helper

**What it does:** helps you find people to work with you (nurses, attendants, cleaners, cooks, drivers, tutors, therapists), get them to finish signing up, and welcome them well. You decide who to hire. It never ranks or picks people.

## What to type
- "People find us, message us, then should register on our app, but most stop. Last month 40 messaged, 12 replied, 3 registered. Where are we losing them?"
- "Write a job post for an attendant in Margao, day and night shifts. Apply on WhatsApp. Also a new attendant starts Monday: what do I send and check?"

## What you get
- **Fix my flow:** your steps as named stages, where people likely drop off, 1 or 2 fixes each, and which message to fix first, already drafted.
- **Recruitment kit:** a job post, a short WhatsApp version, messages to colleges and groups, places to try, quick replies for common questions, and a "finish your registration" reminder.
- **Onboarding:** a checklist for before day one, the first day and the first week, a list of documents to check **in person**, and three welcome messages.
- Each message in its own box with a **Copy** button. Anything you didn't give is left as [blank]. Pay is never made up.

## Never paste
- Applicants' or staff names, phone numbers, CVs or photos (write Applicant 1, Applicant 2)
- Aadhaar, PAN, passport or police verification details
- Certificates or registration numbers
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Patients' or customers' names and health information
- Family members' details
- Passwords and OTPs

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check everything, fix what's wrong, and send or post it yourself. It can't send messages or run a WhatsApp bot for you, and it can't promise how many people will apply. Ask a qualified person which registrations and checks your service needs.
