# Hire and welcome staff: your one-page playbook

## Set up once (15 minutes)

1. On your phone, open https://jfk-cil.github.io/small-business-ai-helpers/hire-and-onboard/ and answer the questions. Counts only, never names.
2. Copy the helper, paste it into Claude (or the AI you use), and type "go".
3. Read your hiring steps and where people drop off. Fix the "fix first" message today.
4. Name one role. Fill in pay, hours and area where it says [blank]. Post the job and the WhatsApp version.
5. In WhatsApp Business, save the applicant replies as quick replies (`/pay`, `/shifts`, `/what-to-bring`, `/finish`). Add labels: New, Replied, Registering, Docs check, Ready.
6. Turn on a greeting message and an away message in Business tools.
7. Send the helper to yourself on WhatsApp so you can copy it again any day.

## Every day while hiring (10 minutes)

- Reply to every new message with your first-reply quick reply. Label the chat **New** or **Replied**.
- Open the **Registering** label. Send `/finish` once to anyone who stopped halfway. Once is enough.
- Book an in-person document check for anyone ready: "Please bring the originals to show us on [date]."
- Once a week, count each label. Compare the counts and fix the step where most people stop.

## When someone new starts

- **Day 0:** send the first welcome message. Tell them who to call (a role) and what to bring.
- **Day 1:** check original documents in person. Hand them back. Pair them with a senior person.
- **End of Week 1:** send "How is it going? What would help?" Listen. Fix one thing.

## When a person must handle it

- Deciding who to hire, or who is not right for now: you.
- Which registrations, licenses or police checks a role needs, and contracts: a qualified person.
- Clinical steps (injections, samples, moving a patient): a qualified person on your team.
- A fully automatic WhatsApp flow: a builder project with an approved provider, people's opt-in and approved templates.

## Never type these

Applicants' or staff names, numbers, CVs or photos. Documents, ID, certificates or police verification. Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers. Registration numbers. Patients' names or details. Wages. Bank, UPI or card details. Family details. Passwords or OTPs. Sales or money figures.

Write "Applicant 1", not a name. Pasted something by mistake? Delete that chat and start again with a [blank].
