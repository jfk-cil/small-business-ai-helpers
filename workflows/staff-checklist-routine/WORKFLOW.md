# Checklists your staff actually use

## Who it's for
Homestays, cafés, cleaners, studios, bakeries and shops where "they keep forgetting the same things." Needs at least one staff member or helper.

## Start from your job card (notes from an earlier session)
- Card table "A checklist"? Start here, unless the job is quotes, deposits or payments (see Get paid on time).
- Your "first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## Today at the table (about 10 minutes)
1. Pick one job that keeps going wrong: opening, closing, room turnover, or kitchen prep.
2. Tap your keyboard's microphone and say how you do it, start to end. Messy is fine. No staff names.
3. Ask the AI to turn it into a short checklist (8 to 12 lines) in your staff's language. Have staff read it before pinning. AI can get Konkani wrong.

## At home this week
- Pin it in the staff WhatsApp group for 30 days; re-pin monthly. Can't pin? Ask the group admin.
- Optional: a paper copy for the wall.

## Every day
- Staff do each line from the pinned text and send one photo of the finished space. No people, no guest belongings, papers or screens.
- You glance for 2 minutes. Missed lines wait for the weekly huddle.
- Never name anyone in the group. Never use photos to track who did the job or when.

## Every week (10-minute huddle)
Ask: "Which line is hard or gets missed?" Change one line. No blame.

## Ask the AI (any free AI chat)
- "Turn this into a short checklist for my [room cleaning], in [language], one action per line: [paste what you said, no names]."
- "Line [number] keeps getting missed. Rewrite only that line more clearly and show the full list."

## Never put into AI
Staff names with comments, wages, ID, phone numbers, health details, photos of people, customer details.

## It's working when
- The same thing stops being missed.
- Staff ask to change a line.
