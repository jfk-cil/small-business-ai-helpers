# Get paid on time

**Who it's for:** Homestays, cake orders, cleaners, studios: anyone paid by deposit or after the job. Needs WhatsApp Business for the label; without it, use the paper list only.

## Start from your job card (notes from an earlier session)
- Card table "A checklist", and the job is quotes, deposits or payments? Start here.
- Your "first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## Today at the table (about 10 minutes)
1. In WhatsApp Business, make a label: **Payment due**.
2. Save 2 reminder texts as quick replies: **/pay1** (friendly) and **/pay2** (final, polite). Use blanks: [amount] [job] [due date] [your UPI ID]. You fill them in yourself before sending.
3. Add one deposit line to your booking confirmation.

## At home this week
- Start one private paper list (or a Google Sheet shared with no one, never "anyone with the link"): **Date · Job (the work, never the customer's name) · Due date · Paid?**
- Set one weekly phone alarm.

## Every day
- **Job booked:** send the confirmation with the deposit line, add the Payment due label, write it on the list.
- **Money arrives:** tick Paid, remove the label.

## Every week (10 minutes, same day)
Open the Payment due label. Past due: send /pay1. Still unpaid a week later: /pay2. Then call or talk in person. No late fees, no threats.

## Ask the AI
- "Write a friendly WhatsApp reminder in [language] that a payment is due. Use blanks: [amount], [job], [due date], [your UPI ID]. No threats or fees."
- "Now write one more friendly reminder, polite and final, same blanks."

**Never in AI:** amounts tied to a person, customer names, UPI IDs, bank details, GST numbers, invoices, payment screenshots.

## It's working when
- Every Payment due chat has a date on your list.
- No deposit is more than 7 days past due on your weekly check.

For GST, tax or legal questions, ask your accountant.
