# Customer messages
For anyone whose WhatsApp fills with questions.

Needs free WhatsApp Business (green B). Without it, use the paper version at the bottom.

## Start from your job card (notes from an earlier session)
- Card table "First contact" or "Replies"? Start here.
- Your "first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## Today at the table (10 minutes)
1. Type your real answers to your 3 most-asked questions into Quick replies (Settings > Business tools). Shortcuts: /price /book /where. AI only shortens or translates (Hindi, Konkani).
2. Turn on Away message: when you reply.
3. Make 5 labels: New, Replied, Follow up, Booked, Not now.

## At home this week
Set a daily alarm. Save this card in "Message yourself".

## Every day (10 minutes)
- Label new chats New. Send quick replies, change to Replied. Booked? Label Booked.
- No answer in 2 days? Label Follow up. One short message per chat, never a broadcast. Max 2 (day 2, day 7), then Not now.
- Said no or stop? Not now, stop messaging.
- Bedtime: reply to anything New, or say "tomorrow".

## Every week (10 minutes)
- New quick reply for any question asked 3+ times.
- Write Booked count on paper.
- Verified Google profile? Reply to reviews, never mentioning dates, room, order, payment or refunds.

## Ask the AI
In any free AI chat:
- "Customer's question (no name or number): [one message]. Short, friendly reply in [language]. My answer: [facts]."
- "Short, friendly follow-up in [language]: they asked about [what] 2 days ago."
- "Google review (no name): [paste]. Short, warm public reply."

## Never in AI
Customer names or numbers, ID, bank or UPI details, passwords or OTPs, health details, staff wages, photos of people. Copy one message at a time.

## It's working when
No Follow up chat older than 3 days. Nothing New at bedtime except promised ones.

## No WhatsApp Business?
Keep your 3 answers in "Message yourself".
Paper list of who to follow up (job, not name).
