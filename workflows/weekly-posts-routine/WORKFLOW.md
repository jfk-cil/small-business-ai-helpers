# The weekly posts routine

## Who it's for
Any small business that wants to be found: homestay, café, bakery, shop, studio.

## Start from your job card (notes from an earlier session)
- Card table "Posts"? Start here.
- Your "first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## The problem
You post when you remember, so people forget you.

## Today at the table (about 10 minutes)
1. Make a phone album called "Photos for posts".
2. Take one photo of your work, your place or your product now. No people, no children.
3. Ask the AI for one caption and post it now, with one clear way to message or book.

## At home this week

### Every day (1 minute)
Put one photo into "Photos for posts".

### Every week (20 minutes, on a quiet day you choose; set a phone reminder)
1. Pick 3 photos from the album.
2. Ask the AI for 3 captions. Fix anything untrue.
3. Post one now. Save the other two as Instagram drafts and post them later in the week.
4. Already use Meta Business Suite? You can schedule them there instead. It needs an Instagram Business or Creator account and usually a Facebook Page.

### Every month
Open your Google Business Profile (it must be verified to post, add photos or reply to reviews). Check hours, phone and the website or booking link. Add 2 new photos.

## Ask the AI
Type this into any free AI chat:

"Write a short Instagram caption in [language] for a photo of [describe it]. End with: message us on WhatsApp to book. Don't invent offers or prices."

For the weekly sitting, ask for 3 captions and describe all 3 photos.

## Never put into AI
Photos of people or children, customer names or numbers, reviews with names, prices or offers you haven't decided.

You post everything yourself. AI only drafts.

## It's working when
- You post 3 times a week for 3 weeks in a row.
- Your Google profile hours are correct.
