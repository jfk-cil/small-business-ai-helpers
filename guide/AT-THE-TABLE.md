# At the table

For a builder or volunteer sitting with one owner. Each package's builder notes have the full script.

## Rules

- **Owner's phone, owner's account.** Everything happens on the owner's own phone and AI account, never yours.
- **No logins.** Never log in for the owner, and never see or type their passwords or OTPs.
- **No photos, screenshots or recordings,** even with a yes. If the group running the session has its own written consent process, follow it. Notes on paper only, with no names or numbers, and only after you ask out loud.
- **Customer data pasted?** Stop. Delete that chat together and start again with "Customer 1" or a [blank].
- **Something goes wrong?** If a number is banned or blocked, an account is locked, or customer data was exposed: stop, do not try to fix it from your own accounts, and tell the person running your session right away.
- **Drafts only.** The owner checks and sends everything. Never promise results.

## Pick one helper

At a public-face table: Nothing posted lately: Posts. No one place to buy or book: One page. Not on Maps: Google. Not sure: Start here.

## The flow (20 minutes)

1. **0 to 5:** say what it does in two lines. Demo on your phone with the builder notes' made-up example.
2. **5 to 9:** the owner opens the phone page and answers the questions. The mic and language picker help. No names or numbers.
3. **9 to 13:** **Make my helper**, **Copy helper**, paste into the owner's AI, and run it once on a real item with names and numbers removed. Read the result together and fill each [blank].
4. **13 to 19:** save and set up what the builder notes say (quick replies, labels, a reminder).
5. **19 to 20:** end clean (below).

## End clean

- Save the helper in "Message yourself".
- Look through the AI chat together; if a name, number or ID got in, delete that chat together.
- Agree one next step with a day.
