# Your follow-up helper

**What it does:** writes your follow-up messages (up to 3 per person), tells you who to follow up today, and turns your notes into a short daily report. You send every message yourself. It is not a bot.

## What to type
- "Make follow-ups for past donors about our school kit drive. WhatsApp. Wait 3 days between messages. No messages after 8 pm."
- "Today is October 19. Wait 3 days. S14 | last message Oct 12 | step 1 | no reply. S16 | last message Oct 16 | step 2 | no reply. S9 | asked to stop. Who do I follow up today?"

At the end of the day: "Sent 12, S3 said yes, S7 call back Friday, S9 asked to stop. Report."

## What you get
- Each message in its own box with a **Copy** button, ending with [your name or group name].
- Stop rules: stop when they reply, say no or ask to stop. Never more than 3 messages.
- Today's list: who gets which message, who to stop, and the next dates.
- A report with counts, what to change in your sheet, and tomorrow's first 3 actions.

## Use codes, not names
Give each person a code in your own sheet (S1, S2, M1...). Type only the codes here.

## Never paste
- Names and phone numbers of supporters, members or customers
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every message and send it yourself. It will not add numbers, deadlines or tax benefits you didn't give it.
