# Routine card: customer messages

The long owner version of the routine, for printing or for a builder to walk through with the owner. The short card in SKILL.md is the one the owner saves in "Message yourself".

Needs the free WhatsApp Business app (green B icon). Without it, use the paper version at the bottom.

## Start from your planning card
- Card table "First contact" or "Replies"? Start here.
- Your "My first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "Keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## Today (10 minutes)
1. Type your real answers to your 3 most-asked questions into Quick replies (Business tools > Quick replies). Shortcuts: /price /book /where. The AI only shortens or translates (Hindi, Marathi, Konkani).
2. Turn on the Away message: when you reply.
3. Make 5 labels: New, Replied, Follow up, Booked, Not now.

## At home this week
- Set one daily alarm for your 10-minute pass.
- Save your routine card and your "my helper" prompt in "Message yourself", and pin that chat.

## Every day (10 minutes)
- Label new chats **New**. Send a quick reply, change the label to **Replied**. Booked? Label **Booked**.
- No answer in 2 days? Label **Follow up** and send /follow-up. One short message per chat, never a broadcast. At most 2 follow-ups (day 2, day 7), then **Not now**.
- Follow up only people who messaged you first.
- Said no or stop? Label **Not now** and stop messaging.
- Bedtime: reply to anything still **New**, or say "Thanks! I'll reply tomorrow."

## Every week (10 minutes)
- Make a new quick reply for any question asked 3 or more times.
- Fix any reply that is out of date (price, timings, areas).
- Write the **Booked** count on paper. Counts only, no money.
- Online reviews: use the **review-replies** helper.

## Ask the AI (any free AI chat)
Copy one customer message at a time, with the name and number taken out.
- "Customer's question (no name or number): [one message]. Short, friendly reply in [language]. My answer: [facts]."
- "Short, friendly follow-up in [language]: they asked about [what] 2 days ago."
- "Make this reply shorter: [your reply]."

## Never in AI
Customer names or numbers, ID, bank or UPI details, passwords or OTPs, health details, staff wages, photos of people.

## It's working when
- No **Follow up** chat is older than 3 days.
- Nothing is still **New** at bedtime, except ones you promised for tomorrow.

## No WhatsApp Business? The paper version
Normal WhatsApp has no quick replies, labels or away message. Instead:
1. Paste your 3 answers into the "Message yourself" chat and pin it. Copy from there when someone asks.
2. Keep a paper list, one line per inquiry, with these columns (what they asked, never a name):

```
Date | What they asked | Follow up on | Done?
```

3. Save this card in "Message yourself":

```
My message routine (paper)
Daily, 10 min: answer new chats from my saved answers. New inquiry? One line on my paper list.
No answer in 2 days: one short follow-up. Once more on day 7, then cross it off.
Only people who messaged me first. One chat at a time, never a broadcast.
Said no or stop: cross it off and stop.
Bedtime: answer every new chat, or say "I'll reply tomorrow."
Weekly, 10 min: save a new answer for any question asked 3+ times.
Never in AI: names, numbers, ID, bank or UPI details, OTPs, health, photos of people.
Working when: nothing on my list waits more than 3 days.
```

When you're ready, the free WhatsApp Business app adds quick replies, labels and an away message.
