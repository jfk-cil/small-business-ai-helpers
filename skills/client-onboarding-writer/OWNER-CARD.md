# Your new client welcome helper

**What it does:** When a new client says yes, it writes the welcome message, what happens next, what you need from them, and your own to-do list for the first week.

**What to type** (two examples)
- "A villa owner said yes to a monthly deep clean. First clean Saturday 17 October at 9. I visit Thursday at 5 pm first. I need gate info and which rooms to skip."
- "A hotel said yes to a brand video. Write the welcome and next steps."

**What you get**
- A welcome message to copy into WhatsApp or email (it starts "Hi [their name]")
- What happens next, 3 to 5 steps with your dates
- A short list of what you need from the client (only what the work needs)
- A first-week checklist for you, with tick boxes
- Gaps shown as [blank] so you can fill them in
- A note if a day and a date don't match, so you can fix it before you send

**Never paste these into the chat**
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

**It won't:** ask your client to send ID numbers or bank details over chat, or write contract terms. For billing paperwork and contracts, check with a qualified person.

**It's a draft: you check every date and amount, and you send it.**
