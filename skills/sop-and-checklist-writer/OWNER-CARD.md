# Your step-by-step and checklist helper

**What it does:** writes down how a job is done at your place, in short numbered steps a new staff member can follow, plus a checklist to print, a welcome guide for guests, or a short form for new guests or clients.

## What to type
- "Write the steps for opening my cafe: unlock at 7, coffee machine on for 20 minutes, wipe tables, check milk, menu board out, doors open 7:30. Plus a checklist to print."
- "Make a welcome guide for my homestay in Mandrem: wifi, check-out at 11, breakfast 8 to 10, no loud music after 10 pm."

## What you get
- **Steps:** numbered, one action each, in your order, with your times.
- **Checklist:** the same steps with [ ] boxes, a Date line and an Initials line, ready to print and stick on the wall.
- **Welcome guide:** wifi, times, house rules, getting around, how to reach you. The wifi password is a gap you write in by hand.
- **Form:** 8 questions or fewer, asking only what you need for the stay or visit.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords (including wifi) and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].
Keep filled-in forms on paper, not in a chat.

## It won't
- Put passport, Aadhaar, visa, date of birth or card number boxes on a form. Form C for foreign guests stays with the official government process.
- Score, rank or blame staff. Checklists track jobs, not people.
- Put health questions on a form. For classes, it adds one line asking students to tell the teacher in person.
- Tell you food safety, fire or licensing rules. Check with a qualified person.

## It's a draft
You check it, fix anything that's wrong, and print or share it yourself.
