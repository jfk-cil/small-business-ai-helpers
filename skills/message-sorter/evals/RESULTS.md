## Assumptions to check with a real owner
1. Owners can copy 10 to 15 customer messages at a time and change names and numbers to Customer 1, Customer 2 before pasting (WhatsApp copies the sender with each message). Check how long this takes on a phone versus WhatsApp Web.
2. The "Needs you" triggers match what owners call "very important, non-generic, uncommon questions". Check with a real estate agent and a cleaning service which routine-looking messages they still want to see.
3. A 25-word holding line is welcome, not annoying, for customers who wait.
4. The three labels ("Needs me", "Routine", "Waiting for reply") are enough, and owners will use them.
5. Owners with free or short-trial accounts can keep "My tone" and "My business facts" in a phone note and paste them each time.
