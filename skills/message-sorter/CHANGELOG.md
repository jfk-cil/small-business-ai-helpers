# Changelog

## v1.0.3 (October 20, 2026)
- "Not for" and the description point to customer-messages-routine for setting up the daily WhatsApp routine (quick replies, an away message, labels, a daily pass, a follow-up rule).

## v1.0.2 (October 20, 2026)
- New references/booking-apps.md for messages from a booking or delivery app: draft for the app's own chat, follow its messaging rules (no phone numbers, outside links or payment outside the app unless allowed), quick replies for pickup time, meeting point and what to bring, and the owner's own activity list only where the app allows it and the customer asked.
- A message asking to pay outside the app or move to another chat goes in Needs you. SKILL.md links the page from Steps and the sorting rules.

## v1.0.1 (October 19, 2026)
- Rules tightened after the first live run.

## v1.0.0 (October 19, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Three jobs: sort a batch of messages (Needs you first, routine drafts, quick replies, one-line count), learn the owner's tone (8 lines or fewer), set up the owner's business facts.
- Domain notes for real estate (no promises on availability, price, possession, loans or title; "RERA reg. no.: [blank]"; site visits need the owner) and cleaning and home services (rate card only, "[check]", arrival windows only from the owner).
- WhatsApp Business setup notes: labels "Needs me", "Routine", "Waiting for reply", greeting and away messages, quick replies, and an honest note on what can't be automated.
- Pre-registered 6 evals and a 16-case test bank (MSR).
