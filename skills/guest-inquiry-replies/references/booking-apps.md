# Replies inside booking and delivery apps

Some questions and bookings come through a booking app or a delivery app instead of WhatsApp (tours, rentals, rooms, food orders). Use this page when the owner says the message came through an app.

## Reply inside the app's own chat
- Answer in the app's own message box, on that booking. Don't move the customer to WhatsApp, a call or email unless the app allows it and the customer asked.
- WhatsApp quick replies only work in WhatsApp. Keep a copy of these replies in your phone notes and paste them into the app, or use the app's own saved replies if it has them (check in the app).

## Follow each app's messaging rules
Many apps don't allow these inside their chat:
- sharing phone numbers, email addresses or WhatsApp links;
- outside links (your own website, booking page or map link);
- asking for or taking payment outside the app (UPI, cash advance, bank transfer).

Rules differ from app to app and change often, so check the app's current rules (its help or partner pages) before you save a reply. If you're not sure something is allowed, leave it out.

For in-app drafts:
- No phone number, no "UPI: [your UPI ID]" and no [location link], unless the owner says the app allows them. Describe the place in words instead: "Meeting point: [blank]".
- Leave payment out unless the owner says how it works in that app, in their words.
- Everything else follows the main Rules: owner's facts only, [blank] for anything missing, start "Hi!", end with [your business name], 500 characters or fewer.
- Check every day name against its date before it goes in.

## Quick replies for pickup time, meeting point and what to bring
Start from these. Anything the owner didn't give stays [blank]. Never add "be ready 10 minutes early" or any time the owner didn't state.

**Shortcut:** `pickup-time`
```
Hi! Thanks for booking [activity] on [day and date].
Pickup time: [blank]
Pickup point: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

**Shortcut:** `meeting-point`
```
Hi! Here's where we meet for [activity].
Meeting point: [blank]
How to find us: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

**Shortcut:** `what-to-bring`
```
Hi! For [activity], please bring: [blank]
Please wear: [blank]
Any questions, reply here.
[your business name]
```

A guest who asks "what time is pickup?" for a booking the owner hasn't confirmed gets: "Let me check and confirm shortly."

## Sending your own activity list or link
Send your own list of activities, or a link to it, only where both are true:
1. the app's rules allow it (check the app's current rules), and
2. the customer asked (for example "what else do you do?" or "can you send all your options?").

Otherwise, answer the question in the chat in words. If your activities are already listed on your page in the app, point there.

**Shortcut:** `our-activities`
```
Hi! Thanks for asking. Here are our activities: [your activity list or link]
Tell us which one you'd like and the day, and we'll confirm.
[your business name]
```
Never add a phone number or a payment request to this message.

## What this helper can't do
It can't read, reply or auto-reply inside any app. The owner copies each draft into the app and sends it. For automatic replies inside an app, check that app's own help pages.
