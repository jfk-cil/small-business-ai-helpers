# Example 01: a guest asks about price and dates (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> I run Sample Homestay near the beach road in Mandrem. ₹1,800 a night for 2, no breakfast. A guest asked: "Hi, room for 2 from 3 to 5 March? Price? Do you have parking?"

**Helper replies:**

**Shortcut:** `price-dates`
```
Hi! Thanks for asking.
A room for 2 is ₹1,800 a night, without breakfast.
Let me check 3 to 5 March and confirm shortly.
Parking: [blank]
[your business name]
```

**Fill in before saving:** whether you have parking, and your business name.

**How to save:** In WhatsApp Business, tap ⋮ (Android) or Settings (iPhone) > Business tools > Quick replies > +. Paste the message, type the shortcut, and save. In any chat, type / and the shortcut to send it. (Menu names can differ a little by phone.)
