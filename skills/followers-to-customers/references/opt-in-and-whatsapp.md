# Opt-in and WhatsApp: the plain rules

The aim is a small list of people who chose to hear from you. A short list of people who said yes is worth more than a long list of people who didn't.

## The rules
1. **People must choose to join.** They reply YES, tap your invite link, or scan your QR code. Silence is not a yes. A like or a follow is not a yes.
2. **Never add people without asking.** Not to a WhatsApp group, a Community or a broadcast list. Not even past customers.
3. **No bought, scraped or copied lists.** Don't use numbers from another business, a group you are in, or a list someone sells.
4. **Never ask people to post their phone number in a comment.** Comments are public. Ask them to send a DM or tap your WhatsApp link instead.
5. **Say what they'll get and how often**, in the invite, in your own words. Keep to it.
6. **Make leaving easy and honor it at once.** Every welcome message says how to leave. When someone says STOP, remove them that day and don't message again.
7. **Keep customers' numbers out of AI chats.** Use codes (Customer 1) if you track anything with this helper.

## Which WhatsApp option?
All three are in the WhatsApp Business app. Menus, limits and any charges change, so check them in your app.

| Option | What it is | Good for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Broadcast list** | You send one message; each person gets it as a private chat from you. | A personal feel; replies come back to you one by one. | It only reaches people who have saved your number in their phone. Ask: "Please save this number so you get my updates." |
| **Community** (with its announcement group) | One space with an announcement group where only admins post, plus optional smaller groups. People join by an invite link. | A growing group of students or regulars; workshop groups. | Check what members can see about each other (for example, phone numbers) before you invite, and tell people. Only admins should post in the announcement group. |
| **Channel** | A one-way feed people follow. | Updates to many people with less back-and-forth. | Followers can't reply in a chat; tell them how to reach you (DM or your WhatsApp link). |

An ordinary group where everyone can post and see everyone's number is usually not the right place for a customer list.

## Starting wording (change to the owner's words)

**Invite (in a DM, after sending the free thing)**
```
Hi! Here's your [free thing]: [link]
I share [what you share] on WhatsApp, about [how often].
Want to join? Reply YES and I'll send the link.
[your business name]
```

**Link sent (after they reply YES)**
```
Lovely! Here's the link: [invite link]
For a broadcast list, please save this number so my messages reach you.
[your business name]
```

**Welcome**
```
Welcome! You'll get [what you share], about [how often].
To leave, reply STOP or exit any time.
[your business name]
```

**Bio or Status line**
```
Free [free thing] + workshop news first: [WhatsApp link]
```

## What this helper can't do, and what would be needed
- It can't send messages, add people, reply automatically or run a bot.
- Automatic WhatsApp messages (welcome sequences, keyword replies, reminders) need the **WhatsApp Business Platform** through an approved provider, the customer's opt-in and approved message templates. That is a builder project, not this helper.
- Automatic Instagram DM replies to comments need a tool Meta allows, connected to a professional account. Also a builder project.
- The manual version works today: save the replies above as WhatsApp quick replies and Instagram saved replies, use WhatsApp labels ("Free thing sent", "Joined", "Booked"), and keep one daily reply slot.

For any question about privacy or marketing law, check with a qualified person.
