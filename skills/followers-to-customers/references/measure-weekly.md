# Measure weekly: a 10-minute review

Once a week, on the same day, count what happened and choose one thing to change. Counts only: no targets, no rates, no comparing with other accounts. The only comparison is with your own last week.

## What to count
Find these in each app's insights for your professional account (menu names vary), in WhatsApp, and in your own booking notes.

| Count | Where to find it | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Posts | Your profile | Did the plan fit your week? |
| Saves | Post insights | People found it useful enough to keep. |
| Shares | Post insights | People thought it was worth passing on. |
| Keyword comments | Comments on your posts | People asked for the free thing. |
| DMs | Your inbox | People started a conversation. |
| New joiners | Your WhatsApp list, Community or Channel | People chose to hear from you. |
| Sign-ups | Your booking notes | People booked a workshop or class. |
| Sales | Your order notes | People bought (count orders, not money). |

Views, likes and follower numbers can go up and down for reasons nobody outside the platform can see. Note them if you like, but don't plan around them.

## The sheet (paste row 1 into a Google Sheet)
```
Week of	Posts	Saves	Shares	Keyword comments	DMs	New joiners	Sign-ups	Sales	One change to try
```
One row per week. No customer names or numbers in this sheet. For formulas, colors or a chart, use **tracker-sheet-builder**.

## The review, in four steps
1. Fill in this week's row.
2. Look at your own last few rows. Which series got the most saves, keyword comments or DMs?
3. Ask one question: where do people stop? (They watch but don't comment? Comment but don't join? Join but don't book?)
4. Write one change to try next week in the last column. Only one, so you can see if it helped.

## Changes to try, by where people stop
- **They watch but don't save or comment:** make the tip smaller and clearer; put the keyword prompt on screen as well as in the caption.
- **They comment but don't take the free thing:** reply sooner (your daily slot); check that the free thing is short and useful.
- **They take the free thing but don't join:** say more plainly what they'll get on WhatsApp and how often.
- **They join but don't book:** tell the list first about the next workshop or product, with one clear way to book.
- **You couldn't keep up:** cut a post, not the reply slot. A plan you keep beats a big plan you drop.

Nothing here guarantees more followers, reach or sales. Try one change for a few weeks and count what happens.
