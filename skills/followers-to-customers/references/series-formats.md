# Ten recurring series formats

A series is one kind of post that comes back on the same day each week, with the same name. People learn what to expect, and the owner stops starting from a blank page. Pick one series per pillar (Value, Process, Offer). Name it with a day and two or three plain words. Build it only from what the owner makes, knows or sells.

Every format below works as a reel, a photo post or a WhatsApp Status. Film on a phone with the app's own music library. Prefer hands, tools, work, food and the owner. Adults' faces only with their permission; never children's faces.

| # | Series (example name) | Pillar | Good for | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tuesday Technique Tip | Value | makers, food, services | One small tip from the owner's own work, shown over their hands. |
| 2 | Mistake and Fix | Value | makers, food, wellness | One common mistake the owner sees, and how they fix it. |
| 3 | Your Question, Answered | Value | all | One real question from comments or DMs, answered on screen. Don't show who asked. |
| 4 | Try This at Home | Value | makers, food, wellness | One small thing followers can try today with what they have. No prizes, no tagging. |
| 5 | Blank to Done | Process | makers, food | One piece from start to finish, sped up. |
| 6 | In the Studio (or Kitchen, or Van) | Process | all | A normal day: setting up, materials arriving, cleaning up. |
| 7 | Meet the Materials | Process | makers, food, wellness | One material, tool or ingredient the owner uses, and why they chose it, in their words. |
| 8 | Job of the Week | Process | services | One finished job, before and after, with the customer's permission. No faces, house numbers, addresses or car plates. |
| 9 | What's On | Offer | all | What is next to buy, book or join, and exactly how. One call to action. |
| 10 | Made For You | Offer | makers, food, services | A commission, custom order or class booking explained step by step: how to ask, what happens next. |

## How each pillar ends
- **Value** posts end with a save or comment prompt that matches the path: "Comment [WORD] and I'll send you [free thing]."
- **Process** posts end with a soft question ("Which color should I try next?") or the same keyword prompt.
- **Offer** posts end with one clear step: "Reply to my Status", "DM me BOOK", "Join my WhatsApp list: link in bio."

## Notes by business type
- **Makers (art, pottery, jewelry, candles):** process reels usually carry the account; keep the Offer post to one a week.
- **Food (bakeries, home kitchens, cafés):** taste and texture words are fine; health words are not ("guilt-free", "good for sugar" and similar are health claims). Food rules: check with a qualified person.
- **Wellness (yoga, movement, art for calm):** show what happens in a class (the space, the mat, the materials). Never promise health effects. "A slow, quiet class" is fine; "heals stress" is not.
- **Services (cleaning, repairs, tutoring, tours):** Job of the Week and Your Question, Answered work well. Ask the customer before showing their place, and hide anything that shows where they live.

## Safe-content checks before posting
- Is everything in it the owner's own photo, video and know-how?
- Is anyone's face in it? Do they agree? Is any child visible? (Then use another shot.)
- Does it promise a result, a health effect or a number the owner didn't give?
- If it's paid or gifted: does it start with "Ad" or "Collaboration with [partner]", and does it also use the platform's paid-partnership label?
