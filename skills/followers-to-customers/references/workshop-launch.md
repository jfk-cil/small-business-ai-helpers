# Launch a digital workshop: starting wording

Use the owner's notes only. Every date, time, price, length, platform, seat limit and material the owner didn't give stays [blank]. No fake rush: no countdowns, seat counts or "price goes up" lines unless the owner says they are true. No student results or testimonials unless the owner gives the real words and the person agreed. Wellness workshops: describe what happens, never health effects.

## Workshop outline
```
Workshop: [title]
For: [who it's for, in your words]
You'll make or learn: [from your notes]
Parts:
- [part, from your notes]
- [part]
- [part]
Have ready: [materials, from your notes]
How: [live online / recorded] on [platform]
When: [day and date], [time]
Length: [blank]
Price: ₹[blank]
Seats: [only if you have a real limit]
```

## Two-week launch plan
Each item is one post (reel, photo or Status) with one caption box of 60 words or fewer. Write the "when" in words and leave the date as [date].

**Teaser** (two weeks before: [date])
```
Something I've wanted to share for a while: [what they'll learn].
A small online workshop is coming.
Comment [WORD] and I'll send you the details first.
```

**Value post** (about ten days before: [date]): one real tip from the workshop, then the same keyword prompt.

**Value post** (one week before: [date]): a "Blank to Done" or "Mistake and Fix" from the workshop topic, then the keyword prompt.

**Open** (sign-ups open: [date])
```
Sign-ups are open for [title].
[what you'll make or learn], live on [platform], [day and date].
DM me JOIN and I'll send the details.
```

**Reminder** (a few days before sign-ups close or the workshop: [date]): one line on who it's for, the date, and the same DM prompt.

**Last call** (only if the owner set a real closing date: [date])
```
Sign-ups for [title] close on [closing date].
If it's for you, DM me JOIN and I'll send the details.
```
If there is no real closing date, write a plain "Still time to join" post instead, with no deadline.

## Messages
**Sign-up reply** (when someone DMs the keyword)
```
Hi! Thanks for asking about [title].
When: [day and date], [time], on [platform]
Price: ₹[blank]
To book, pay by UPI: [your UPI ID]
Send me a message once it's done and I'll confirm.
[your business name]
```

**Thank-you** (after the owner has checked the payment)
```
Hi! You're in for [title], thank you.
I'll send the joining link [when you'll send it].
Have ready: [materials]
[your business name]
```

**Reminders** (day before, on the day): use **booking-confirmations**.

## After the workshop
```
Hi! Thank you for joining [title].
Could you tell me honestly: what helped most, and what should I change next time?
Your answer stays with me.
[your business name]
```
Ask for a public review only if the owner wants one, and use their review link: [review link]. Never write the review.
