## Assumptions to check with a real owner
(These come from one owner's notes. Someone who works with owners should check them.)
1. Owners whose reels bring new people would rather have a small repeatable system (three named weekly series, one path, one reply slot) than more post ideas.
2. A "comment a word, get a free thing" step feels natural to their followers, and owners can make one small free thing (a checklist, a mini-lesson, a template) from notes they already have.
3. Followers are willing to join a WhatsApp Community, Channel or broadcast list when asked plainly, and owners will ask rather than add people.
4. Owners can find saves, shares and DMs in their app's insights and will fill one sheet row a week.
5. The copy boxes paste cleanly into Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp on an Android phone.
