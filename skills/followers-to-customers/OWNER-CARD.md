# Your followers-to-customers helper

**What it does:** helps people who watch your reels stay, join your WhatsApp list and then book or buy. It plans a simple weekly system, writes a week of posts that ask for one clear next step, and plans an honest launch for an online workshop.

## What to type
- "I run art workshops in Assagao. My reels get new people but nobody books. People book by DM. I have 3 hours a week. Build me a system."
- "Write a week of posts that convert for my pottery studio. Keyword CLAY for my free glaze guide. 4 posts."

## What you get
- Three kinds of posts (useful tips, behind the scenes, what's on sale), each a weekly series with a simple name like "Tuesday Technique Tip".
- The path: post, "comment a word", a free small thing you make, an invite to your WhatsApp list (only if they say yes), then your workshop or product.
- A weekly plan that fits the time you gave.
- A one-line sheet to count saves, shares, DMs, sign-ups and sales each week.
- Captions and replies, each in its own box with a **Copy** button. Anything you didn't tell it is marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check everything and post or send it yourself. It can't post, reply or add people for you. It won't buy followers, invent reviews, fake "only 3 seats left", or promise more sales. Only add people to your list if they say yes.
