# Post bank: reusable ideas and the monthly refresh

The post bank is a list of post ideas the owner keeps in a notes app or sheet and comes back to every month. Ideas, not captions: one line each, 15 words or fewer. For captions and hashtags, use **social-content-planner**.

## How to build it
- 12 to 24 ideas (18 if the owner gave no number), in 3 to 6 themes.
- Only from what the owner makes, does and said. Unknown details become [blank], or leave the idea out.
- No numbers in ideas unless the owner gave them (no "3 ways to...", no prices, no counts).
- Every idea can be shot without faces: hands, bakes, tools, the counter, the oven, the delivery bag. People only with their permission; never children's faces.
- No health words, no "best in Goa", no claims about other businesses.
- Each idea fits more than one month, so the bank can be reused.

## Themes to choose from (pick the ones that fit the owner)
| Theme | What it shows | Idea starters |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the work | How it's made | Hands shaping dough at dawn; the oven door opening; packing an order |
| The products | One item, up close | One item, one close-up, its name and how to order; a new item this month |
| Seasons and festivals | The owner's own calendar | What you bake for [festival the owner named]; the first week of [the owner's busy season] |
| Where to find us | Business accounts, markets | Our [item] now at a café near [area] (only with the café's permission); market day setup |
| How to order | The path to buy | How to join the WhatsApp list; how to order for a party; what to order ahead |
| Your story | Why the owner does this | Why you started; a favorite ingredient and where it comes from (owner's words) |
| Moments | Customers enjoying it | A box on a table, a gift being wrapped (no faces without permission) |

## The box in the reply
```
Theme: Behind the work
- Hands shaping sourdough before sunrise
- The first tray out of the oven
Theme: How to order
- How to join our WhatsApp bake list
```
Use hyphen bullets, not numbers, inside the box.

## Monthly refresh (four steps, about the same time each month)
1. **Look back:** from your monthly check, mark the ideas that got the most saves or messages.
2. **Keep and repeat:** move those to the top; post them again with a new photo next month.
3. **Swap out:** remove ideas that got nothing after you tried them twice.
4. **Add for next month:** add new ideas for next month's theme (season, festival or new item, in your words), then ask **social-content-planner** to write captions for the ones you'll post.
