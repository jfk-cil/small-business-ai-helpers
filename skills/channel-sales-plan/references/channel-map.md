# Channel map: one block per channel

Up to 3 channels. Each block has the same five lines. Fill them only from what the owner said; anything missing is a [blank].

```
**[n]. [channel name in the owner's words]**
- Who buys: ...
- What they buy: ...
- Next action: ...
- Have ready: ...
- How orders come in: ...
```

## The three common channels

### Selling to other businesses (cafés, shops, homestays, offices)
- **Who buys:** describe by what the business needs ("cafés that want fresh bakes for breakfast", "gift shops near the market"), never by name. The owner picks which ones; for finding them, point to **target-customer-finder**.
- **What they buy:** only the products the owner names. If the owner hasn't said which items suit businesses, write [blank: which items for cafés].
- **Next action:** one step, small enough for this week: "send the first message to one café you choose", "book one tasting visit", "check in with the shops that already stock you".
- **Have ready:**
  - a price list for businesses: [blank: your business prices]
  - samples or a tasting box (the owner decides what goes in)
  - a menu card or product card with names and ingredients in the owner's words (wording: **listing-and-catalog-writer**)
  - how and when you deliver: [blank]
  - order and payment terms: [blank] (check with a qualified person)
  - for food: labels as required (check FSSAI labeling with a qualified person)
- **How orders come in:** the owner's words (a WhatsApp message from the café owner, a weekly call, an order sheet). Lead times, minimum orders and delivery days only if the owner gave them; otherwise [blank].

### Selling direct on WhatsApp
- **Who buys:** people who already know you (regulars, neighbors, past buyers who said yes to updates), visitors who met you at a market or counter.
- **What they buy:** the owner's products for home, gifts or celebrations, in the owner's words.
- **Next action:** "put the join line on your counter card, bio and Status", "set up labels: New order, Paid, Delivered".
- **Have ready:**
  - a WhatsApp join line (see below)
  - a short catalog or price message ([blank: your prices])
  - payment: [your UPI ID]
  - quick replies for "price?", "how to order?" and "delivery?" (to write them, use **guest-inquiry-replies**)
- **How orders come in:** a chat to the business number; the owner confirms by hand.

**Which WhatsApp option** (all in the WhatsApp Business app; menus and limits change, so check in the app):
- **Broadcast list:** one message, each person gets it privately. Only reaches people who saved the owner's number.
- **Community announcement group:** only admins post; people join by link. Check what members can see about each other before inviting.
- **Channel:** a one-way feed people follow.

**Join line** (40 words or fewer, for a counter card, bio or Status):
```
Want [what you share] on WhatsApp, about [how often]? Tap [WhatsApp link] or scan the code, say YES and save our number. Leave any time: reply STOP.
[your business name]
```

**Other people's groups** (a building group, a neighborhood group, a market group):
1. Read the group rules. If selling isn't allowed, don't post.
2. Ask the group admin first, in a private message.
3. If yes, post once, at the time the admin allows, with the join line so people can choose to hear more.
4. Don't post the same thing in many groups, and don't add anyone from a group to your own list.

### Social posts (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Status)
- **Who buys:** people nearby and visitors who see the posts, in the owner's words.
- **What they buy:** what the posts show; each post points to one way to order.
- **Next action:** "pick 3 ideas from the post bank and photograph them this week."
- **Have ready:** a bio with the join line or order link, a few good photos of the products (hands and bakes, no faces without permission, never children's faces).
- **How orders come in:** a DM or the WhatsApp link in the bio, then the owner confirms by hand.

## Other channels the owner may name
A weekly market stall, a pop-up, a counter at home, a delivery app. Use the owner's words for every line. A paid delivery app's fees and terms: [blank], and check the terms yourself.

## Never
- Invent prices, discounts, margins, minimum orders, delivery days, credit periods, sale-or-return or shelf life.
- Name a real café, shop or app.
- Suggest bought, scraped or copied contact lists, or adding people to a list or group without asking.
