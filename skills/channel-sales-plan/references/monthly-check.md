# Monthly check: counts only

Once a month, on about the same day, count what happened on each channel and choose one change for next month. Counts only: no money, no rates, no comparing with other businesses. The only comparison is with your own last month.

## What to count
| Count | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Orders from each channel | Your order notebook or sheet (count orders, not money) |
| New business accounts | Cafés or shops that placed a first order this month |
| WhatsApp sign-ups | People who joined your list, Community or Channel this month |
| Saves or messages from posts | Post insights (menus vary) and your DMs |
| Tasting visits done | Your notebook |

Revenue, takings and money owed stay out of this sheet and out of any AI chat: "Keep them in your own accounts book or software, with your accountant, not in an AI chat."

## The sheet (paste into row 1 of a Google Sheet)
Use the owner's channel names. For three channels it looks like this:
```
Month	Café and shop orders	WhatsApp orders	Orders from posts	New business accounts	WhatsApp sign-ups	Saves or messages from posts	Tasting visits	One change next month
```
One row a month. No customer or café names or numbers in this sheet. For formulas or a chart, use **tracker-sheet-builder**.

Targets stay with the owner: "Target: [blank: your target]." Never fill a target in, and never say what a "good" number is.

## The review, in four steps
1. Fill in this month's row.
2. Compare with your own last month: which channel moved, which didn't?
3. Ask where people stop: they taste but don't order? They join but don't buy? They save posts but don't message?
4. Write one change for next month in the last column. Only one, so you can see if it helped.

## Changes to try, by where people stop
- **Cafés taste but don't order:** ask what would make it work for them; send the quote they asked for (**quote-and-proposal-writer**).
- **Few people join the WhatsApp list:** put the join line in more places you already use (counter card, bags, bio, Status).
- **People join but don't order:** tell the list first about what's new this month, with one clear way to order.
- **Posts get saves but no messages:** end each post with one way to order.
- **You couldn't keep up:** drop one action, not a whole channel. A plan you keep beats a big plan you drop.

Nothing here promises more orders. Try one change for a month and count what happens.
