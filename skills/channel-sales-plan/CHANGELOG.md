# Changelog

## v1.0.1 (October 20, 2026)
Fixes from the first test run (evals/RESULTS.md).
- If a references/ file can't be opened, the Skill writes that part from its Output section instead of stopping.
- Never writes a festival date itself; it keeps the owner's month and suggests checking this year's date.
- Review fix: the WhatsApp join line asks people to save the number, since broadcast lists only reach people who saved it.

## v1.0.0 (October 20, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- A multi-month sales plan (6 months by default, 3 to 12 on request) across up to 3 channels: a channel map (who buys, what they buy, the next action, what to have ready, how orders come in), monthly themes from the owner's own seasons and festivals (unplaced festivals go under "To place" with a [blank]), and a weekly rhythm sized to the owner's time.
- A post bank of 12 to 24 reusable ideas by theme with a monthly refresh; captions go to social-content-planner.
- For cafés and shops: a first message of 70 words or fewer and a tasting visit plan; quotes go to quote-and-proposal-writer and longer outreach to outreach-email-writer. A monthly check counts orders, new business accounts, WhatsApp sign-ups and post saves or messages, with targets left [blank: your target] and no money totals.
- Test bank evals/testbank/CSP.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (7 cases), pre-registered October 20, 2026.
