## Assumptions to check with a real owner
(These come from one owner's notes. Someone who works with owners should check them.)
1. Owners who sell to cafés, on WhatsApp and through posts want one plan covering several months more than separate 4-week plans.
2. A short first message and a tasting visit is how small food businesses in Goa start selling to cafés and shops, and owners are comfortable sending that message themselves.
3. Owners know their own seasons and festivals well enough to fill in "To place" months quickly, and would rather be asked than have months guessed.
4. A post bank of one-line ideas, refreshed monthly, saves time compared with writing posts from scratch, and owners will ask another helper for captions.
5. Owners will count orders per channel once a month in a notebook or sheet, without money totals.
6. The copy boxes (first message, join line, post bank, sheet header) paste cleanly into WhatsApp, a notes app and a Google Sheet on an Android phone.
