# Your tracker sheet helper

**What it does:** Designs a simple Google Sheet for one thing you want to keep track of: stock, orders, clients, room bookings, staff tasks, deliveries or vendors.

**What to type** (two examples)
- "Make me a stock sheet for my bakery. Things keep running out and some expire in the fridge."
- "I want a sheet for my homestay rooms so I stop double booking. Just me fills it in."

**What you get**
- A table to copy and paste into Google Sheets, with 3 made-up example rows (they start with "Sample", delete them later)
- The formulas, with the exact box each one goes in
- Which colors to set (for example, red when something needs reordering)
- 5 steps to set it up in the Google Sheets app on your phone (set the sheet to India before you paste, so dates come out right)

**Never paste these into the chat**
- Your contact list
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

Your real lists stay in your own sheet. If you want a list cleaned up, ask how, and you'll get steps to do it in your sheet.

**Not here:** cash books, sales money, invoices, payroll, GST or tax. Keep those on paper or ask a qualified person. No sheets that rate or rank staff.

**It's a draft: you check it, you build it, you decide what goes in it.**
