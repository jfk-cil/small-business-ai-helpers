# Example 01: a pet store's monthly expiry check and supplier returns (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Pet Supplies, Porvorim. Every month we have to arrange pickup of expired and near-expiry products, and my staff needs hand holding for it. Near expiry for us means 60 days or less. Our steps: 1) check the actual expired and near-expiry stock against the count in our Google Sheet, 2) make the purchase return in our billing software, 3) pack the products, 4) follow up with the vendor. The store assistant counts on the 1st, billing staff makes the purchase return by the 3rd, and I follow up. Give me the routine, a how-to for a new staff member, the tracker and the supplier messages.

**B. First-time how-to for new staff**
```
Monthly expiry check: how to do it (first time)
Near expiry here means 60 days or less before the expiry date.

- Get the tracker sheet, a pen and the "Set aside" box.
- Ask the owner to show you each step once.

Steps
1. Open the Google Sheet count. Go shelf by shelf, then the storeroom.
2. Read the expiry date on each pack. Write expired and near-expiry items in the tracker: item, batch, expiry date, quantity.
3. Put those items in the "Set aside: owner to decide" box.
4. Open [menu name in your billing software] and make one purchase return per supplier. Write its number in the tracker.
5. Pack each supplier's items in one labeled box with a copy of the purchase return.
6. At pickup, get your copy signed or stamped. Write the date in the tracker.

What to check
- The expiry date, not the packing or manufacturing date.
- Quantity in the box matches the purchase return.

Common mistakes
- Skipping the storeroom or the back of the shelf.
- Putting set-aside items back on the shelf.
- Packing without the purchase return copy.

Not sure? Stop and ask the owner.
```

**C. Tracker columns** (paste into cell A1 of a new Google Sheet)
```

```
Reason: Expired, Near expiry, Damaged or Slow. Credit note received: Yes or No. No money columns. For formulas, color warnings and phone setup, take these columns to **tracker-sheet-builder**.


**If the supplier still doesn't reply:** call them yourself, note the date and what they said in the tracker, and check your supplier's terms or agreement. For a dispute, check with a qualified person.
