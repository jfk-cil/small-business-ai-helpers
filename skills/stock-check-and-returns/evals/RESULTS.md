## Assumptions to check with a real owner
(These come from one owner's notes. Someone who works with owners should check them.)
1. The monthly job is the same each month (count, purchase return, pack, follow up), so a fixed routine with roles and days helps more than a one-off list.
2. A plain-line routine in one copy box, with a "Sign-off: ________" line under each step, is easy to forward to staff on WhatsApp and to print. Check whether owners would rather have it as a sheet tab.
3. Owners will type in the menu names from their own billing software once, since the helper can't see it.
4. Suppliers answer return requests on WhatsApp, and owners prefer to call (not send more messages) when a supplier stays quiet.
5. Owners are comfortable keeping money amounts (stock value, credit notes) out of the tracker and in their own accounts.
