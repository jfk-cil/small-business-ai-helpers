# Your stock check and returns helper

**What it does:** turns a monthly stock job (expiry and near-expiry check, returns to suppliers, damaged or slow stock) into a routine your staff can follow, a how-to for new staff, tracker columns and messages to your supplier.

## What to type
- "Pet shop in Porvorim. Every month we do the expiry check and return stock to the vendor. Near expiry is 60 days. Store assistant counts on the 1st, billing staff makes the purchase return by the 3rd. Staff needs hand holding."
- "6 lipsticks came broken from the distributor. Write the return request and a follow-up."

## What you get
- **The routine:** each step with who does it (a role, not a name), which day, how long, what "done" looks like, and a sign-off line.
- **A how-to** for a new staff member doing it the first time, with common mistakes.
- **Tracker columns** with quantities and dates (no money). Ask the tracker-sheet-builder helper to build the sheet.
- **Supplier messages:** a return or pickup request, one polite follow-up, and what to do if they don't reply.
- Each part in its own box with a **Copy** button. Anything you didn't tell it is marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people
- Stock value, credit-note amounts or other money records (quantities are fine)

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check everything, share it with your team, and send or call the supplier yourself. It can't see your billing software, so menu names are left as [menu name in your billing software]. It won't say whether expired items are safe to sell, donate or discount (check with a qualified person and your supplier's terms), and it won't make up your supplier's return rules.
