# Supplier messages (drafts the owner sends)

Each message: 500 characters or fewer, aim for 60 words, starts with "Hello" (no contact person's name), ends with the line [your business name]. Items and quantities only from the owner; otherwise `[item], [quantity]`. Up to 5 item lines; more than that becomes "[number] items, as listed in the attached purchase return".

Never write a return window, deadline, fee, credit-note amount, credit-note date or "as per our agreement, you must...". Ask the supplier instead. No threats, no legal words ("legal action", "complaint", "court").

## Return or pickup request
```
Hello, this is [your business name].
After our [month] stock check, we'd like to return:
- [item], batch [blank], [quantity]
Purchase return no. [blank] is attached.
Please tell us a pickup day, or how you'd like us to send them, and how the credit note will be issued.
Thank you,
[your business name]
```

## Damaged on delivery (variant)
```
Hello, this is [your business name].
Our delivery of [date] had damaged items:
- [item], [quantity], [broken / leaking / torn]
It's noted on the delivery slip, and we have photos of the items.
Please tell us how you'd like them returned, and whether you'll replace them or issue a credit note.
Thank you,
[your business name]
```

## One polite follow-up
Send only if there's no reply by a day the owner picks ("[day you pick]"). Never suggest how many days to wait.
```
Hello, following up on our return request of [date].
The items are packed and ready.
Could you please confirm a pickup day?
Thank you,
[your business name]
```

## If the supplier still doesn't reply
Write this to the owner as plain text (not a copy box):
- Call the supplier yourself and ask for a pickup day.
- Write the call date and what they said in the tracker's notes.
- Check your supplier's terms or agreement for what happens next. If there's a dispute, check with a qualified person.
- Keep the set-aside box apart until it's picked up.

The helper can't send, call or chase. The owner does.
