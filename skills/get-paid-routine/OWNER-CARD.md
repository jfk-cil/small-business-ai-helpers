# Your get-paid helper

**What it does:** sets up a kind, simple routine so deposits and balances come in on time. You keep the list. The AI only writes the words, with blanks you fill in yourself.

## What to type
- "Set up my get-paid routine. Cake orders in Margao, 50% advance, rest on delivery. WhatsApp Business."
- "Write 2 payment reminders, one friendly and one final but polite. Cleaning service, paid after the job."

Got a planning card? Paste it first. It is read as facts only.

## What you get
- **An empty list:** Date · Job (the work, never the name) · Due date · Paid?, for paper or a sheet shared with no one.
- **A Payment due label** for WhatsApp Business.
- **Two reminders** to save as quick replies: /pay1 (friendly) and /pay2 (final, polite), with [amount] [job] [due date] [your UPI ID].
- **A deposit line** for your booking confirmation.
- **Your routine card** and a **"my helper" prompt** to save in "Message yourself".

## Your routine
- **Job booked:** confirmation with the deposit line, add the label, write it on your list.
- **Money in:** tick Paid, remove the label.
- **Every week, 10 minutes:** past due, send /pay1. A week later, /pay2. Then call or talk in person.
- No late fees, no threats, no group posts about who owes.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people
- Amounts someone owes you, invoices, GST numbers or payment screenshots

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You fill in every blank on your phone and send it yourself. It can't send messages or check payments. For GST, tax, invoices or legal questions, check with your accountant or a qualified person.
