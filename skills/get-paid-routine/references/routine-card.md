# Routine card: get paid on time

The long owner version of the routine, for printing or for a builder to walk through with the owner. The short card in SKILL.md is the one the owner saves in "Message yourself".

**Who it's for:** homestays, cake orders, cleaners, studios: anyone paid by deposit or after the job. The label needs the free WhatsApp Business app (green B icon); without it, use the paper list only.

## Start from your planning card
- Card table "A checklist", and the job is quotes, deposits or payments? Start here.
- Your "My first small move" becomes today's step 1 if it fits.
- Your "Keep out of any AI" line is your own no-go list, on top of the one below.

## Today (about 10 minutes)
1. In WhatsApp Business, make a label: **Payment due**. (Android: usually ⋮ > Labels; iPhone: usually Settings > Business tools > Labels. Tap + and type the name.)
2. Save 2 reminder texts as quick replies (Business tools > Quick replies > +): **pay1** (friendly) and **pay2** (final, polite). They have blanks: [amount] [job] [due date] [your UPI ID]. You fill them in yourself, on your phone, just before sending.
3. Add the deposit line to your booking confirmation.

## At home this week
- Start one private list on paper (or a sheet shared with no one, never "anyone with the link"):

```
Date | Job | Due date | Paid?
```

  Job is the work, never the customer's name.
- Set one weekly phone alarm, same day each week, named "Payments".

## Every day
- **Job booked:** send the confirmation with the deposit line, add the Payment due label, write it on your list.
- **Money arrives:** check it in your own payment app or bank, tick Paid, remove the label.

## Every week (10 minutes, same day)
1. Open the Payment due label (or your list).
2. Past due and no reminder yet: send /pay1.
3. Sent /pay1 a week ago and still unpaid: send /pay2.
4. Sent /pay2 and still nothing: call or talk in person.

No late fees, no threats, no group or public posts about who owes. One person, one private chat.

## Ask the AI (any free AI chat)
- "Write a friendly WhatsApp reminder in [language] that a payment is due. Use blanks: [amount], [job], [due date], [your UPI ID]. No threats or fees."
- "Now write one more reminder, polite and final, same blanks."

**Never in AI:** amounts tied to a person, customer names, UPI IDs, bank details, GST numbers, invoices, payment screenshots.

## It's working when
- Every Payment due chat has a date on your list.
- At your weekly check, no deposit is more than a week past due without a reminder.

For GST, tax, invoices, legal or debt questions, check with your accountant or a qualified person.

## No WhatsApp Business? The paper version
Normal WhatsApp has no labels or quick replies. Instead:
1. Paste /pay1, /pay2 and the deposit line into the "Message yourself" chat and pin it. Copy from there, fill in the blanks, and send.
2. Your paper list is your only "Payment due" list. Check it on the same day each week.
3. Save this card in "Message yourself":

```
My get-paid routine (paper)
Job booked: send the confirmation with the deposit line. Write it on my list.
Money arrives: tick Paid.
Every week, same day, 10 min: check my list. Past due: send reminder 1.
Still unpaid a week later: send reminder 2. Then call or talk in person.
Kind and private: no added fees, no threats, no public posts.
Never in AI: amounts with a name, names, UPI or bank details, GST numbers, invoices, payment screenshots.
Working when: every unpaid job on my list has a due date.
```

When you're ready, the free WhatsApp Business app adds the Payment due label and quick replies.
