# Example 01: a homestay's room turnover checklist (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Homestay, Siolim. Room turnover keeps going wrong. Here's how I do it: open the windows, strip the bed, take used towels to the laundry basket, wipe the bathroom and the mirror, refill toilet paper and soap, sweep and mop, make the bed with fresh sheets, put 2 fresh towels, empty the dustbin, check the AC remote is there, switch off fan and lights, lock the room. My staff read English.

**Helper replies:**

**1. Your checklist** (room turnover, in English)
```
Room turnover checklist
1. Open the windows.
2. Strip the bed.
3. Take used towels to the laundry basket.
4. Wipe the bathroom and the mirror.
5. Refill toilet paper and soap.
6. Sweep and mop the floor.
7. Make the bed with fresh sheets.
8. Put out 2 fresh towels.
9. Empty the dustbin.
10. Check the AC remote is in the room.
11. Switch off the fan and lights.
12. Lock the room.
Done: send one photo of the finished room. No people, guest belongings, papers or screens.
```

**2. Pinned message for the staff group**
```
Room turnover checklist (pinned)
Follow each line. When you finish, send one photo of the finished room. No people, guest belongings, papers or screens.
1. Open the windows.
2. Strip the bed.
3. Take used towels to the laundry basket.
4. Wipe the bathroom and the mirror.
5. Refill toilet paper and soap.
6. Sweep and mop the floor.
7. Make the bed with fresh sheets.
8. Put out 2 fresh towels.
9. Empty the dustbin.
10. Check the AC remote is in the room.
11. Switch off the fan and lights.
12. Lock the room.
```
**To pin:** send it in the staff group, long-press it, tap Pin and choose 30 days. Pins end after that, so re-pin it every month. Can't pin? Ask the group admin. (Menus vary a little by phone.) Optional: a paper copy for the wall.

**3. Your routine** (save it in WhatsApp "Message yourself")
```
My checklist routine
Every day: staff follow the pinned list and send one photo of the finished room.
You: glance for 2 minutes. A missed line waits for the huddle.
Never name anyone in the group. Photos are never used to check who or when.
Every [day]: 10-minute huddle. Ask "Which line is hard or gets missed?" Change one line. No blame.
Every month: re-pin the list.
It's working when: the same thing stops being missed, and staff ask to change a line.
```

**4. My helper** (save it in "Message yourself" and paste it into any AI chat when a line gets missed)
```
My room turnover checklist is below. Line [number] is hard or gets missed. Rewrite only that line so it is clearer: one action, starting with a verb, 12 words or fewer, in English. Keep every other line word for word, with the same numbers. Use only my words; put [blank] for anything missing. No names, no blame.
[paste your checklist here, no names]
```

**This week**
- Today: pin the checklist in the staff group for 30 days.
- Every day: one photo of each finished room; glance for 2 minutes.
- On [day]: a 10-minute huddle. Change one line.

**Fill in:** the day for your weekly huddle.

It's a draft: you and a staff member check it before you pin it.
