# Your staff checklist helper

**What it does:** turns how you do one job (opening, closing, room turnover, kitchen prep) into a short checklist in your staff's language, ready to pin in your staff WhatsApp group. Then it helps you fix one line a week. No blame, no tracking.

## What to type
- "Closing checklist for my shack. We stack chairs, wipe tables, close the gas, cover the bar, lock the shutter. My staff read Hindi."
- "Line 6 of our opening checklist keeps getting missed. Here's the list: ..."

## What you get
- **A checklist** of 8 to 12 short lines, one action each, in your order.
- **A pinned message** for the staff group. Pin it for 30 days; pins end, so re-pin every month. Can't pin? Ask the group admin.
- **A routine:** staff send one photo of the finished space (no people, guest belongings, papers or screens). Once a week, a 10-minute huddle: "Which line is hard or gets missed?" Change one line. No blame.
- **My helper:** a short prompt for "Message yourself" to fix a missed line.
- Each part in its own box with a **Copy** button. Anything you didn't say is marked [blank].

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff names with comments, staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people (staff photos never go into AI)
- Anything on your planning card's "Keep out of any AI" line

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check it, and a staff member reads any translation before you pin it (AI can get Konkani and other languages wrong). It won't add steps you didn't give, and it never rates, tracks or punishes staff.

**It's working when:** the same thing stops being missed, and staff ask to change a line.
