# Your target customer helper

**What it does:** Tells you which kinds of customers to go after, where to find them, and 10 things to type into Google Maps, Instagram or LinkedIn.

**What to type** (two examples)
- "I run a small testing lab in Verna. Most work comes from 2 hotels. Who else should I sell to in South Goa?"
- "I make handwoven bags and stoles. Who would buy them and where do I find those people?"

**What you get**
- 2 or 3 kinds of customer (like "colleges that run fests" or "cafés that need monthly checks") and why they fit
- Where each kind can be found in public
- 10 search phrases to copy and try today
- One small step for this week

**Never paste these into the chat**
- Your contact list
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details (it writes [your UPI ID])
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

**It won't:** make lists of people with phone numbers, copy someone's followers, or help you WhatsApp strangers in bulk. WhatsApp can block your number for that. You do the searching; first contact goes by email or their business page.

**It's a draft: you check it, you search, you decide who to contact.**
