## Assumptions to check with a real owner
(These come from owners' notes about posts and reels. Someone who works with owners should check them.)
1. Owners would rather film 3 short reels a week from what they already do than plan a big content calendar.
2. Every shot can really be filmed alone, with the phone on a stand or a ledge, in a few minutes during normal work.
3. A 70 to 140 word voice-over (about 30 to 60 seconds) is a length owners will record in one or two takes, or paste as a caption.
4. Owners accept "no faces without permission, never children's faces" even when their customers are families or kids' classes.
5. The copy boxes (on-screen text and voice-over) paste cleanly into the video app's text tool and caption box on an Android phone.
