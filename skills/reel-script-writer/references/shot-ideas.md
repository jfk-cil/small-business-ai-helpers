# Shot ideas by business type

Every shot here can be filmed alone, with the phone on a stand, a ledge or in one hand. Build reels only from what the owner says they do; these are ideas for how to film it, not facts to add. Faces: adults only with their permission. Never children's faces.

| Business | Shots that work alone | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Surf or water-sports school | Boards lined up on the sand; waxing a board, close on hands; the instructor's feet walking into the water; a wide shot of the line-up from behind; rinsing gear at the end of the day | Students' faces only with permission; never children's faces (film boards, the beach, backs from far away); no "stand up on day one" or safety promises |
| Pottery or craft studio | Hands centering clay; trimming; dipping in glaze; the kiln shelf; a slow pan across finished pieces | No "food-safe", "dishwasher-safe" or "lead-free" unless the owner says so |
| Café or bakery | Dough being folded; the oven door opening; a slice being cut; the counter at opening time; the menu board | Taste and texture words are fine; health words are not ("guilt-free", "good for sugar", "boosts immunity") |
| Homestay | Morning light on the veranda; the made bed, close on the linen; breakfast being set out; the garden path; the view from the gate | No guests' faces or belongings; no "best in Goa" or ratings |
| Cleaning or repair service | Tools laid out; a close-up of one job in progress; the finished surface; packing up | A before and after only from the same real job, with the customer's permission, and no faces, house numbers, addresses or car plates |
| Rentals (scooters, cameras, party gear) | The item being checked and wiped; the hand-over bag being packed; keys or parts laid out | No safety promises; license and insurance rules: check with a qualified person |
| Yoga, dance or fitness class | Mats being rolled out; the empty room at sunrise; the teacher's own hands and feet in a pose; props set out | Describe what happens in class, never what it heals or fixes; no before-and-after of people |
| Nonprofit or community group | Gloves and bags set out; hands sorting; the place before people arrive; a wide shot from behind | Volunteers' faces only with permission; never children's faces; no counts the group didn't give |
| Shop (pets, groceries, gifts) | Shelves being stocked; one new product, close up; the counter; packing an order | No brand logos you don't own; no medical claims for pet or health products |

## Five reel shapes that work for most owners
1. **How it's made / how we do it:** three to five steps of one real piece of work.
2. **A tip from your work:** one small thing you know, shown over your hands.
3. **What's on:** what's new or coming up, and the one way to book or buy.
4. **A normal day:** opening up, the middle of the day, closing.
5. **A question customers ask:** the question as on-screen text, the answer in your words (never show who asked).
