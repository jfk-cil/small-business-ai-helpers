# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (October 20, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Two jobs: a weekly set of 3 to 5 reel ideas from what the owner actually does, or one reel with a second hook to try. Each script has a hook of 12 words or fewer, 3 to 6 shots the owner can film alone on a phone, 2 to 4 on-screen text lines, a 70 to 140 word voice-over or caption (about 30 to 60 seconds) and one next step.
- Honesty rules: no trending-audio, algorithm or view promises; no fake reviews; no before-and-after claims about people or results; no health or result claims; paid or gifted collabs start with "Ad" or "Collaboration with [partner]" plus the platform's paid-partnership label; no faces without permission and never children's faces.
- New references/shot-ideas.md (shots by business type and five reel shapes). Test bank evals/testbank/RSW.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (7 cases), pre-registered October 20, 2026.
