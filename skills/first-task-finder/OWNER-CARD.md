# Your first task helper

**What it does:** Asks you up to 5 quick questions about your business, then picks ONE job to try with AI first and gives you the first message to send.

**What to type** (two examples)
- "I have a homestay in Candolim and I'm not sure where AI can help."
- "I run a small café. Everything takes my time. Where do I start?"

**What you get**
- Up to 5 short questions in one message (answer them all at once, short is fine). If you already said it, it won't ask again.
- One starting job, in plain words
- The name of the helper that does that job
- A ready first message to copy, with [blank]s for you to fill in

**Never paste these into the chat**
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

**Not here, for now:** bookkeeping, cash records, payroll, tax, or judging staff. these helpers do not cover those; meanwhile, ask a qualified person. A polite payment reminder message is fine.

**It's a draft: you check it and you decide what to send.**
