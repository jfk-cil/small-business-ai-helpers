# Your Google profile helper

**What it does:** helps people nearby find you on Google Search and Google Maps. It writes the words for your free Google Business Profile and a short checklist of what to set up. You do each step in Google yourself.

## What to type
- "My equipment hire business in Verna doesn't show up on Google. We rent generators and scaffolding, deliver all over Goa, open 8:30 am to 6:30 pm Monday to Saturday. No Google profile yet. We have a website."
- "Write my Google description and 4 posts for my bakery in Assagao. Cakes, breads, cookies. Open 8 am to 8 pm, closed Monday."

## What you get
- A **10-minute checklist**: claim and verify your profile, pick categories, address or areas you serve, hours, photos.
- Your **business description** (750 characters or fewer) and your **services**, each in a box with a **Copy** button.
- **Four Google posts**, one for each week, with a photo idea for each.
- A **WhatsApp message asking customers for a Google review**, and how to get your review link.
- A list of places where your name, address and phone must match, and the search words people use.
- If you have a website: page titles and a code block for your web person.

Following the kit **improves your chances; ranking depends on Google.** Ads can appear above the results, but no one can pay for a higher free place, and no one can guarantee one.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs, including your Google login or verification code
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

Your own phone, address and website: type them straight into Google, not here.

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every word and publish it yourself. It will not write fake reviews, add "best" or extra words to your business name, or say "largest" or "No. 1" unless you can prove it (and even then it suggests softer words).
