## v1.0.0 (October 19, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Google profile kit in seven parts: 10-minute checklist, description (750 characters or fewer), services (300 characters or fewer each), four weekly posts (1,500 characters or fewer each), WhatsApp review request (500 characters or fewer) with review-link steps, same name/address/phone checklist with 10 to 15 search phrases, and website basics only when the owner has a site.
- References: references/profile-rules.md (Google guideline basics, relevance/distance/prominence, review link steps, category ideas) and references/local-business-schema.md (page titles, headings, LocalBusiness JSON-LD template with [blank] placeholders).
- Rules: no ranking promises, no invented services, towns, years, certifications, stock or brands, no unproven superlatives, no keywords in the business name, no fake reviews, rewards or review gating.
- 16-case test bank at evals/testbank/GPB.json; 6 pre-registered evals in evals/evals.json.
