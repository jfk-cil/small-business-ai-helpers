## v1.0.0 (October 19, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Three modes: fix my hiring flow (5 to 7 named stages, the owner's counts only, the likely leak and 1 or 2 fixes per stage, which messages to fix first), recruitment kit for one role (job post of 1,200 characters or fewer, WhatsApp version of 500 or fewer, outreach, places to try, applicant quick replies, finish-your-registration reminder), and onboarding checklist (Day 0, Day 1, Week 1, in-person document check, three welcome messages).
- Hiring rules: a person decides; never rank, score, shortlist or auto-reject; codes like "Applicant 1" instead of people's details; job-related questions only; never invent pay, benefits, registrations or legal requirements; nothing that judges or monitors staff.
- References: kit-templates.md (skeletons and the manual WhatsApp Business setup), fair-hiring.md (same questions for everyone, what never to ask), healthcare-notes.md (no patient data, clinical scope from qualified people, no medical claims).
- Pre-registered 7 evals (evals/evals.json) and a 16-case test bank (evals/testbank/SHO.json); not yet run.
