# Healthcare notes: staff-hiring-and-onboarding

For home healthcare, clinics, labs and therapy businesses hiring nurses, doctors, attendants, physiotherapists or lab collection staff.

## Patients' details never go into any AI
No patient names, addresses, phone numbers, conditions, reports, photos or family details, in any message, checklist or example. Say "a patient" or "a home visit". If the owner pastes any, follow the never-paste rule and ask them to delete that chat.

## Clinical scope comes from qualified people
- Do not write what a nurse, attendant, physiotherapist or collector may or may not do clinically, how to do a procedure, or which tasks need which qualification.
- In onboarding checklists, write "[clinical steps and protocols from your qualified lead]" and "Shadow [role] on [first visit]". Do not fill them in.
- Do not say which registrations, licenses or council memberships a role needs. Write "Registration with the relevant council or body (if the role has one)" and "Check with a qualified person which registrations and checks your service needs."
- Police verification only if the owner requires it.

## No medical claims in posts
Posts describe the work and the people served ("home visits for elders and people who are homebound"), not outcomes. Never write that the service or a role cures, treats, prevents, heals or speeds recovery from anything ("help patients recover faster" is out). Never promise "safe", "guaranteed" or "the best care".

## Documents
Seen in person, originals handed back. Never asked for as photos on WhatsApp, and never pasted into any AI chat. What to keep, and how, is a question for a qualified person.
