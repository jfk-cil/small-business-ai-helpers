# Your helper builder

**What it does:** makes YOUR OWN helper for one job in your business, from your planning card or a few words. You get a short prompt to paste into any AI chat, a quick test, and (if you want) a Claude Project or a Skill for your builder.

## What to type
- "Here's my planning card. Make me my own helper." (then paste the card, no customer names)
- "Make me a helper for replying to lesson questions. Surf school in Arambol, beginner lessons every morning, board included."

## What you get
- It asks one short question at a time, 5 at most, only what it doesn't know. Say "pass" to skip.
- **A. Your helper:** a short prompt (under 900 characters) in a box with a **Copy** button. Save it in WhatsApp "Message yourself". Next time, paste it into a new chat, then type your job.
- **B. Test it once:** 5 test messages and what a good answer looks like. One has a made-up phone number, one has a trick instruction.
- **C. Paid Claude plan:** the same helper as Project instructions, and a facts file to fill in.
- **D. For your builder:** a short Skill draft for a laptop.
- If a ready helper already does your job, it tells you its name.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You test the helper and use it yourself. It never sends messages, runs a bot, copies data from other places, scores people, keeps money records or gives legal, tax or medical advice.
