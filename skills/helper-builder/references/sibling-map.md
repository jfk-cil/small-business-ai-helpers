# Sibling map: which helper already does the job

Use this before building. If a helper in this repo already does the job, say so in one line before part A, and start part D with "The Skill **[sibling]** already does this job. Install it instead; use this draft only if your job is different." If the owner asked you to do the job itself (reply to this message, write this week's posts), hand off in two or three lines and build nothing.

A sibling does not stop you building: the owner's helper is a short personal version with their own facts. It only changes the one line before A and the first line of D.

## Jobs never to build, and where to point

| The job needs | Say (one line) | Point to |
|---|---|---|
| Sending, posting or replying on its own; a WhatsApp bot; scheduled sends | "A helper can't send for you. An automatic WhatsApp bot needs the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, the customer's opt-in and approved message templates: a builder project." | The manual version: quick replies, labels, greeting and away messages (**message-sorter**, **customer-messages-routine**) |
| Copying data from websites, groups, profiles or map listings | "I can't build a helper that copies people's details from other places." | **target-customer-finder** (kinds of customers and where to meet them) |
| Scoring, ranking or rating customers, staff or applicants | "I can't build a helper that scores or ranks people." | Plain fit questions: **lead-qualifier**; staff: **sop-and-checklist-writer** (steps, not scores) |
| Money records (who owes what, takings, expenses, loans, payslips) | "Keep money records in your own accounts book or software, with your accountant, not in an AI chat." | Reminder wording the owner sends by hand: **get-paid-routine** |
| Legal, tax, GST, FSSAI, licensing or medical advice | "That needs a qualified person." | **a person** (a qualified one) |
| Collecting names, phone numbers, ID, health details or photos of people | "A helper must never collect personal details." | A form or register the owner keeps outside AI; ID and Form C rules: check with a qualified person |

If a safe part is left (for example, polite wording for a reminder the owner sends by hand, with [amount] and [your UPI ID]), build only that part and say so in one line.
