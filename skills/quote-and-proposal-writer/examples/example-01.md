# Example 01: Sample Bakery quotes an office order (fictional)

## Owner types
> Quote from Sample Bakery for an office in Panjim: 40 veg puffs at Rs 30, 2 chocolate cakes at Rs 900. Delivery Rs 200. Full payment on delivery.

## Helper replies

**QUOTATION**
From: Sample Bakery | [your phone or WhatsApp]
To: [their name]
Date: [date]
Valid until: [date]
For: Office order, Panjim, [delivery date]

**Items**
1. Veg puffs: 40 x Rs 30 = Rs 1,200
2. Chocolate cake: 2 x Rs 900 = Rs 1,800
3. Delivery to Panjim: 1 x Rs 200 = Rs 200

Subtotal: Rs 1,200 + Rs 1,800 + Rs 200 = Rs 3,200
[GST if applicable]: [blank] (check tax with your accountant)
Total: Rs 3,200 + [GST if applicable]

**Terms**
- Full payment on delivery.
- Payment: [your UPI ID] or [cash]
- Not included: [blank]
- This quote is valid until the date above.

**Check before sending:** every number above, plus [your phone or WhatsApp], [their name], [date], [delivery date], what is not included.
It's a draft: you check it and send it yourself.

## Why it looks like this
- Every price is the owner's own. Each line shows quantity x price, and the subtotal shows the addition, so the owner can check it on a calculator.
- Tax is a blank because tax rules are a question for an accountant.
- The client's name and the dates are blanks for the owner to fill in.
