# Your one-link page helper

**What it does:** puts all your offers (things you sell, classes, custom orders) on one simple page with one button each, so people find their own way to buy, book or ask. One link for your Instagram bio and WhatsApp profile.

## What to type
- "Make my checklist. I sell paintings and prints on a shop app, run workshops booked on WhatsApp, and take commissions on Instagram."
- "Write my page." Then: "Build the page file." Then: "What happens after someone taps Book?"

## What you get
- **Offer map:** each offer, who it's for, the one thing you want people to do, and what's missing.
- **A 10-step checklist** in a box with a **Copy** button. Paste it into a phone note.
- **Your page words:** a headline, one block and one button per offer, how to order, and up to 5 questions and answers.
- **The page file:** one file a helper can put online for free, plus how to set your Instagram bio link and WhatsApp Business website link.
- **After the click:** a reply, a thank-you and one follow-up for each offer, and a short weekly count.
- Every link is left as a placeholder like [your shop link]. You type your real links in yourself.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs, and keys or logins for your shop, payment or website tools
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank]. If you pasted a password or key, change it in that tool too.

## It's a draft
You check every word and publish it yourself. It won't make up prices, dates, sizes, shipping or refund rules, or reviews, and it can't log in, take payments or put the page online for you.
