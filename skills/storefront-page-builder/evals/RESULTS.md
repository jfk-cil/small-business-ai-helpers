## Assumptions to check with a real owner
1. Owners with several offers want one page with one button per offer more than a full website with menus. Check with an artist who sells and runs workshops.
2. "Buy, book, ask, join list" covers every offer owners have. Check custom and bulk orders, gift cards and digital workshops.
3. Owners on a phone will use the Mode B words in Google Sites or a link-in-bio service, and the HTML file mostly goes to a helper with a laptop. Check who actually publishes.
4. Moving "ask" offers (commissions, custom cakes) from Instagram DMs to WhatsApp with a ready message is welcome, not extra work.
5. A weekly count of inquiries, orders and bookings, without amounts, is enough to see whether the page helps.
6. The dashed red outline on unfilled links is understood without explanation when the owner previews the page.
