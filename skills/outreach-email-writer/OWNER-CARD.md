# Your outreach email helper

**What it does:** writes a short, honest first email to a business you'd like to work with (a hotel, a cafe, an office). If you ask, it also writes up to 3 polite follow-ups for if they don't reply.

## What to type
- "Write a first email from my bakery to a cafe on the beach road in Mandrem. They do big breakfasts. Croissants Rs 60 each, minimum 20."
- "I emailed a hotel in Panjim about yoga classes last week and they didn't reply. Write my follow-ups."

## What you get
- **Email 1** with a subject line, ready to paste into your email.
- Up to **3 short follow-ups** if you ask for them, with when to send each one (about 5 days, 1 week, 2 weeks).
- A "no thanks" line in every email, so it's easy for them to say no. If they say no, you stop writing.
- A list of anything you still need to fill in, marked [blank].

## Tell it one true thing
The best emails mention one real thing about the other business: what they sell, where they are, something on their own business page. Tell the helper that one thing. It won't guess or look anything up.

## Never paste
- Lists of emails or phone numbers you copied from social media or bought
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It won't
- Say "offer ends tonight" or "only 2 spots left" unless it's true and you tell it so.
- Make up happy customers, numbers or reviews.
- Write a 4th follow-up.

## It's a draft
You check every email, fix anything that's wrong, and send it yourself.
