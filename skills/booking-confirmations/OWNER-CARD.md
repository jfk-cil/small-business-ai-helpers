# Your booking messages helper

**What it does:** writes the messages that get customers to confirm and turn up: a confirmation, reminders, a "please reply YES" nudge, change and cancel replies, a kind note if they don't come, and a thank-you or results-ready note. Ready to save as WhatsApp quick replies.

## What to type
- "Water testing lab in Margao. Customers drop the sample at our lab, Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 1 pm, in our bottle. Remind them the day before. Make my booking messages."
- "Confirm this booking: deep cleaning, Saturday 24 October, 10 am. We go to the house. They must keep water and power on."

## What you get
- Each message in its own box with a **Copy** button.
- A short **shortcut** for each (like `tomorrow`). Type / and that word in any WhatsApp Business chat to send it.
- A 5-step daily routine: morning reminders, evening reminders for tomorrow.
- The columns for a simple booking sheet, and how to see tomorrow's bookings.
- A list of anything to fill in, marked [blank]. It never makes up how much, how long or by when. Use the prep template once to write your own instructions.

## Never paste
- Guest or customer phone numbers and names
- Passport, Form C, Aadhaar or PAN details
- Bank, UPI and card details
- Passwords and OTPs
- Health information
- Staff ID numbers and wages
- Family members' details
- Photos of people

If you paste one by mistake, stop, delete that chat, and type it again with a [blank].

## It's a draft
You check every message, make sure the slot is really free, and send it yourself. This helper can't send or schedule messages. It won't say what a test result means or that anything is safe: that's for a qualified person.
