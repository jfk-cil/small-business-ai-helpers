# Message set: starting wording

Start from these and change them to the owner's facts. Keep every [blank] the owner hasn't filled. Every message starts "Hi!", ends with [your business name], and stays at 500 characters or fewer. Remove a line in [square brackets] if the owner has nothing for it; never fill it with a guess.

Three versions are given where it matters: **A** for "the customer comes to you" (lab drop-off, salon, class, homestay check-in), **B** for "you go to them" (cleaning, repairs, home visits) and **C** for "you pick them up" (tours, activities, transfers, rentals with pickup).

## Confirmation (`booked`)
Send only after the owner has checked the slot is free. If not checked yet, send: "Thanks! Let me check and confirm shortly."

A, customer comes to you:
```
Hi! Your [service] is booked.
When: [day and date], [time]
Where: [location link]
Please bring: [what to bring or do]
To change it, reply here [how far ahead].
[your business name]
```

B, you go to them:
```
Hi! Your [service] is booked.
When: [day and date], [time]
We'll come to you.
Before we come: [what to have ready]
To change it, reply here [how far ahead].
[your business name]
```

C, you pick them up:
```
Hi! Your [service] is booked for [day and date].
Pickup time: [time]
Meeting point: [meeting point]
Please bring: [what to bring]
To reach the driver: [blank]
To change it, reply here [how far ahead].
[your business name]
```
Pickup time, meeting point and what to bring come only from the owner; never add "be ready 10 minutes early". Keep "To reach the driver: [blank]" and let the owner fill it in when sending; never type a driver's number into this chat. Check the day name against the date before it goes in. If the booking came through a booking app, the owner sends it in that app's own chat and follows its messaging rules (many don't allow phone numbers or outside links: check the app's current rules).

## Reminder, the day before (`tomorrow`)
```
Hi! A reminder: your [service] is tomorrow, [day and date], at [time].
Please bring: [what to bring or do]
Reply YES to confirm, or tell us if you need a new time.
[your business name]
```
For version B, change "Please bring" to "Before we come".

C, you pick them up:
```
Hi! A reminder: your [service] is tomorrow, [day and date].
Pickup time: [time]
Meeting point: [meeting point]
Please bring: [what to bring]
Reply YES to confirm, or tell us if you need a new time.
[your business name]
```

## Reminder, on the day (`today`)
Send at the owner's time only (for example, "[time] on the morning").

A:
```
Hi! See you today at [time] for your [service].
Map: [location link]
[your business name]
```

B:
```
Hi! We'll be with you today at [time] for your [service].
Before we come: [what to have ready]
[your business name]
```

C:
```
Hi! See you today for your [service].
Pickup time: [time]
Meeting point: [meeting point]
To reach the driver: [blank]
[your business name]
```

## Please confirm (`pls-confirm`)
One nudge only, when there's no reply to the reminder.
```
Hi! We haven't heard back about your [service] on [day and date] at [time].
Please reply YES to keep it, or tell us a better time.
[your business name]
```

## Reschedule reply (`reschedule`)
Never promises the new time.
```
Hi! No problem. Which day and time suit you?
Let me check and confirm shortly.
[your business name]
```

## Cancellation reply (`cancel`)
Add a cancellation rule only if the owner gives one, in their words.
```
Hi! Thanks for letting us know. Your [service] on [day and date] is canceled.
Message us here anytime to book again.
[your business name]
```

## No-show follow-up (`missed`)
Kind, one line, no blame, no fee unless the owner states one.
```
Hi! We missed you today. Would you like a new time? Just reply with a day that suits you.
[your business name]
```

## After-service (`thanks`)
Pick the one the owner wants. Results times and how results are sent come only from the owner. Never say what a result means or that anything is safe.

Results ready:
```
Hi! Thank you for your [service].
Your results will be ready [when, owner's words].
For what your results mean, please speak to a qualified person.
[your business name]
```

Thank you:
```
Hi! Thank you for choosing us for your [service] today.
We'd be happy to see you again.
[your business name]
```

Thank you with a review request (only if the owner asks):
```
Hi! Thank you for your [service] today.
If you were happy with it, a short review would help us a lot: [review link]
[your business name]
```
