## Assumptions to check with a real owner
(These come from one owner's notes. Someone who works with owners should check them.)
1. Owners book by phone or WhatsApp and send confirmations and reminders by hand from the WhatsApp Business app, not a paid API tool.
2. A "reply YES" nudge works better than reminder calls for getting customers to confirm.
3. Owners will write their own prep steps once (the template) rather than expect the helper to know them.
4. A booking code in the sheet (not the customer's name) is workable day to day.
5. The copy box pastes cleanly into WhatsApp on an Android phone.
