# Example 01: a beach shack sets up its weekly posts (fictional)

**Owner types:**
> Sample Beach Shack, Morjim. I never post and people forget us. Set me up. I just took a photo of our grilled kingfish plate on a table with the sea behind it. Our customers read English. People should WhatsApp us to book a table.

**Helper replies:**

**Today (about 10 minutes)**
1. Make a phone album called "Photos for posts" (in your gallery or photos app: new album).
2. Put the kingfish photo in it. From now on: no people, never children.
3. Post it with the caption below.
4. Set a weekly phone reminder: "Posts, 20 minutes", every [day]. Pick a quiet day.

**Post 1** (photo: grilled kingfish plate, sea behind it)
```
Grilled kingfish on your plate, the sea right behind you.
At Sample Beach Shack, Morjim.
WhatsApp us to book a table.
```

**Your routine** (save it in WhatsApp "Message yourself")
```
My posts routine
Every day (1 minute): one photo into "Photos for posts". No people.
Every [day] (20 minutes): describe 3 photos, get 3 captions. Post one, save two as drafts.
Every month: Google profile, check hours, phone and links. Add 2 new photos.
It's working when: 3 posts a week for 3 weeks in a row, and your Google hours are right.
```

**My helper** (save it in "Message yourself"; each week paste it into any AI chat and fill in the photos)
```
Write 3 short captions in English for my beach shack in Morjim, one per photo:
1. [photo 1]
2. [photo 2]
3. [photo 3]
Use only what I say. Put [blank] for anything missing. No prices, offers or reviews I didn't give. No names or numbers of people. 60 words or fewer each, no hashtags. End each with: WhatsApp us to book a table.
I post them myself.
```

**This week**
- Today: post the kingfish photo.
- Every day: one photo into "Photos for posts" (the food, the bar, the view).
- On your reminder day: describe 3 photos to your helper and post one.

**Fill in:** the day for your weekly reminder.

It's a draft: check every caption, fix anything untrue, and post it yourself.

(Caption: 21 words. My helper: under 600 characters.)

## The next week (weekly mode, fictional)

> Sample Beach Shack again. Photos: crab xec xec in a clay bowl, our bamboo bar at sunset, beach chairs under the palms. WhatsApp us to book a table.

```
Crab xec xec, served in a clay bowl.
At Sample Beach Shack, Morjim.
WhatsApp us to book a table.
```

**Post 2** (photo: bamboo bar at sunset)
```
Our bamboo bar at sunset.
Come and watch the sky change from here.
WhatsApp us to book a table.
```

**Post 3** (photo: beach chairs under the palms)
```
Beach chairs under the palms, waiting for you.
WhatsApp us to book a table.
```

**This week:** Post 1 now. Save Posts 2 and 3 as drafts in the app and post them on [day] and [day]. (Already use your app's scheduling, if it has one? You can schedule them there instead.)

**Fill in:** the two days you'll post the drafts.

It's a draft: check every caption, fix anything untrue, and post it yourself.
