# Caption patterns (weekly-posts-routine)

Three simple shapes. Use a different one for each of the week's 3 photos so the posts don't sound the same. Every line comes from what the owner said or what the photo shows. Missing facts are [blank].

Each caption: 2 or 3 short lines, then the owner's contact line on its own. 60 words or fewer, 500 characters or fewer. No hashtags or emojis unless the owner asks.

## 1. What it is
Name the thing in the photo and one detail the owner gave.
```
[the thing], [one detail the owner gave].
[where or when, if the owner said]
[your contact line]
```
Example (fictional): "Sourdough, cut open on the board this morning. / Baked at Sample Bake House, Aldona. / Order on WhatsApp."

## 2. Behind the work
Show the work or the place before guests or customers see it.
```
[what is happening in the photo].
[one line about how the owner does it, in their words]
[your contact line]
```
Example (fictional): "Our veranda before breakfast. / Two cane chairs in the morning light. / Message us on WhatsApp to book."

## 3. Come and see
Invite people to the place, using only the owner's own facts (open days, times, what's included).
```
[the place or view in the photo].
[a fact the owner gave: open days, what's included]
[your contact line]
```
Example (fictional): "The garden path, hibiscus in bloom. / Breakfast is included with every room. / Message us on WhatsApp to book."

## Words to leave out (unless the owner says they are true)
best, famous, freshest, award-winning, number 1, five-star, limited offer, only a few left, healthy, detox, heals, boosts immunity, reduces stress. No health effects for food, drinks, yoga, massage or treatments, even if the owner says so.

## The contact line
Use the owner's own words, the same in all 3 captions, for example:
- "WhatsApp us to book a table."
- "Message us on WhatsApp to book."
- "Book here: [booking link]"
- "Order on WhatsApp: [your WhatsApp number]"

If the owner gave two ways ("call or WhatsApp"), pick the one they named first and say in one line that one clear way works best; they can change it. Never put a UPI ID, bank details or a customer's number in a post.

## Saving drafts and scheduling
Most apps let you save a post as a draft before you share it. Post one now and save two as drafts; post those on the days you pick. If you already use your app's scheduling, if it has one, you can schedule them there instead. Nothing new to install.
