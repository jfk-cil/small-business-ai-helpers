# "My helper" prompts (weekly-posts-routine)

The owner saves one of these in WhatsApp "Message yourself" and pastes it into any AI chat each week. Fill in what the owner told you (language, business type, place, contact line). Leave the photo lines for the owner to fill each week. Keep it under 600 characters, counting spaces. Never put a customer's name or number, a UPI ID or a price the owner hasn't decided into it.

## Weekly captions (the default)
```
Write 3 short captions in [language] for my [business type] in [place], one per photo:
1. [photo 1]
2. [photo 2]
3. [photo 3]
Use only what I say. Put [blank] for anything missing. No prices, offers or reviews I didn't give. No names or numbers of people. 60 words or fewer each, no hashtags. End each with: [your contact line]
I post them myself.
```
(About 330 characters before filling in.)

## One caption (when the owner posts one photo at a time)
```
Write one short caption in [language] for my [business type] in [place]. The photo shows: [photo]. Use only what I say; put [blank] for anything missing. No prices, offers or reviews I didn't give. 60 words or fewer, no hashtags. End with: [your contact line]
```

## Monthly reminder (optional, for the same "Message yourself" chat)
```
Monthly: open my Google Business Profile. Check hours, phone and links. Add 2 new photos with no people.
```

## Filled example (fictional)
```
Write 3 short captions in English for my beach shack in Morjim, one per photo:
1. [photo 1]
2. [photo 2]
3. [photo 3]
Use only what I say. Put [blank] for anything missing. No prices, offers or reviews I didn't give. No names or numbers of people. 60 words or fewer each, no hashtags. End each with: WhatsApp us to book a table.
I post them myself.
```

## In another language
Translate the whole prompt into the owner's language (Hindi, Marathi, Konkani in Devanagari or Roman script, or Hinglish) and keep the [blank] placeholders as they are. Add, outside the box: "Please check this with someone who reads it well."
