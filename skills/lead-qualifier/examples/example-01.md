# Example 01: Sample Cleaning Service checks a move-out inquiry (fictional)

## Owner types
> Sample Cleaning Service. We work in North Goa only, our minimum job is Rs 3,000, and we need 2 days' notice. A lead: a villa in Siolim wants a move-out clean tomorrow, budget about Rs 5,000, and the villa owner is the one booking. Is this lead worth it?

## Helper replies

**Your fit questions**
1. Do they need a clean we offer? (must-have)
2. Is the budget at least Rs 3,000? (must-have)
3. Do we get at least 2 days' notice? (must-have)
4. Is the job in North Goa? (must-have)
5. Are we talking to the person who books? (nice-to-have)

**This lead**
| Question | Answer | From what you said |
|---|---|---|
| Need | Yes | Move-out clean of a villa |
| Budget | Yes | About Rs 5,000 |
| Notice | No | They want it tomorrow |
| Area | Yes | Siolim is in North Goa |
| Decision | Yes | The villa owner is booking |

**Verdict: Not now**
Why: they need it tomorrow, and you need 2 days' notice.

**Next step:** reply kindly, ready to send:
"Thanks for asking! We need 2 days' notice, so we can't do tomorrow. If [date] works, we'd be glad to help."

**Fill in:** [date] (the next day you're free)
This is a guide, not a rule: you decide.

## Why it looks like this
- Every question comes from the owner's own rules (area, minimum, notice). Nothing about the person.
- "Not now" comes from one must-have being No, and it still gets a kind reply with a door left open.
- The numbers are exactly what the owner typed; the next free day is a [date] blank, not a guess.
