## v1.0.0 (October 21, 2026)
- First version, written for the small-business AI helpers reference packages.
- Four jobs: set up listings (one fixed column set, a "New listing" note for logging from a WhatsApp message, one ready row from pasted details), a client needs form with no name or number field, matching pasted rows to one client's needs (Fits, Close with "Misses", "Has" or "Check", up to 5 listings), and WhatsApp-ready messages per listing plus a shortlist summary.
- Rules for this helper: codes and areas only (no names, numbers or flat or house numbers), facts from the row only, prices as listed, no promises of prices, returns or yields, title, approvals, RERA and zoning left out, honest about what the helper can't do (read WhatsApp, open photos, keep listings between chats, send).
- References: sheet-columns.md (columns, sample rows, the "New listing" note, photo folders, filter steps, client forms), matching.md and share-formats.md (property for rent and sale, land, vehicle or equipment rentals, homestay rooms, shortlist summary).
- Test bank evals/testbank/LMT.json (16 cases) and evals/evals.json (7 cases), pre-registered October 21, 2026.
