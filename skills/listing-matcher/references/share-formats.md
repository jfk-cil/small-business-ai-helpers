# Share-ready message formats

One message per chosen listing, then one shortlist summary. Each in its own copy box. Only that row's facts go in. A line whose fact the row doesn't give is left out, or shown as [blank] if the client asked for it. Every box: 500 characters or fewer, aim for 60 words, ends with the business name the owner gave, or [your business name] if they didn't.

Never include: names, phone numbers, flat, unit, plot, survey or house numbers, title or approvals wording, "negotiable" (unless the row says so), returns, yields or "price will go up".

## Flat, villa or house for rent
```
PV-03 · 2BHK flat for rent, Porvorim
Size: 1,050 sq ft
Rent: ₹35,000 a month
Available from: November
Features: covered parking, lift
Photos and videos: [photo/video link]
Want to see it? Tell me a day that suits you and I'll check and confirm.
[your business name]
```

## Shortlist summary (send first, or on its own)
```
Hi! These match what you asked for:
1. PV-03: 2BHK flat for rent, Porvorim, ₹35,000 a month
2. PV-11: 2BHK flat for rent, Alto Betim, ₹38,000 a month
Tell me which ones you'd like to see. I'll send photos and check visit times.
[your business name]
```
One line per listing: code, type, area, price as listed. No reasons, no extra adjectives.

## Language
If the client wrote in Hindi, Konkani, Marathi or Hinglish and the owner wants to reply in it, write the messages in that language, keep codes and prices as they are, and add for the owner: "Please check this with someone who reads it well."
