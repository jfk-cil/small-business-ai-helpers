# Example 01: Sample Homestay, slow weekday bookings (fictional)

## Owner's message
> Sample Homestay in Candolim, 3 rooms. Weekends fill up, weekdays are empty in November. I can do 15 minutes a day. I use Instagram and Google Business Profile. I can give a free airport pickup for stays of 3 nights or more.

## Output

**4-week plan: Sample Homestay, more weekday stays in November**

**Goal:** more weekday bookings in November.
**Who:** people who work from anywhere and can travel on weekdays; couples taking a mid-week break.
**Offer:** free airport pickup for stays of 3 nights or more (your offer).
**Channels:** Instagram; Google Business Profile.

**Week 1:** add "free airport pickup for 3+ nights" to a Google Business Profile post (10 min). Post a photo of a quiet weekday morning at the homestay (5 min).
**Week 2:** post a 15-second reel: the room, the bed made up, morning light through the window (15 min, once).
**Week 3:** post "Weekday or weekend: when do you like to travel?" as a question (5 min). Reply to every comment (5 min a day).
**Week 4:** repeat the post that got the most replies. Update the Google Business Profile post (10 min).

**How you'll know:** on paper, tally each week: weekday inquiries, and weekday nights booked. Ask each guest how they found you and mark it. Compare with the 4 weeks before. Target: [blank: your target].

**To fill in:**
your target

**Next:** ask me to write the posts (social-content-planner) or update your Google Business description (google-profile-builder).

## Why it looks like this
- The offer is the owner's own; nothing is added.
- No promise about how many bookings it will bring.
- The counts are things the owner can tally on paper without sharing guest details.
