## C1. Appointment-based service

- **Business type:** an appointment-based service such as a testing lab.
- **The job:** confirming bookings and sending reminders.
- **What it costs them today:** customers find the business online, then call to ask about the service. Confirming each booking and reminding each customer by phone takes time, and reminder calls often don't get through. It matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** the customer confirms what they need to bring, and turns up with it.
- **Keep out of AI:** passport scans and Form C details; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs; staff ID numbers and wages; family members' details; photos of people; health information.
- **Package:** [booking-reminders](../packages/booking-reminders/). A full set of booking messages (confirm, remind, reschedule, no-show, thank you) with what to bring, a simple booking sheet and a daily routine. The owner saves the messages once and sends each one.

## C2. Marketing agency

- **Business type:** a marketing agency.
- **The job:** finding the kinds of clients who need them.
- **What it costs them today:** it comes up now and then, mostly when work is slow. The owner can't picture who needs the agency or who to work with, and hasn't tried anything yet. It matters a little.
- **What success looks like:** a steady list of leads.
- **Keep out of AI:** the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [find-clients](../packages/find-clients/). Customer types to look for, search phrases the owner can use themselves, who could refer them, and first messages in their own voice. The owner does the searching and sends every message.

## C3. Real estate

- **Business type:** a real estate agent.
- **The job:** replying to questions on WhatsApp.
- **What it costs them today:** dozens of messages a day, every day. The owner replies in one long block late in the day, after other work. It works, but it drains their energy and leaves little for new business and new inquiries. It matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** the owner answers only the important messages, and the routine ones are handled with AI.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer names and phone numbers; passport scans and Form C details; bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs.
- **Package:** [customer-replies](../packages/customer-replies/). The owner pastes a batch of messages with names changed to Customer 1, Customer 2. The helper lists the few that need the owner, each with a calm holding line, and drafts the routine replies in the owner's tone with quick replies for repeated questions. The owner checks and sends every reply; nothing is sent automatically.

## C4. Cleaning service

- **Business type:** a cleaning service.
- **The job:** replying to questions on WhatsApp.
- **What it costs them today:** many inquiries every day, and giving each one the attention it deserves takes a lot of time. Unread chats pile up. The owner wishes WhatsApp connected straight to the AI. It matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** replies in the owner's own tone go out, and the owner is brought in only for unusual, non-generic questions.
- **Keep out of AI:** passwords and OTPs; bank, UPI and card details; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; photos of people.
- **Package:** [customer-replies](../packages/customer-replies/), as for C3. The helper is honest that it can't read or send WhatsApp messages: an automatic bot needs the WhatsApp Business Platform through an approved provider, customer opt-in and approved templates. It sets up quick replies, labels and an away message the owner can use today.

## C5. Café

- **Business type:** a café with staff in a few different roles.
- **The job:** a checklist or how-to for each job.
- **What it costs them today:** the owner wants each staff member's tasks clearly structured every day, in a way that keeps them engaged and helps them grow as well as do the work. Nothing tried yet. It matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** the café runs well without the owner there.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer names and phone numbers; passport scans and Form C details; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs; health information; staff ID numbers and wages; family members' details.
- **Package:** [staff-checklists](../packages/staff-checklists/). One checklist per role and shift, a short version for the wall, and a weekly 15-minute check. Checklists describe tasks, never ratings of people.

## C6. Equipment hire

- **Business type:** an equipment hire business.
- **The job:** getting found on Google search.
- **What it costs them today:** when people search Google for equipment rental in the area, the business doesn't show up. The owner looked for someone to manage their Google presence, but found no one reliable at a fair fee. It matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** showing up near the top when people search for equipment rental in the area.
- **Keep out of AI:** bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs; family members' details.
- **Package:** [get-found-on-google](../packages/get-found-on-google/). A Google Business Profile checklist, a description and services list within Google's limits, weekly posts, a review request message and a list of search words. No helper can promise a ranking; these are the steps the owner controls.

## C7. Home healthcare

- **Business type:** a home healthcare service for patients and elders who cannot leave home.
- **The job:** bringing skilled specialists, such as nurses and attendants, on board.
- **What it costs them today:** the path from finding the service, to getting in touch, to signing up breaks along the way. Too few specialists reach out or finish signing up. It happens every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** specialists reach out on their own, with real interest in serving homebound patients and elders.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer names and phone numbers; passport scans and Form C details; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; passwords and OTPs; staff ID numbers and wages; photos of people; family members' details; health information; bank, UPI and card details.
- **Package:** [hire-and-onboard](../packages/hire-and-onboard/). Where people drop off and what to fix first, job posts and outreach messages, replies to applicants, and a first-week checklist. Patients, applicants' details and clinical steps stay out of AI; see the health and hiring rules in [guide/PRIVACY.md](../guide/PRIVACY.md).

## C8. Nonprofit or membership group

- **Business type:** a nonprofit or membership group.
- **The job:** following up with past supporters.
- **What it costs them today:** the owner reaches out to everyone but can't follow up on each call or each unanswered call. It takes time and attention, and contacts go cold. They keep a notebook or sheet and want each follow-up to have a purpose, two or three times, until a reply comes. It happens every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** every contact gets a first message, follow-ups go out two or three times as planned, every reply is collected, and there is a short report of the day's follow-ups.
- **Keep out of AI:** the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [follow-ups](../packages/follow-ups/). A ready follow-up sequence with a purpose for each message, a tracker that shows who is due today, and a short daily report from the owner's notes. The owner sends each follow-up; an automatic WhatsApp bot is a separate builder project.

## C9. Maker with products and workshops

- **Business type:** a maker or artist who sells finished work, runs workshops and takes custom requests.
- **The job:** one place online where people can see every offer and buy, book or ask.
- **What it costs them today:** the work is ready to sell, and it already lives online in pieces: a portfolio site, a free shop app with a payment link, and Instagram. But nothing ties them together, so visitors can't tell where to shop, where to book a workshop or how to ask a question. Working alone, the owner has no time to find the common thread. It comes up every month and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** one entry point, with a quick link for each offer, and simple steps that turn visits into orders and bookings.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer names and phone numbers; passport scans and Form C details; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs; health information.
- **Package:** [one-page-shop](../packages/one-page-shop/). An offer map with one action per offer and a 10-item checklist, page words with one button per offer, a plain page file with placeholder links and free hosting steps, and the reply, confirmation and follow-up after each click. The owner adds their own links and publishes the page; the helper never asks for logins or payment details.

## C10. Workshop or class business

- **Business type:** a workshop or class business that posts short videos.
- **The job:** posts and captions for the week.
- **What it costs them today:** short videos bring new viewers every week, but few of them stay, engage or book, and there is no steady format for sharing something useful. The owner has notes and plans but no time to follow them. Nothing tried yet. It happens every week and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** more engagement that leads to more sign-ups, so the owner can keep making new products and online workshops.
- **Keep out of AI:** photos of people; bank, UPI and card details.
- **Package:** [social-posts](../packages/social-posts/). Three weekly series (a tip, behind the scenes, what's on), an honest path from a post to a free small thing the owner makes, then to a WhatsApp list people choose to join, then to a workshop or product, a weekly rhythm that fits the owner's time and a short weekly count. Also a week of posts that convert with quick replies, and a launch plan for an online workshop with no fake urgency. The owner posts and sends everything.

## C11. Shop selling packaged goods

- **Business type:** a shop that sells packaged goods with expiry dates, such as pet supplies.
- **The job:** assigning a monthly stock task to staff, with training and a timeline.
- **What it costs them today:** every month, expired and near-expiry products have to be found and sent back to the supplier. The steps are checking the real stock, making a purchase return in the billing software, packing the products and following up with the supplier. Staff need hand-holding at each step. The owner uses a sheet to check the counts and the billing software for the return. It comes up every month.
- **What success looks like:** each step has a role and a date, and staff can follow it without hand-holding.
- **Keep out of AI:** the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [stock-and-returns](../packages/stock-and-returns/). A monthly routine with a role, a day, "done when" and a sign-off for each step, a first-time how-to for new staff, tracker columns with quantities and dates only, and a return request to the supplier with one polite follow-up. It never says whether expired items are safe to sell and never invents supplier terms. The owner checks everything and sends the messages.

## C12. Tours and rentals

- **Business type:** a tours, activities or rentals business.
- **The job:** replying to inquiries that come through booking and delivery apps.
- **What it costs them today:** bookings come in every day. Guests call or message to ask the pickup time, and each one needs a reply. For online leads, the owner shares their own activity list and closes each booking by hand. On busy weekends there is no time to reply to everyone, and some follow-ups are forgotten. The owner wishes the routine replies were automatic. It happens every day.
- **What success looks like:** routine replies such as pickup times are ready to send, and no follow-up is forgotten on a busy weekend.
- **Keep out of AI:** the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [customer-replies](../packages/customer-replies/), with [booking-reminders](../packages/booking-reminders/). Replies are drafted for the app's own chat and follow its messaging rules, with quick replies for pickup time, meeting point and what to bring. Booking-reminders writes the pickup confirmation and reminders. For a busy weekend, [follow-ups](../packages/follow-ups/) lists who to catch up first, with two "sorry for the slow reply" drafts. The helpers are honest that they can't reply inside any app on their own; the owner sends every reply.

## C13. Small food business

- **Business type:** a small food business, such as a bakery.
- **The job:** a simple marketing plan across several ways of selling.
- **What it costs them today:** the owner wants one plan for three channels: supplying cafés, selling direct to customers through WhatsApp, and posting on social media. Marketing hasn't started yet, mainly for lack of time. It comes up every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** a 6-month sales plan and a bank of posts to reuse.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer names and phone numbers; Aadhaar or PAN numbers; bank, UPI and card details; and the rest of the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [sales-plan](../packages/sales-plan/). A channel map, a 6-month plan with monthly themes from the owner's own seasons and weekly steps that fit their time, a bank of post ideas with a monthly refresh, a first message and tasting visit plan for cafés, and a monthly check with counts only. WhatsApp messages go only to people who chose to join. It never promises sales or invents prices or wholesale terms; the owner visits, posts and sends everything.

## C14. Solo founder of a young company

- **Business type:** a solo founder running a young company.
- **The job:** AI that plans, prioritizes and carries out work across the whole business, so the founder doesn't have to manage every small task.
- **What it costs them today:** every day means switching between many projects and jobs. AI tools help with single tasks, but getting them to work together on bigger goals takes a lot of explaining, breaking down, checking and prompting. It matters a great deal.
- **What success looks like:** one request with several goals; the system plans, keeps the context, tracks progress, flags what is stuck and asks before important decisions.
- **Keep out of AI:** money commitments, big business decisions and outside actions the founder hasn't approved.
- **Closest safe help:** no ready helper does this. An agent that acts across tools on its own is a builder project, see [guide/AUTOMATION.md](../guide/AUTOMATION.md). Start with [start-here](../packages/start-here/) to pick the first job, then [make-your-own](../packages/make-your-own/) to make one helper per job (each with a short self-test). Every approval stays with the founder.

- **Business type:** a business that runs events or club nights.
- **The job:** finding leads when the best customers are senior executives and high-income people.
- **What it costs them today:** the owner hasn't worked out how to market to these clients or reach them. Nothing tried yet. It comes up every day and matters a lot.

- **Keep out of AI:** the standard never-paste list.

## C17. Facilitation and team offsites

- **Business type:** a facilitation business that designs and runs team offsites.
- **The job:** finding who the best customers are.
- **What it costs them today:** every engagement follows the same arc (the need, the purpose, the design, the harvest and the outcomes), but each one feels personal. General outreach hasn't worked; clients so far came by word of mouth. It comes up every week and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** offsites for 25 to 60 people once or twice a month.
- **Keep out of AI:** passport scans and Form C details; passwords and OTPs; and the rest of the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [find-clients](../packages/find-clients/). Best customer types and where to meet them, fit questions, a first email that names the arc the owner already uses, and a welcome pack for a new client (client-onboarding-writer).

## C18. New app business

- **Business type:** a new business building an app.
- **The job:** weekly posts and captions, and learning how to launch online.
- **What it costs them today:** there are no posts beyond the founder's personal account, and the app needs ways to reach people. The founder has never done this. It comes up every week and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** one page that brings in users and buyers, works as a portfolio and has a link for possible backers.
- **Keep out of AI:** photos of people; customer names and numbers; bank, UPI and card details; passwords and OTPs.
- **Package:** [social-posts](../packages/social-posts/) for a first week of posts and a weekly rhythm, with [one-page-shop](../packages/one-page-shop/) for one page with a button per offer. The helpers give no fundraising or investment advice.

## C19. Real estate agent (listings and matches)

- **Business type:** a real estate agent with apartments, villas and land for sale or rent.
- **The job:** keeping every listing in one place, then sending each client the listings that fit what they asked for.
- **What it costs them today:** listing details sit in WhatsApp chats and written notes. A sheet was tried but was too clumsy to keep up to date, wasn't linked to the photos and videos, and every listing had to be retyped for WhatsApp. It happens every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** listings logged in one fixed way, a short form for a client's needs, the matching listings shown, and the chosen ones ready to send.
- **Keep out of AI:** guest or customer phone numbers and names, and the rest of the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [customer-replies](../packages/customer-replies/), with the new Skill listing-matcher: one fixed column set and a "New listing" note, a client needs form with no name or number field, up to 5 matches from the rows the owner pastes (each with what it misses), and one WhatsApp-ready message per listing plus a shortlist. tracker-sheet-builder builds the sheet. The helper can't read WhatsApp, open photos or keep listings between chats; it uses listing codes and areas, never names, numbers or flat numbers, and never says anything about title, approvals or returns.

## C20. Wellness or class studio

- **Business type:** a wellness, massage or class studio.
- **The job:** finding the students and clients who suit the classes.
- **What it costs them today:** paid social ads bring low-quality leads that don't become paying clients. A person was tried; nothing has helped yet. It comes up every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** good leads arriving by DMs, social and WhatsApp from people who are truly interested.
- **Keep out of AI:** passwords and OTPs; passport scans and Form C details; and the rest of the standard never-paste list.
- **Package:** [find-clients](../packages/find-clients/) for best client types and fit questions to sort serious leads from browsers, with [social-posts](../packages/social-posts/) for posts that lead people to ask. The helpers never promise results from ads and make no health claims about classes or treatments.

## C21. Café (pricing and paperwork)

- **Business type:** a café.
- **The job:** pricing, daily billing, cost of goods and stock, simple and in one place.
- **What it costs them today:** an AI-made spreadsheet was tried but wasn't complete and wasn't practical on a phone. It comes up every day and matters a lot.
- **What success looks like:** quick and easy to use, everything in one place, nothing typed twice.
- **Keep out of AI:** bank, UPI and card details, and every figure from the books.
- **Closest safe help:** billing, cash records and costs are money records, which these helpers don't handle: keep them in your own accounts book or billing software, with your accountant (**a person**). For item counts only, [stock-and-returns](../packages/stock-and-returns/) and tracker-sheet-builder build a stock sheet with quantities and dates and no money columns.
